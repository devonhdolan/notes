# Issue 242

Hello\! Picking things up where we left off… the now is a once in a generation opportunity. And that’s coming from someone who despises hyperbole. The window to strike shortens with each passing day. It’s time to build toward a future you believe in, do it for yourself, and your next of kin.  
Where do you think the industry, the world, is going to be in five to ten years…  
Ten years ago, A24 hadn’t yet released its first film, and Netflix hadn’t yet dropped House of Cards for all to binge. Ethereum hadn’t been founded. Fortnite battle royales were just barely in development. Disney was still kicking the tires on LucasFilm. Multichannel networks on YouTube were all the rage. Social media was considered cool and innovative. And I was still in university.  
A lot can change in a decade, and this one is going to be nothing short of monumental. This is where it all comes together. A global, accessible, tech \+ media convergence.  
As my new friend Joe aptly put it in the Telegram group\*, *distribution is now digital but production is still analogue*. The cost of failure is too high (for now..), even when we have the best, cheapest and simplest, filmmaker and developer tools at our fingertips. The only thing standing in the way is the *industry* itself, which purposefully keeps itself imprisoned in a glass box.  
But really, it’s just a glass ceiling, that’s meant to be shattered. Democratization.  
The broader issues are what we’ve let transpire over the last decade or two. The streaming and social era was a rug pull, a heist. It has totally morphed and warped our behaviors \-- our viewing and spending habits. Our relationships with people and art. We’re moving beyond the information age, and need to take back control. The audience is more unconsciously sophisticated than ever before.  
The people are the problem, and the solution. Tune in, turn on, drop out. Rally cry.  
We must make stories that stand for something, that have something to say. Sublimity. Give the people something to talk about, a new concept to research, or culture to experience. If you have nothing valuable to add, don’t say anything. Create staying power, through stories of substance. With spectacle.  
And never preach from the pulpit.  
To that end, the chat group\* is now home to about a dozen \~multidimensional\~ thinkers sounding off on similar topics. I generally tease newsletter subjects before writing about them here. Message me if you want to join \-- I will prob cap it at \~25 people before it gets too unwieldy. It’s just a fun experiment; nothing to lose. Gotta say, it’s rewarding having healthy conversations that spoke off this thing. I’ll likely be swimming around there in the weeks to come.    
Once the newsletter comes to its natural end with issue 250, future “issues” will be more thoughtful. A mix between educational essay and op-ed, and generally in service of something larger. We must equip the next generation with information and the tools to be lethal. New norms. Stay tuned.  
Where do you think you might be in the world in five to ten years?  
Make an impact, bet on yourself.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Auteurship awaits. See ya next week. 8 left.
