# Issue 107

Hello\! This one’s pretty light and personal today. If we’ve talked the last couple of months, then you might know this, but I figured I’d start spreading the word a bit more. I’m keeping myself busy with an array of projects and engagements that span stories and startups (interactive media). I’ve written a bit about my work on the latter, but wanted to clue you more in to the former. I’m strictly focused on working on content that orbits around STEM (science, technology, engineering, math) topics, built for any medium (film, tv, pods, games, etc). I’m coming on board irrationally early and doing my best to help raise capital, provide notes, attach talent, and secure distribution — all things much easier said than done. Why STEM? It’s my favorite. It’s what I read, seek out, pay for, and want to watch. Works like The Matrix, Ex Machina, Westworld, Arrival, Interstellar, Portal, Her, Imitation Game, and Black Mirror have left a serious impression on me, and I believe that these types of stories will inspire future engineers and entrepreneurs. I’ll talk more to the specifics of the projects when appropriate, but if you want to be helpful you can just reply to this email. I have some things planned for this newsletter, more on that in the next issue. Appreciate the bday wishes last week. Oh, and if you happen to know what Plepler is doing — hit me up. Very curious. I won’t drop a bomb like I did with the Quibi notes last summer. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
