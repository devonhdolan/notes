# Issue 244

Hello\! I’ve been thinking about lifecycles, beginnings and ends, and the ever-expanding middle ground. The day, from dawn ‘til dusk, brings night. Fresh starts and cold turkeys. Ramp ups and downs. Seeds and harvests. Growth. Decay. You get it…  
Life events, as productions, as dividing lines; more so, processes. Intersectionality.  
Before. During. & After.  
Pipelines, from one point to another. All the in-betweens can be timestamped, colored in, with context \-- information and experience. They’re distances, marathons and sprints. The infinite race, the first launch is just the start of the process. And vice versa.  
Projects. It is a market. It is constant, seismic. Incessant, eternal. A scam, where speed and quality are everything.  
Recognizability. This might draw parallels to some of the recent headlines around actors licensing their name, image and likeness (facial and vocal) through the use of AI. If you haven’t, it’d be cool to [fill out the poll](https://forms.gle/vxq36Xoy3V3EyFqe8) before looking at [the results](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dGgHpqRAKwCPA-eLV50MMJzkZfg7d-pVhTlf9rhGMb8/edit?usp=sharing) to last week’s questionnaire about The Use of AI in Media. The \~1% rule holds true yet again with about 20 replies. I still need to try to gather more responses on a few other social platforms.  
Doubles. I imagine some agent might get clever enough to parse out an actor’s life into segments with this technology. I want 70s Harrison Ford and late 90s Salma Hayek together in a movie, with a cameo from Tupac. Enter in public figures to the public domain, know you’re generally in the clear if you take a non-commercial approach and don’t carry malice in your work. Marked.  
Indicate fair use with good intents, ask for forgiveness.  
Civic centers. Municipalities might try to do this with cities as “digital twins” in virtual worlds. Landlords prevail, rent seeking missiles. I want the actors above to be set in Florence during the Renaissance, or maybe Prohibition era Chicago. If I took about an hour, I could whip up a slew of storyboards with variations of these characters and settings with AI. Mise en Scène.  
Seek high-density, hyper-local territories.  
Collectivity. The brand names aren’t enough, but they’re a start. Association games play to the audience, both studio execs and/or movie goers (B2B2C). The familiarity with talent is the common edge. Owning these fandoms, perhaps with a steady drip of content, will result in the ultimate benefit(s) through conversion. Again with 1% rule here, unless there’s more aligned interests though progressive community involvement. Get points on the board, together.  
Barbells. The power vacuums rest on either side of the spectrum. The creators (few) and the consumers (many); fuse. All are instruments in a symphony, ingredients in a special dish. Interest groups as focus groups. Style and aesthetic parameters beget originality. To be tagged and labeled.  
This has all happened before, and it will happen again.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. History rhymes. See ya next week. 6 left.
