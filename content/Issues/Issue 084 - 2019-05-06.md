# Issue 84

Hello\! It’s 2019, let’s pivot to audio. Hollywood is actively figuring out the key deal points for podcasts — it’s come up in every conversation the last two weeks. There are no precedents, so just have fun with it. Aim to retain your derivative rights because that’s usually the end goal. Flip it into a tv show, or manage both productions concurrently. Meanwhile, I think one of the more lucrative (and boring) plays in this space would be to create programmatic ad inventory and insertion tech that maps to specific territories and countries. It’s the antiquated radio model dressed up for the “new” podcast dance. For example, Australians should not be bombarded with Chipotle ads because they sadly don’t exist in the Asia-Pac market. That’s a problem (well, two — Aussies need Chipotle). I must say that one of the most beautiful things about podcast ads is that they’re conveniently skippable (for the most part). People have asked for my thoughts on Luminary. I think it’s an absolute coup that they managed to raise \$100m from NEA to build a SVOD podcast platform. Good for them. Will it be successful? I’ve not a clue. Time will tell. One thing’s for certain — creators will be getting paid. Just peachy. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
