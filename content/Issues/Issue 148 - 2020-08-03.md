# Issue 148

Hello\! Online applications. Not a fan. For jobs, fellowships, grants, labs and anything of the sort. It all seems to be a waste of time, unless you’re one of the final contestants. I’ve done some in my day, and I’ll certainly do a few more. I’ve sent two the last 18 months, for Sundance and Schmidt Futures. I got neither. Their loss. I shared my responses to [BoxGroup’s questionnaire in issue 67](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-67-12-24-18-68e16853e7c4), Xmas 2018\. [And here’s an app I forgot about from April 2017](https://www.dropbox.com/sh/hekzz5v49jd7h2r/AAC0wvH2qJQaCw0QPqZcKtwja?dl=0). It was to more or less head video for the Opinion section at The Times. Think it was pretty damn strong. This was years before any NYT film/tv initiatives announced themselves. I still think they need to do an AI series. Fun fact, if I got the job I would’ve been paid four times my then salary. I repeat, four times\!\!\!  
I’m sharing these because I feel for my friends in film/tv who’ve been laid off the last few weeks, and unfortunately missed that \$600/week bonus. Really shitty timing. Maybe they’ll be able to lift some of the ideas and wordage presented in these. Let me know if you’ve been affected and I’ll slip you nifty jobs that come across my desk.  
[Nike’s newest ad, You Can’t Stop Us,](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WA4dDs0T7sM) is superb. It incorporates that split screen aesthetic in a really thoughtful way. Just lovely. I wrote about the side-by-side look back in [issue 82, April 2019](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-82-4-22-19-7225f5f016c). In it, I applauded the Mid90s site, a Kahlil Joseph art installation, and a couple others. It dawned on me that [this Rules of Attraction scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6EPa9Fw3Lk) has the same style. It’s the first, to my knowledge \- do let me know if there are others I’m blanking on. The film came out in ‘02. And in that issue, I wrote about the Bret Easton Ellis podcast (I listen to it religiously for better or worse), who wrote the Rules of Attraction book in ‘87. Don’t know why it didn’t occur to me then. Fun fact, I worked on [The Canyons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canyons_\(film\)) when I was a young pup. He wrote. The stories from that set…  
Last night I watched [Luce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luce_\(film\)) on Hulu \- the distrib Neon has an output there. It’s very good. Exceptionally punk, even though it’s steeped in ideology. It’s a great piece of writing that consistently subverts expectation. Nuanced, anti-authority. A good game. I also discovered last week that [NatGeo has 8 Fine Art Galleries](https://www.natgeofineart.com/gallery-locations/). Thought that was pretty rad. And finally, a very happy birthday to my ride or die, Anmari. You the best.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
