# Issue 11

Morning from Zurich\! I’m going to make this one quick as I’ve been manic prepping for my presentation tomorrow at [Expanding Immersive Design](http://bit.ly/2ARY2sg) where I’ll be talking about The Language of Immersive Storytelling. Since we’re on the subject, I’ve been musing on the idea of shared stories. Social stories. Story consumption is individualized in nature. Stories can be experienced with many in a theater, or devoured alone in a reading nook. If they work, they’re quick hits of escapism. But how do stories work as a group activity, both physical and digital? Who controls the story? What does it even look like? My feeling is that there’ll always be an assigned lead, an omnipresent Dungeon Master, acting as a guide and conduit into a fictitious world dolling out tasks and puzzles. Examples that come to mind are escape rooms and games like Portal and Destiny, but those concepts live in a hyper-scripted setting. I’m patiently waiting for when we become our own storytellers who can veer off track in a free-roam option to construct a shared narrative with friends and foes… Westworld?
