# Issue 31

Hello\! I’m trying something new today — testing how this performs on a lazy Sunday afternoon instead of scheduling it for Monday around LA lunch. Hopefully it gains a bit more traction, and if not, we’re back to normal programmed hours. (I’m always open to feedback, so feel free to reply to this email stating which you prefer). I have a [Sunday Routine](https://www.nytimes.com/column/sunday-routine) where I wake up, make a coffee, and watch clips from last night’s episode of SNL on YouTube. I absolutely adore the program and believe it to be the best platform to showcase comedic talent, both performers and writers. I remember when I was first introduced to the show, I was 15 and hanging out at my friend’s house. We plopped down on his couch and his parents put in a DVD and one of the skits was of Will Ferrell and Cheri Oteri acting as overly enthusiastic cheerleaders ([this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WifF0kZ0zp4) one\!). I was in stitches and have been hooked ever since. One of the neatest things about the show is in its production. Each episode is literally made in less than a week. [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live#Production) (In the early 90s, it was a cutthroat proving ground where the cast wouldn’t laugh at jokes during the Weds read-throughs). Lorne Michaels oversees a well-oiled machine that impacts culture like nothing else. The amount of A-list talent that have come through the SNL ranks is super inspiring. It works because its always fresh, natural, and original. Anyone can relate to it. It has legs. (Seriously, it’s been on the air for a record 43 seasons). I like that it wasn’t built to prop up another pillar of Hollywood like the press junket talk-show. SNL is literally the foundation of entertainment. Putting constraints and deadlines on creatives and seeing what they produce at the end of the week. It’s brilliant. Nothing revelatory from me today, just my appreciation for an old idea that stands strong in today’s age. (It’s ratings the past few years are off the charts). Love it. See you next week\!
