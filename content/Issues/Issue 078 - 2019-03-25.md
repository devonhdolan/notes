# Issue 78

Hello\! Or should I say, konichiwa. I’m in Tokyo gearing up for Sakura season — cherry blossoms\! It’s nice being back in a big city. If you feel inclined, please reply with any must-dos. Loving it here. Okay, top line return on marketing investments (ROMI) is on my mind given a few things I’ve seen this week. 1\) I’ve been talking and writing about the combination of computer vision, AR, and out-of-home advertising for a few years now, and it’s finally taking shape. Snap had interactive billboards in LA promoting one of their shows in Feb, and Burger King just shipped a feature in their app that burns their competitors’ ads in exchange for a burger. [Here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGByvh25uE0) is a 1min video that went viral this week. Chaotic good. Eagerly anticipating the checkout experience to be integrated with these types of media buys. Perhaps this will lead to QR phase 2.0. China’s WeChat knows what’s up. 2\) As does Instagram. Brands and their ad agencies will finally see viable performance metrics on their social media / influencer campaigns thanks to Insta’s e-comm. ROMI, not falsified impressions. They’ll need to train their marketing department on the concept of cash inflows. 3\) I’m very excited to see how all of this will pan out for studios and pre-order movie tickets via Fandango (owns the market), Atom, or directly with the exhibitors (theater chains). Same goes for musicians and their concerts. The Rock gets paid \$1m per post by studios to promote his own films. I actually think there might be something to do here… That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
