# Issue 227

Hello\! The weekend getaway is my preferred form of entertainment… it’s an immersive escape. Resort to recreation. Hit reset.  
Experience the environment.  
The script as blueprint. Add vision. At a fundamental level, there are similarities between producing stories and developing retreats. Ask Disney. Create connection, form meaning, make memories, and share fondness over one big idea. You’ll have your lasting impression, with company. Legacy.  
Excursions, the antidote to le metaverse. The call of the wild… not the void.  
Fertile grounds. From a time and budget perspective, it’s accounted for the same, as entertainment, with the added benefits of travel. Where you can dive deeper into the local culture and its history. The cost flexes up or down given your means.  
Leisure needn’t be a luxury.  
This new interest has slowly creeped into my field of view, and recently, I haven’t been able to take my eyes off it. This’ll come as no surprise to the longer reader ([issue 47\)](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-47-8-6-18-c001428b5868). About once a month I feel the pull to go off-grid and become an adventure guide in New Zealand…  
Life’s appeals. Orienting desire is a central component of human interaction design. The outcome should be beyond satisfactory.  
Our world’s a stage. Everything’s theatre.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Exit stage left. See ya next week.
