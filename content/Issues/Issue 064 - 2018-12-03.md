# Issue 64

Hello\! So I have a problem. I’m pretty bad at self-promotion and cross-pollination, especially when it comes to this newsletter and its preamble, and it’s not like I’m too humble to market it. I have no shame. There’s just a tasteful way of going about it. The thing is, I know it and know what I need to do to ameliorate it. I need to take action into my own hands and do a ground-up rebrand. A strategic overhaul. No excuses, just do it. This is one of my favorite aspects of running this little newsletter: I am fully responsible for everything about it, and have complete ownership over its successes and shortcomings. That’s half the the reason why I started it. Pretty selfish. The other half was to create, curate, and distribute content that adds value to others. Pretty simple. And deep down I felt that I couldn’t call myself a media industry professional if I wasn’t actively producing and distributing content. I had it up to my eyeballs with all the armchair experts, leaching intermediaries, and self-righteous critics, so I figured I’d put my money where my mouth is and whip up a niche counterweight. Plainly, I was compelled to build something myself. To that end, I made a fun 90 second video of my Saturday surfing with my dad and friends. I filmed everything on my iPhone and GoPro in the morning, and edited it in iMovie on my phone in the afternoon. A quick video in less than a day\! [Here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CUbMNuIOPVg) is the YouTube link and [here](https://www.instagram.com/devonhattondolan/) is my Instagram. I had to practice making silly videos before going on my world tour. Expect many more to come, but I’ll publish them on my socials and on a separate site because they’re pretty off-brand here. That’s all for now. Go make something. See you next week from Cape Town\!
