# Issue 255: k factor

This is a private email to a dozen early-access readers. I’m relaunching my “newsletter” in early December. If you want out, just say so. No hard feelings. All feedback is welcome.

Hello! It has finally happened. A post of mine went “viral” on Twitter last night. The numbers kept rising. Is this really the high that people chase? 

Cut it off. I muted my notifications. I went back to being private, which ironically is called “protect your posts.” Everything stopped. It went quiet again. Thank god. I’ll rot in the shadow(ban).

The tweet was about banning pervert glasses in preschools. My eyewitness account. 

Today is election day. This would garner unanimous bipartisan support. Maybe I should’ve run on that platform alone.

Patient zero. I stopped trying to pinpoint the cataclysm. I asked Grok. Not helpful, said it was organic and algorithmic spread. The latter certainly.

I hovered over profile pics. Multi-colored hair. 18+ only. Strange emojis. About Me from German to Japanese. Never a mutual. No conversion. Wait was that a furry?

My conclusion. It’s all fake. The metrics*. The media. The majority. Burn it down. 

Or embrace? I should’ve dropped a Change Dot Org petition. Too late. 

The campaign begins. I hooked up an agent to a dummy email to blast LA school districts, then nationwide. A taste of their own medicine (these political spam texts are out of hand). We’ll see what happens. I’m trying to get Meta (Muse) to ban itself. Chef’s kiss.

Platform risk. Just exist. 

*I learned recently that a major media platform counts views as just a tile open and counts them across every ad and episode break (so 1 click inadvertently leads to 60+ views on a title). How dare people inflate their numbers to raise capital. I won’t tell you where this company is domiciled. 

That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. By invite only. See ya next.
