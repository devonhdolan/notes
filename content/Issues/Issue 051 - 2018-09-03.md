# Issue 51

Hello\! Hope all of you based in America are enjoying the day off. Three things. 1\) We’re entering film festival season. Venice and Telluride are happening now, and Toronto is next week. Movie reviews from the trades have been flooding my dummy email account. I wish they were all more succinct. I dislike clicking through to the article to read the first paragraph (I stop there before the writer starts summarizing \[spoiling\] the movie). I just want the headline. The gut reaction. The body of the review is where the writer can explore the why. Just tell me whether the film was good, bad, mediocre, marvelous, or garbage. I enjoy reading reviews and critiques after I’ve watched the movie. Then it becomes more of a dialogue between reader and writer. (eg: Oh I totally missed that. I disagree there. This person gets it.) I have mixed feelings on the impact of individual critics nowadays. There’s definitely collective power. Friends outside of the business tend to base their viewing habits off the Tomatometer. I do too, sometimes. Perhaps I’ll write something longer about this subject one day. It might get me into some trouble with Cinetic’s publicity department, so maybe not. 2\) I was hoping Jack Ryan was going to be better. Amazon poured tens of millions into making and marketing this show and it’s a run of the mill broadcast drama. It totally lacks flavor, tension, and originality. I’m disappointed, but the joke’s on me, I’m still watching it. 3\) Showtime is offering the Kidding pilot for free on YouTube ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTrqSWS2oaw)). This is a novel idea, and one that will surely be replicated by other companies in due time. In the series, Jim Carrey plays a Fred Rogers type of character. Need I say more? If you haven’t seen it, be sure to check out the doc Won’t You Be My Neighbor. That’s all for now. See you next week\!
