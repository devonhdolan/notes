# Issue 19

Hello from Sundance\! I’m working out of the Cinetic lodge today as I’ve fallen quite ill. It seriously blows. I wanted to write a lengthier post about blockchain/crypto for this week’s letter, but time has escaped me with film festival mania. On the bright side, only one person has brought up crypto the last week, which is somewhat relieving. The crypto echo chamber is gross — a few puritans, many charlatans. I’ve been inundated with ideas, questions, and insights on this sector, and I only have a few myself because I’ve yet to take a deep dive into it. My topline remark is that if the “internet 3.0” is supposed to be built on trust, then these pump-and-dump schemes, price controllers, quick cash grabs with BS ICOs (have you read these white papers?), and general tomfoolery will be its swift downfall. I hope that you’re taking money off the table if you’re up on crypto in 2017, and it appears that a lot of people did last week. It’s only natural for people to have commitment issues when you’re liquid. So volatile and nefarious. Warren Buffett even said he’d want a five-year put on crypto ([Barron’s](https://www.barrons.com/articles/warren-buffett-wants-a-five-year-put-on-bitcoin-1515597388)). It’s funny to think that perhaps the best alt coin is the one the criminal underworld uses as cash, a bit more stable. My favorite quote in this space is from NYT’s Mike Isaac blasting someone on charitable crypto donations ([Twtr](https://twitter.com/MikeIsaac/status/950430971144187904)). My favorite announcement is probably the most boring — IBM and Maersk to utilize blockchain to manage the logistics and shipping supply chain process ([IBM](https://www-03.ibm.com/press/us/en/pressrelease/51712.wss)). Great use case for the technology, plus I just love containers and ports — Lego for adults. Check out the Container podcast in the “listen” section below — it’s my fave podcast by a long shot. I need to run to drop off a screener and hydrate before our infamous party tonight. Catch you all next week\!
