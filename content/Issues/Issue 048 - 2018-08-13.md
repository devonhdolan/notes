# Issue 48

Hello\! I had another successful weekend getting away from NYC. I was on a retreat upstate at the Garrison Institute with the great folks of New Inc. Everyone in attendance was some sort of artist, entrepreneur, hacker, professor, curator, scientist, or business person… and the conversations were flowing. I hate to sound like a snob, but the experience was highly cerebral and technical, and I was absolutely stimulated by it. I was fortunate to engage in heady debates around affective computing, big data, deep learning, privacy (surveillance/encryption/sabotage), communication systems, and network theory; many of which were crossed with sentience, moral rights, social contracts, and civil liberties, but with a core focus on ethical value systems. These issues are extremely topical in today’s age because public and private institutions are constantly gathering and monitoring our behaviors, interpreting patterns from that data, and using it for their own benefit (financially, politically, or otherwise). It’s difficult to discuss these topics objectively because there are a wide range of implicit biases that support our ideologies. Morality is subjective. As I see it, to provoke new insight and meaning around these subjects is through motivated discourse from a mix of backgrounds and communities. New Inc provided a platform just for that this weekend, and it was a major success. I’d like to continue to participate in similar types of conversations, and plan on initiating them by organizing small social things (read: drinks, walks, walks with drinks etc) with a flexible agenda. Let me know if you’re based in or around NYC and are interested. That’s all for now — heaps of news below. See you next week\!
