# Issue 14

​​Hello from NYC where we had our first snow on Saturday\! The weather, the pine-tree lined bodegas, and Google’s cutesy holiday search banner lead me to a random unimportant observation: the love for producing new holiday films seems to have dwindled, but the tech companies are taking their curatorial approach to content and maximizing on the hits of days past. Amazon Prime is jamming holiday fare down my throat like it’s trying to stuff a goose with its “Holiday Favorites” carousel. Currently three out of the top ten films in the iTunes Store are Christmas classics. This week, Netflix acquired the JINGLE JANGLE pitch from David E. Albert ([Deadline](http://bit.ly/2km2Wai)), which hopefully is bit more endearing than their A VERY MURRAY CHRISTMAS special. The recurring seasonality of the holidays creates massive opportunities for CPG brands ([L2](http://bit.ly/2yeWs1R)), and the same should be said for that habitual, precious family bonding time for the movies. But now we all get the gift of a massive Hollywood tentpole (cough, STAR WARS, cough, not complaining) and the studios call it a day. Where’s the heart? Anyways, to end today’s spiel, check out the “Simpsons Films” Twitter handle — it’s ingenious ([link](http://bit.ly/2Aujc4h)).
