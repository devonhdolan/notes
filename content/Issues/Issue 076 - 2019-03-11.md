# Issue 76

Hello\! I’ve been in Bondi the past few days — this place, and all the people here, are beautiful. It’s actually silly. Okay, as a few of you know, I’ve been closely following the rise of digital art in recent years as I believe there’s a lot of opportunity to be had in the space (e.g. [issue 55](https://mailchi.mp/666c397391f5/temeeco-issue-55)). First let’s drop the “digital” in front of art. It’s cleaner. Same goes for “digital” media. It’s art or it’s not, it’s media or it’s not. It’s a perfunctory qualifier. Digital just implies new. Traditional and digital businesses are integrating — mixed media. To begin, there was a fascinating [piece](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2019/03/ai-created-art-invades-chelsea-gallery-scene/584134/) in the Atlantic this week about AI art that got me scratching my chin\*. I’d argue that nearly all digital art falls under the domains of CGI and VFX (animation, modeling, mo cap), and AI art is a distinct burgeoning field that’s also housed under digital because it’s new. The means of producing AI and CGI work are wildly different, so they’re separated (for now). I’m intrigued by the cross section, which I think is found in certain formats of data visualization. Data is the new paint. I grew up hypnotized by music visualizers and the concept of [cymatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cymatics), so I think that has something to do with my obsession. As with many new media forms, it’s easy to point to the entertainment industry as an early adopter and pioneer. The smartest, most successful, filmmakers are CGI/VFX mavens (Spielberg, Cameron, JJ, Lucas, Scott) that lowered the barrier of entry for the masses. I expect the next crop of great storytellers to emerge from the video game industry as they know the mechanics of story and CGI inside and out, and AI artists will likely be minted from PHD programs and big tech. That’s all for now… Disclaimer, I’m currently taking [AI for Everyone](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-for-everyone) in Coursera, so take whatever I say with a grain of salt (beginner status). Refik Anadol is one of most-followed AI artists, [here](https://www.instagram.com/refikanadol/?hl=en) is his Instagram (it’s pretty badass). Thanks for reading. See you next week.
