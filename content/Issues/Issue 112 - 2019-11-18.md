# Issue 112

Hello\! So this migration from Mailchimp to Medium \+ Substack is going to be an iterative process. I was familiarizing myself with the bells and whistles of this site and realized last issue was made public, and this one’ll be too. I wanted to give you advance warning in one fell swoop that you’ll be receiving an email this week saying you received a “complimentary” subscription. That’s the pro bono for being an OG reader. I’m still learning here \- thanks for bearing with me. I’ll share some takeaways in due time because there are a couple of differences in the services and a surprising lack of info on it.  
Nothing crazy swirling around my head as of late. I put a fair amount of pressure on myself to try to come up with a diatribe with digressions for this preamble, and have been pretty disappointed in myself recently. I harken back to Eleanor Roosevelt’s quote about discussing ideas, events, and people. I find commenting and opining on the news utterly boring. If I see another streaming wars article, I will gouge my eyes out with a spoon. My crutch is my observations and activities from the last week. So, here are a few:  
1\) I attended RLab’s science fair and met with [all the bootcamp companies](https://medium.com/@therlabnyc/meet-the-teams-in-rlabs-xr-startup-bootcamp-c4c4235f7320). I’m always curious at who turns out at these types of events, mostly classmates this time around. Excited to see how they progress in a month for their demo day on Dec 12th. Let me know if you want to join me in attending. It’d be sweet to get a group together. There’s a volumetric filmmaker event later that day too at WarnerMedia. It’ll be a good one. 2\) Netflix. They did a mini explainer video on the actual history of The King \- [found here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l9hFxE2GlmU) \- which I really appreciate. I almost always do some sort of post-movie investigation, whether it be reading a review or two (public and critic) or watching a hackneyed video essay. I will only watch a trailer beforehand (and suspiciously take to word of mouth of course). On Netflix, I think they got rid of their recently watched tab \- strange. Last note on Netflix (re Irishman), Scorcese is to gangsters as Marvel is to superheroes. Both are franchises, capiche? 3\) I saw Ford v Ferrari and really enjoyed it. It was pure Americana poetry with a dash of propaganda. Satisfying. It had the full kit and kaboodle. In entertainment, most producers and execs are out to build an undeniable package (cast \+ crew), how about flipping it to try to create something truly unforgettable. I couldn’t tell you half the stuff I watched last week (and it was all made).  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
