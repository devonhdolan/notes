# Issue 256: issue 256 - deadfish from within

This is last week’s dispatch. I have no excuse, I wasn’t hacked. This week’s arrives tomorrow.

Hello! I search for it. A feeling I used to have. The high I seek. The a-ha moment. Climax.

It now comes infrequent, insignificant. The machine fills in my gaps. Synaptic (mis)fire. The lightbulb above my head dims, to dust. I get answers without understanding.

I can’t get no - satisfaction. 

Atrophy, a phenomenon. We've outsourced our mental faculties. I think therefore I am? Swipe swipe swipe.

We are parrots. Gone do we “break” story. ‘Tis a structure to be pattern-matched. To oblivion. Nothing’s original, everything’s derivative. Yeah we know, foundational models ate everything. Regurgitate. 

Go DIY. The antidote, artisanal. Word wrestle. A locked room with pencil and paper, an hour or two. Scrawl. Call ‘em morning pages. Those are these preambles. I scratch that forgotten itch. Here I earnestly self inflict - pleasure, pain. Some nihilistic representation of self. Paint and pour. My heart out. 

Labor of love. This is my form of productivity. It is a treat. Recess. A reward. I’m still getting back into the habit. A knife sharpens. 

Though I have a problem: endless ideas, some lack of focus (distractions abound!). A lost library. I used to have too many potential projects: essays, stories, startups, and client work. I still do. But they now live in my receptacle, a heep of project files. Chat history. As barely working Claudeslop artifacts, decks, and documents.  

Good enough for me, good enough for thee. No. It’s litter. Used tissues from a quickie. Caught in the act. I promised my wife I wouldn’t bring up auto-eroticism with AI any more. Whoops. It’s my gutterbrain.

The masochistic machination, a process. Here’s a page of something I’ve been chewing on - eat my used gum. I hand it over. It spits back a fresh pack. I go a couple rounds, we call it iteration. Blow some bubbles, smoke up the wazoo. Finito. I have “worked” through a project. Frictionless. No pain, no pleasure. The final version reads okay. The scope seems sound. It rings true. Nothing gained.

Applied knowledge. You cannot be both teacher and student, grading yourself. The public is the arena. Have the discipline to ship, have the respect (for yourself and the other) to let people grade. Know it will be lost to the algorithm. Sacred garden, ha! 

The flower bed needs water.I do nothing. I can’t be bothered. The spark came as easily as it went, it is now housed in the rubbish bin, in need of disposal. Left to be forgotten in the dump. Landfill. I barely retain anything from them, recall is shot. Don’t do yourself this disservice. Pester your preciousness (we’re a LOTR kick).

Here’s my contradiction. I shared my first one last week. I just got off a call about it. It’s an art exhibit. They want to take it out on my behalf. I’m flummoxed. Maybe I should do this more often. My trash, treasure?

Do not make me play your prompt. But here’s mine. Insult to injury.

That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Dumpster dive. See ya next week.
