# Issue 147

Hello\! It’s nice to be back in Laguna. There’s nothing like starting the day with a quick dip in the Pacific. A couple slight observations and musings for this week that harken back to older issues. There are a lot of friendly new faces around here, so I like linking to the archives, and well, I’m probably being a bit lazy too. A trip down memory land.  
Discovery goes hand in hand with curation. I think Publisher Weekly could get dressed down for today’s age; unbundled into specific niches. Careful considerations. I hope by creators, not by critics. Which makes me think of Wilde’s The Critic as Artist. It also got me thinking about the much-adored Pauline Kael, who was chewed up and spit out by Paramount in ‘79, then under the reign of Barry Diller (a bo$$). I know few \**recent*\* critics who made the transition into becoming the almighty creator. Now it seems, more than ever, the aggregate audience usurps the critic. Rotten tomatoes. Goodreads. Cahiers du Cinéma. Oh the gloried French New Wave. Aesthetics win.  
Only five weeks ago I wrote about about novel use-cases for [virtual production and how it could be incorporated into theater](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-142-6-22-20-646a291769db). I was watching 60 Minutes last week and was shocked to find out that West Side Story (the 2020 Broadway revival) has a giant screen above the stage, which looks so epic (and expensive). Spielberg has a West Side Story film that’s set to come out at the end of the year. Curious to know if there’s crossover between the play and the film. Real-time motion capture on stage is on the horizon, and the WSS production is certainly a step in the right direction. Can’t wait.  
The next big thing is making customers out of those who don’t have the *current* big thing. I wrote about [providing internet and low-latency content](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-26-3-12-18-1035f28559a4) to emerging markets back in March of 2018\. There’s been so much hullaballoo around big tech and private equity investing in Jio Platforms\* ([Barrons](https://www.barrons.com/articles/indias-jio-platforms-is-emerging-as-a-tech-star-google-and-facebook-are-betting-on-it-51595770200)), an Indian telco con-glom affiliated with Reliance (which owns a piece of Amblin, and a grip of [other influential entertainment companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Entertainment)). I’m psyched to share that my friends at [1497](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/hollywood-inclusion-lab-south-asian-screenwriters-selects-mentors-1302679) and [The Salon](https://deadline.com/2020/07/20th-century-fox-tv-reena-singh-hillman-grad-rishi-rajani-the-salon-south-asian-mentorship-program-1202990949/) are doing big things to mentor, shepherd, and accelerate the careers of South Asian creators in Hollywood. I believe it’s a beautiful approach to start very specific, traffic in a narrow lane \- though the medium can be expansive \- and then expand outward. Good stuff.  
As I was flipping through the archives trying to find my writings on entertainment for developing countries \- I really do need to title these things \- I stumbled upon some of my Quibi issues, when it was then known as NewTV. [Here’s one when I shared meeting notes](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-42-7-2-18-46032f0d0965) from one of the agencies, and [another where I spit venom](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-59-10-29-18-5bd3f273d4b9) in Oct of 2018, exactly a year and a half before launch. Welp, at least some friends got paid :)  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
