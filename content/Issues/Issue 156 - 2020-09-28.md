# Issue 156

Hello, from New Zealand\! This is home for the foreseeable future. I’ll be working LA hours, but a day ahead. The main purpose for us to be here is rather grim, but on the flip-side, we’re in NZ. This is the promised land. Production is a go. Covid cases are all but obsolete. There are no fires, nor murder hornets. And I finally have health care\! The last near 18 months in the states have been a gamble, and it was certainly a death wish riding my bike in NYC… worth it.  
We’re currently in day 3 (of 14\) of managed isolation in a hotel next to the airport. Wifi is seriously pitiful \- they preemptively took my shoelaces and belt upon arrival. No local SIM card either. The staff are suspiciously nice, Cuckoo’s Nest. Anmari might divorce me after this. We’re cooped up lookin’ out the window like puppies, eating the equivalent of airplane dog-food (at least it’s free). We touched down on Saturday and the militia immediately shipped us down to Christchurch for two weeks (like planning on being in LA then flown to San Francisco). I have Tenet to see carefree, upon my release. I’ll be living in Auckland, and have a few trips planned down to Wellington to visit Weta and the blue people on Avatar. I’m trying to fulfill my true destiny in being cast as an orc on Amazon’s Lord of the Rings series. Two billion dollar+ projects are currently filming out here. Nuts.  
If you have kiwi friends, I’d like to meet them, especially if they’re named Taika. I’m pretty dialed with the film commission and some industry people out here, so I’m going to try to get creative in being a sort of conduit to NZ greenlights for my fanciful producer friends back in the states (hi, that might be you). Or I might just retire early and start a sheep farm. Having gone through the visa process – such a slog – I can say I’m pretty much qualified to be an immigration attorney out here. It was.. a lot.  
And on the professional front, I’m thrilled to share that my friends at Artie are starting to emerge out of stealth. [Here’s a profile from the new LA tech pub, dot.LA, that dropped last week](https://dot.la/artie-2647773915.html). Artie is building a next-gen instant gaming platform that sidesteps mobile app stores and their 30% fees, a very hot topic right now. They run Unity games on the cloud and render the graphics at the edge on players’ phones without any app download needed (rather than streaming video like typical cloud games, which causes latency). The result is higher-quality instant games than the HTML5 games you’ve seen on Snapchat and FB Messenger, and their links can be shared anywhere on social, like YouTube links for video.  
Some of the first games also incorporate optional AI gameplay features, including voice, vision, and texting inputs. We’re leaning into partnering with celebs and IP holders with large followings on social media to promote organic UA \- user acquisition. The cap table is a who’s who of media, tech, and entertainment: Chad Hurley (YouTube), Mark Pincus (Zynga), etc. Hit me up if you want to learn more. They may take another check or two in the round and go above their initial target for the right investor. More to come, very soon :)  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
