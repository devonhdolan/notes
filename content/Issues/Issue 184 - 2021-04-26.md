# Issue 184

Hello\! I’ve been thinking about the resell, secondary market and how it could open up new income lines for creators and their works. Re-usable *tickets* with set royalty splits, sounds like it could get pyramid-y. It didn’t really occur to me prior to the whole NFT thing, but here we are. The fashion industry and its consumers have embraced it, turning it inside out. It challenges the current thinking around entertainment: subscribe or free plus ads. The distributor or filmmaking team would need to limit and control access to encourage people to transact on a one-off or bundled basis. There could be box sets of director editions. Collections. Stubs. Hard part is marketing an initiative of this sort in plain-speak and reaching global scale. Align incentives, on consignment. Winner winner, chicken dinner.  
I miss social interactions in the workplace. I think the recovery in the US is coming quick and a hybrid at-home/office work setup will be the norm. There’s something to be said about everyone being focused and collaborative \- working in symphony \- in the same room. Jamming. Togetherness. I’ve been a lone wolf the last 2.5 years (\!) so I’ve grown accustomed to self-motivating, but I gotta say there’s something special about teamwork and feeding off everyone’s energy \- sounds vampiric. Guess I’m no introvert.  
I’ll quickly give my opinion of the Oscars because it might be expected. The tone felt off, it generally lacked enthusiasm and fervor. Triumph, to a degree. Probably because last year was the worst year ever to be in the entertainment industry, and holding a pageant to celebrate might be construed as a bit tone-deaf. Not saving Best Picture for last was a strange decision. If you’ve been following along, Nomadland was a shoe-in to win as it pretty much swept all the award shows leading up to last night. There wasn’t a sense of mystery, not anything like last year with Parasite ([issue 123](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-123?)).  
Oscar viewership has steadily dropped \- it used to hover around the 40m mark a decade ago \- last night was just shy of 10m. The producers need to go non-exclusive, split up the rights to broadcast on YouTube and elsewhere. I had to find a bootlegged stream. The industry’s biggest event has seemingly grown uneventful. My two cents.  
And thanks to Zach for sharing this [FT piece](https://www.ft.com/content/85ff1488-82ec-11ea-b6e9-a94cffd1d9bf) about China and science fiction that I asked for last week \- it doesn’t quite nail exactly what I was looking for with the state backing sci-fi writers, but close enough. Wild they’re censoring the Chloé Zhao victories.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Did you watch Mank? See you next week.
