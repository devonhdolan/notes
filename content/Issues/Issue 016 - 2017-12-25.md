# Issue 16

Merry Christmas from New Zealand\! This place is as mystical as I could’ve imagined. If you ever have the chance to visit, take it. Okay, so with 30+ hours of travel time to get here, complemented by no phone/laptop use (courtesy of the airline), I spent too much time devouring movies. Loads of movies. So much so that my eyes started to hurt. Seriously. I took a quick break from the screen, gave myself a temple massage, and mustered up the opportunistic question: why aren’t there podcasts, books, and comics on in-flight entertainment systems? I couldn’t pin-down a straight answer except that there might be issues with translating speech for podcasts, which I noted in my “good” idea pad. Then, I got to thinking about media in contained spaces, and felt so lucky that airline companies don’t have transit advertisements, similar to the ones found in NYC subway cars. I’m sure they could charge a premium on those lucrative, dastardly, media buys. Alright, I’m veering off on a bit of a tangent — I hope you all have a wonderful day with loved ones. (:
