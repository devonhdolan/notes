# Issue 207

Hello\! Bonjour\! Hola\! We collectively welcome the era of internationalization. The slow erosion of territory and embrace of the other. We now play across border with beauty, spectacle. Entertainment is the world’s language. It’s music. We dance. Our future’s closed captioned… or not. No matter the case, the story must travel.  
Globalization through Localization.  
I firmly believe it’ll be the next evolution in media \- I don’t say that lightly. It’s a tune I’ve been humming here the last couple of years. The gold rush is imminent, the core tenet — Sound.  
Audio Automation. Mixed & Leveled.  
Content, meet global scale. Investors like to speak to their pick-and-shovel plays, I think we’re well on our way to the bridge-and-tunnel stage. Railroads, tolls. The board is set, the entities are no longer disparate. Streamers own customer and creator. Pay TV outputs are cracking under our feet. Talent deals are being reimagined. And people are *still* coming online. Eager to be entertained by *all*.  
Everything must now be media company… widespread cultural phenomena.  
Access. Prioritize the arrival of the emerging market. Big tech subsidizes connectivity for the back-half. The rest of the world sees it as utility. Small satellite constellations are launched with catapult, laser, and rocket. Fiber is laid. Underwater cables are piped. Routing protocols. Traffic plumbed. Exchanging information. Our world, superhighway.  
Pivot tables. The content opportunity swells with the population size and ISPs. [Here’s a Wiki list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_languages_by_number_of_native_speakers) of languages by number of native speakers. Fund the search. My ears perk to African French, Hindi, Spanish, Indonesian (Malay), and Arabic. Think macro. Scout out those with the state’s support.  
Spoken knowledge transcribed in real-time. Our voice as compressed data. Apple, Google, Amazon collect our speech with their virtual assistants (Siri, Alexa). Agents of interaction, recognition. The data is shielded. It is value. With it, AI models can learn the subtleties and nuances of conversation. Our intonations, animation, sarcasm, and inflection. Our rhythms. Subtitles…  
Synthesis. The human must finish what the machine prints. Our entertainment will be instantly translated to any native tongue. The actor’s lips and dialogue coming from them will be unrecognizably deep-faked. It’ll be the greatest magic trick ever performed. A foundational format. The race to the audio track will be done discrete, in gentle whisper and hushed voice.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. But does it translate? See you next week.
