# Issue 178

Hello\! So I’ll probably break this into a two or three part series as I took the weekend off for my one-year anniversary, you all knew about the matrimony in [issue 152](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-152) before I shared with the rest of the world on social. [Here are some photos](https://www.instagram.com/p/CMX2ttxFmog/) (my IG account) from the weekend and last couple months in NZ. It’s funny, most of my best friends from home don’t even read this thing, but I actually called them that weekend to share the happy news like a real person. We didn’t post anything about it for six months… secrets are fun. I can’t believe it’s been a full year. So much has changed. Damn.  
So speaking of childhood friends, one of my besties went viral about a week ago [for this video that he took of a whale’s eye](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CL-s5r2nJ7G/). It is otherworldly. Beautiful. I shed a tear. He [followed it up with another banger](https://www.instagram.com/p/CMFMwhSnRWf/) that took off like a rocket. In a week he got about 15k new followers. I know a couple of NatGeo execs are on here, call me if you want to discuss the footage (email reply direct). Coincidentally, this is the same place in Baja that we visited in [issue 152](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-152) (referenced above) for our ‘honeymoon’ and I shared a short montage from that trip then, which definitely did not go viral. \[Disclaimer: I should quickly mention that I’m not speaking for my buddy here, but he did give me approval to publish this.\]  
I bring this up because dozens of meme and outdoor outlets reposted my friend’s videos across a bunch of different social sites, many did not ask for permission nor did they give appropriate credit. WTF. They cashed in on its social status, garnering hundreds of thousands of likes, views, etc. My friend has been a professional nature / adventure filmmaker for almost two decades, and is borderline left in the dust \- holding the bag. The state of social media is seriously broken, but who is to blame. Consumer behavior has drastically changed where everything is expected to be free, and creator-distributor power dynamics have gone lopsided by the ‘brands’ with millions of followers and/or dollars.  
“Being paid in exposure is absolute bullshit. Exposure does not pay rent.” \-Socrates.  
The irony here is that I sift through dozens of other newsletters and rip out the headlines that interest me and drop them in this thing…. and over lunch I’ll waste five minutes of my day to mind-numbingly scroll through some comedy meme account that just re-post TikToks. I am part of the problem. I don’t have the solution (except maybe: lawyer up), but I’ve spent a fair amount of time thinking and writing about this ecosystem… and the stock license content sites like Getty, Unsplash, and Shutterstock probably won’t come to the rescue. So two quick mentions before I jump.  
[In issue 115](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-115), I alluded that there’s this strange “clout transfer” model in reference to creative-collective social accounts like Roam Media ([IG](https://www.instagram.com/roam/?hl=en)), which ultimately will probably become more individualistic in nature. I think online media companies like these will devolve into a sort of YC-like accelerator for digital centric creators. Identify a community \- or an account with audience (or on the brink of breaking out) \- that fits your preferred genre (niche), join it and *get paid to make content with and for them*, and slowly build a following off of there’s like some sort of beneficial, positive parasite. Then branch off on your own. Voila, there’s your digital talent incubator. Conversion, of both earned and owned media. Hell, one of the more personable VFX artists from the beloved Corridor Crew [did just this in their latest episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pMIVZ9bQ3uo). Love to see it. More in next week’s note, but to tease, it will touch on DeFi, legacy media, and going direct-to-consumer.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Nobody likes intermediaries. See you next week.
