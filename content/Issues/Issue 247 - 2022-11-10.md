# Issue 247

Hello\! I’m an automaton, played on loop. Pitch, eat, sleep. Repeat. Routines as rote processes.  
We’re stuck in eternal elevator, all acting. Robots. Our mouths open. A squawk box. It crackles to life, in humble salutation.  
I’ve introduced myself thousands of times. I give my same spiel. I make the same stupid little jokes. They land; they laugh. I feel good. Nobody refuses a harmless hit of dopamine. I use the same intonations, my ears bleed from my faux enthusiasm that sounds oh so real. They don’t know the difference; I’ve convinced myself of it. Maybe I don’t either. It’s a well-honed craft.  
I read from the same script. More artifice than artificial. I let the other person read from theirs. We smile. We judge. The quest for appeal. Who made themselves sound better. Are they articulate? Beautiful? Does their taste align with mine? Their interests? Do we consummate now or later? Or never. When eyes see red, malevolence or lust. Spirited. Away.  
We spin the same yarn. Run the same wheel. Gauntlet. Round and round we go, the ascending drain. New people. Similar conversations. Maybe new projects. The cycle continues.  
Gesticulations. Nobody smiled behind the mask, we just squinted our eyes. We developed early-onset crow’s feet… and attention disorders. We did it for the other. For them, we say to ourselves. We are good. Selfless. Sanitized.  
Larp. It is not improvisation. More like.. interpretation. Instantiation, implementation. Intuition.  All the world’s a stage. [Shakespearean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_the_world%27s_a_stage).  
Hope is lost anticipation. The cynicism is at least real. We grow; calloused with rejection.  
Fake it till you make it. No, break it. A lullaby. Talk about it or be about it. Forget about it.  
Credentialism. Build immediate rapport. I consider giving myself over to some sort of virtual entity. It is lifeless anyways. An embodiment, disfigurement. We all know what we want from the situation. A simulation. “A yes.” A talent attachment. A capital injection. Distribution. Love. A job, for them or for us. Paid or pro bono. I’ve mostly given myself away for free. We do it for approval. For affirmation. To get made, or to get killed. We die by the project. We succumb to its glacial pace. Pump milk and honey into the machine. The system is bloated. A racket.  
Kamikaze. The ultimate self-sacrifice.  
Catacombs. It’s a playing field. A team and its players. Bones are the ashes beneath our cleats. A company and its employees. Disgruntled. The jerseys we wear, clean or dirty. Put me in coach.  
It’s a sport. It is sales. Pitching is training for the big league. Prepping for go time. Being in-character is exhausting. It’s constant. The calls go on for hours upon hours, each and every day. Every year. The disassociation from the personal; the professional.  
The playbook doesn’t make the player.  
It is the *act* of playing. Losers don’t even partake. If you’re complaining about it, you’re at least *doing* it. Nobody wants to hear your lamentations, nor associate with them. The ultimate energy vampire. A parasite. A quick pass. Hell, when I had a few hundred bucks in my bank account a year ago (true story), I’d still paint a beautiful self-portrait. It works. Though I was dead behind the eyes. It really is darkest before dawn.  
Perception is a warped reflection of reality.  
The game needs to be omni-win. Collectivism. Not all is zero sum competition. Though that has its moments. My little victory does not come at a cost to you. Dog eat dog. All for one. One for all.  
The search for autonomy. Agency. Protect yourself. Your only goalkeeper.  
We can rebuild him. We have the technology.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Beep beep boop. Game, set, match. See ya next week. 3 left.
