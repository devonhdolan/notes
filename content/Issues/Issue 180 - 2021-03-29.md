# Issue 180

Hello\! So this one’s the final piece of a loosely connected series of three, here is [one](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-178-3-15-21-1899575aa83c?source=friends_link&sk=7da89e35b778f4b55b0c82c89409c3bb) and [two](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-179-3-22-21-9152e4f78487?source=friends_link&sk=b321fe40bd44f4b0ffcc8cdd157d7613). The first was around the individualistic nature of online content distribution, fleeting virality, and accounts re-posting creative’s work under the guise of exposure. The second was around the online creator economy, owning the audience relationship, creating cost effective formats, and generally the end-goal of going traditional in success. And this post will hopefully, rather chaotically marry them all together, it will touch on creative collaboration, interest (incentive) alignment, and those elusive accelerants. Now kiss, and prepare for battle.  
The Odessa Steps. It takes buy-in from a whole host of power players \- participants \- to get a film/tv project made, and no one is trying to make anything bad. The activities boil down to a few core concepts (“the steps”): identify story, attach talent, raise capital, oversee production, and secure distribution. It takes dozens, if not hundreds, of relationships to will a project into existence. To get it across the finish line. Take it one step at a time, know that each step is just as hard as the next. Try not to play out too many scenarios down the line, or you’ll trip yourself up off the starting block. Potemkin.  
Assemble an army. The industry is built on strings of delicate, safe-guarded relationships. They’re precious, protected. Founded in trust. They usually start, uhm, relationally (friends of friends ad inf.), and with time comes validation. And once the stage is set, the favor exchange begins, friendships become banked. And you’ve only got a few shots on a goal as they’re centered in taste (subjective) and opportunity. Prioritized based on name recognition and influence. Reputation. Hand-curated selection. If you send garbage, waste time, and support a team who fails to execute \- your node, lineage, branch, can get chopped in an instant, banished to the bottom of the submission pile \- never to be seen again. Judgement day. Declaring a project is emblematic of your tastes is intrinsically tying your reputation, your ethos \- your identity \- to it. You wholly back it. You believe in it. Faith. Fight.  
“Trust takes years to build, seconds to break, and forever to repair.” \-The Kremlin.  
Storm the kingdom gates. Each step can be unpacked at the individual level. Industry professionals generally specialize in one or two steps in the content value chain \- they can stake their domain knowledge and access to its stakeholders, which is borderline unquantifiable. Terms truly aren’t that complicated to get a deal done, but getting incumbents, the heads of state, to take you and your project seriously is alchemy. The transfer of audience, relationships, is value. It’s patronage. Surround yourself with those who ideally share similar ideals in taste and genre, and fill in your stop-gaps with complementary skills and relationships. Shared passion, economy. Trade together, vote together. Share in the upside. Agree to the terms upfront, co-sign. At the end of the day, the creator drives everything. Work for the project, not each other. Self-govern, transparently. Camaraderie. Democracy.  
Code is law. Aspects of the steps can be broken out \- finance and distribution can be, and usually is interlinked, and talent is bolted onto the existing system \- first looks de-risk the long-lead sales cycle. It’s all messily glued together at current. And near impossible to penetrate. Although I might not be saying much here, and am probably stating the obvious, there are new methods (math) that can be applied to this madness to re-patch, re-tool the creative infrastructure. Contracts. Bring clarity to the opaque, ugly sides of the business, which is literally the business in and of itself \- build literacy and turnkey solutions to company formation and financials. Perception becomes truth. There’s an underlying structure to what I’m proposing, but I’m purposefully tap dancing around it. [This was my a-ha moment back in issue 136](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-136). It’s what I needed most when I took my leap of faith, and I still do to be honest. A social system. An industry-specific platform, a marketplace, to serve creators who believe in themselves.  
“The most powerful person in the world is the storyteller. The storyteller sets the vision, values, and agenda for an entire generation that’s to come.” \-Steve Jobs.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Go create. See you next week.
