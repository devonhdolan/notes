# Issue 106

Hello\! I watched Joker on Friday. It’s certainly a film that necessitates a productive conversation afterward, and if a piece of art does that — it’s a winner in my eyes. People tend to be surprised by this, but I never read critic reviews before watching a movie. I don’t care to be influenced by their ideologies prior to viewing. Craft your own opinion. I had a friend lambast the film (something about incel glorification), but I’m near positive he had those sound bites loaded before even waltzing into the theater. Same person had choice words about Green Book, a wholly enjoyable film. Parroting high horse hot takes drives me mad. I find that critics still wield some power as a collective, but individually they’ve fallen by the wayside. Just checking IMDB, user ratings for the film sit at a 9.0, while RT is 70% and Metacritic is 55%. That’s a strange, nose in the air, disconnect. Everyone’s a critic these days. The film also did \$96m its opening weekend, so yeah, it’s a winner. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
