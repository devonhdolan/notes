# Issue 94

Hello\! Plenty of announcements I found relevant last week, so it’s a longer one. The next couple of preambles are going to be shorter than usual as I have a few projects that need more of my undivided attention, so I might focus on a couple of slight observations coupled with maybe a swirling thought or two. It’ll feel more personal rather than prescriptive. Last week’s writing was far too flowery and wordy, I know. So let’s begin. I feel that there’s been a rise of the non-actor, meaning someone who plays themselves onscreen, almost like a caricature. It’s more of an embodiment of their social media shtick, and how they want to be perceived or desired by the public. That’s fine, I guess. I like tradition though. VidCon happened last week, this week it’s Comic Con. I think influencer culture has done infiltrated popular culture, especially amongst gen-z. When I was growing up, counter-culture was the aspiration. Surf, skate, and streetwear reigned supreme. Now, we find people peddling coffee grind face scrubs, voodoo crystals, and hoodies labeled with the name of their YouTube channels. I guess that’s the new punk. It’s set our media world ablaze. Reality tv was the tinder, ancillary revenue lines (merch, tix) were the kindling, and the democratization of content was the fuel for this fire. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
