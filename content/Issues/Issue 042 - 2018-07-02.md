# Issue 42

Hello\! I have a gift for you. No, it’s not Bitcoin\*. It’s [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/zhu9770hnr5tdil/Katzenberg%20TV%20Newco.pdf?dl=0). These are notes from a credible source about Jeff Katzenberg’s NewTV. I haven’t confirmed these with the folks at NewTV/WndrCo, so don’t take it as gospel. It is not an internal document — it’s secondhand and a couple times removed before it was sent to me. It doesn’t contain many key insights, and the notes will generally confirm your suspicions if you’ve been following the company closely as Katzenberg has been pretty straight forward with his strategy: “the mobile-first HBO.” I’m still scratching my head with the Meg Whitman hire. A 61-year-old billionaire (three times over) who most recently ran HP was tapped to run an incredibly well-capitalized premium short-form content startup geared towards millennials… okay? I do wish them the best of luck. We need more meaningful bite-sized content of substance\*\*. The world is full of mindless distractions on social media, we need to ameliorate the ‘snackable content’ landscape before it’s beyond repair. See you next week.
