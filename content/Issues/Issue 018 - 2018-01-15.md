# Issue 18

Happy MLK day everyone\! Keeping the note somewhat light today and jotting down technologies and ideas that I’m excited for in 2018\. Over the next few months I’ll be writing more in-depth pieces on how I envision these topics growing in the current temee landscape. Lidar assets get incorporated into game engines (watch the Metalhead episode in the latest Black Mirror season: S4:E5). The news media makes its own voice apps — I’m tracking [Earplay](https://www.earplay.com/), [SpokenLayer](https://www.spokenlayer.com/), and [PullString](https://www.pullstring.com/) in this space. Podcasts will be Hollywood’s new testing ground, then super short films will be used to drum up material interest in concepts. I’d like to test this theory and produce a sci-fi mashup with [Gimlet](https://gimletmedia.com/), [Oats Studios](https://oatsstudios.com/), and [Blackpills](https://www.blackpills.com/). Character-based AR propels the medium forward. I’m waiting with bated breath to see how [Niantic Labs](https://nianticlabs.com/blog/wizardsunite/) rolls out its Harry Potter experience. Brands start to use their logo with computer vision/AR in outdoor advertising formats. They could capitalize on Niantic’s activation this way. Premium VOD will finally take shape with [Screening Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Screening_Room). [MoviePass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MoviePass) gets hit with a major lawsuit. Ephemerality goes ephemeral — bye Snap. The resurgence of live media, online. Long Disney. I have a few prepared remarks for my take on crypto in next week’s letter. Hint, I’m very short-term bearish (and I could be dead wrong). I’m headed to Sundance this week — send me any tips or hit me up if you want to meet there. We’re going to be full on representing 13 films. See you in Park City\!
