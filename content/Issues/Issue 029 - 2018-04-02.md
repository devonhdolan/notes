# Issue 29

​​Hello\! So, I’ve been thinking a lot about city planning, and I’m no urbanist. Ever since I visited Florence last November I’ve taken a real interest in the mapping of local environments to improve society. To propagate, I think we’re going to enter into an architectural renaissance to accommodate and optimize for frontier technologies in a zero-marginal cost society. Like, we’re going to see self-driving flying cars in our lifetime. W.T.F. We need a visionary akin to Walt Disney to dream up a fanciful new prototype city, like EPCOT, to understand our infrastructure needs. Financiers are making their pick and shovel plays in these technologies (lidar), but who will be building the transportation systems of our future? Municipalities? Likely not. Our capitalistic world will be even more privatized. I’m curious to know who will be developing the Roman Roads Version 2.0. Above ground; not under it (Boring Company), nor on it (Hyperloop). To leave on a foreboding note: aside from your professional skill-set, how can you make your community future-proof? See you next week.
