# Issue 39

Hello\! My favorite films from 2014 (Ex Machina) and 2017 (Call Me By Your Name) collide in [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ft-5UzXka_c&feature=youtu.be) fun mashup video made by Cinetic’s ex-intern Brian Baldocchi. This was his last assignment before leaving us for LA to be a full-on content creator. I feel that the notion of purely being a filmmaker is lost on the ambitious film school grads because they realize that there’s opportunity aplenty to make entertainment for a range of destinations to grow your brand/namesake and to build an audience. Anyway, I hope you all enjoy the clip — it put a smile on my face when I first watched it. See you next week.
