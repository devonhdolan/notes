# Issue 89

Hello\! I settled on a name for what I’m calling my newco and went to lock down the domain and socials. I didn’t get the exact one I desired, but snatched up everything around it. After my experience, I figured this has to be one of the earliest headaches for young companies. So I asked around, and the consensus was yes, it certainly is. (Something to note, always check the trademark database to ensure that you can use the name you want. Also, just buy the domain name after searching for it because web crawlers monitor domain registrar searches and bots will beat you to the punch — pretty much Ticketmaster on crack. The .com still holds the crown in my book.) There should really be a streamlined domain and social account management system to gather these up for people in one fell swoop. I’d be curious to know how much seed money goes into acquiring domains and the like. It can surely be a decent capital outlay. Last comment on this topic: I loathe cybersquatters, they’re like the first iteration of digital collectible dummies. They suck, along with patent trolls. A couple other things on my mind: 1\) I wonder how much Facebook is paying the Smith family. Will et al have really doubled down on their IG content, and I doubt Will created his profile for free. Jada and Will both have FB Watch shows: Red Table Talk and Bucket List. These programs should just be called lifestyle envy. It’s working, I’m jealous. They’re lobbying the globe. Some of Will’s vids are also on YouTube — curious how that works. 2\) I recently came across an amazing mobile game \+ tv show called [Avo](https://www.playdeo.co.uk/) that nails my thesis to a tee. I’ve been sharing it directly with a gaggle of people who are interested in the same things as me (e.g. AR, customizable avatars, STEM-friendly story, CGI character in a live action world, episodic storytelling, etc). Like most great things nowadays, I discovered the app through a Medium series — Dan Hill’s [blogs](https://medium.com/a-chair-in-a-room/avo-stadia-arcade-bandersnatch-and-the-new-grammar-of-television-and-games-part-1-form-is-cf5188d142b1) to be exact. 3\) I doubt The Office would have been made in today’s environment. Too many uncouth jokes. It’s one of Netflix’s most-watched shows, so there’s that. It’s just reliable entertainment. 4\) This morning I watched the PC Gaming Show on Twitch, Twitter, and Facebook. Congrats to my friends at [Hovercast](https://www.hovercast.com/) for making the livestream interactive. Killing it. 5\) Thank you Alex for the love. I don’t really market this thing because I find self-promotion unsavory, so I mostly rely on word of mouth (read: email forwards and shoutouts). Redesign coming soon though. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
