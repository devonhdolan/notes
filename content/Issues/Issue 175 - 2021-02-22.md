# Issue 175

Hello\! A bit late in hitting send today, my bad. A quick thank you to those who might’ve shared [last week’s note](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-174-2-16-21-37344c6367d4?source=friends_link&sk=e98f8ca93c31abe74418b47821dcc58d) \- strangely, it was one of my most widely disseminated to date. If you missed it, I wrote a truth-bomb about the failing middle class of entertainment execs (myself included perhaps) and getting a job in this mess of an industry… and that there’s a sort of loss of initiative, of individualism. Reality is harsh, and I feel a bit of remorse for dream stomping. Welp, you need thick skin so they say.  
Before we get into it, I’m thrilled to share that [Artie announced their \$10m seed round](https://venturebeat.com/2021/02/17/artie-raises-10-million-as-it-pivots-to-make-instant-message-games/) for their *AppStore-less* mobile games, which are instantly playable inside your favorite social media app. [Here’s a sampling](https://twitter.com/ryandhorrigan/status/1363896916505845761) of how a game might appear in your timeline They’re backed by a murderer’s row of strategics. Some names you might recognize: Scooter Braun, Kevin Durant, Mark Pincus, Winklevoss Twins, Josh Harris/David Blitzer, Jon Oringer, Macro (Charles King), Cyan Bannister, WndrCo (Katzenberg), Matthew Ball, Warner Music Group, and many more. Yes, I am involved. We’ve got some seriously exciting projects in the works. If you’re interested in learning more, please email direct. In September’s [issue 156,](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-156?) I shared Artie was in the midst of a raise, and some very, *very* productive things came of it. So yeah, inbox is always open.  
Okay, onto the topic(s) du jour. Let’s talk about sticker slapping. The Tech Twitter hype machine is currently high on NFTs (non-fungible tokens). Some other buzzwords to note: spatial audio, community, virtual production, metaverse, digital collectibles (art), and SPACs. No, I’m not talking about any of these in particular. But rather trend-chasing. Band-wagoning. Digital art is all the rage at the moment. And you know what, jumping on the red hot thing can probably lead to some quick cash (and recognition), and I do love artists getting paid, so what’s to say.  
Namely, I generally see the tech boy types reaping the returns, not the tried and true artists. They seek affirmation, a reflection of self, and pump that particular type. Subjectivity, please meet bias. That, and bad actors quickly appear to squeeze, then tarnish, the fertile land before the real ones can sow seeds. I’ve seen this gross wave of ‘supplantation’ one too many times. A quick reminder: ICOs. What the hell did Chris Woodrow do with all those MovieCoins, [made false promises](https://variety.com/2019/biz/news/moviecoin-ballet-worthless-tokens-1203198171/). Healing takes time.  
I tend to view technology as a tool, not the answer. Prophecies might be declared, golden guises, but they’re often solutions in search of a problem. I do truly appreciate blockchain as a concept, and many of the other ones I rattled off above. But I more readily rejoice in the practical, justifiable applications of these tools \- for storytellers, for artists. For creators.  
I’ve been preaching the digital art space a few years now, [here’s issue 55](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-55-10-1-18-ce15aed33832) from October 2018 where I elaborated some thoughts. I actually explored starting a company around it then \- a type of online gallery with friendlier economics built on smart contracts \- as I’ve been around a deal or two in this space. I’m still actively exploring it, hit me up to discuss if you want. Always happy to talk shop and trade secrets.  
It’s funny, I find that many of my best ideas end up boiling down to the same key ingredients, the digital art galley was just the beginning. The idea I had last summer in [issue 136](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-136) combines many of these elements. Communistic capitalism. I will most definitely rekindle that flame soon. Just trying to action a few things off my plate to give me some more capacity (and cash). To that end…  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Storytelling is information control. See you next week.
