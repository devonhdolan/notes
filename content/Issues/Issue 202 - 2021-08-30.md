# Issue 202

Hello\! Last week’s email was the most shared note since I started this thing, probably because I blatantly wrote about web 3.0. If you read between the lines, it’s pretty obvious I’ve been closely tracking the space, and subtly promoting it, while also doing my part to poke holes. The holes are usually the people. The individuals. The motivations. Faux populism. I’ll get to that in a sec.  
Based on all the positive responses, I moved my flight up to LA to next week. It makes it easier to turn-tail when there’s a maximum security lockdown here in Auckland. It’ll likely remain shut until mid-October, if not longer. I changed travel plans just before the country-wide announcement. It’s illegal to surf or be found too far from home.  
Tough, but understandably fair.  
It puts me back in the mental state of March/April 2020 and I want nothing to do with that. My life, along with so many others, was seriously uprooted. And for those of you who might think the last year in New Zealand was a vacation, know that it wasn’t chock-full of rainbows and butterflies. Fond memories were very much made…  
But the family circumstances are still very dire. My father-in-law has a degenerative disease called multiple systems atrophy, which is not too dissimilar from what Stephen Hawking had with ALS. We were in the right place \- the place we had to be, both physically and situationally. My wife is the real hero. A beautiful caretaker. I just played emotional support animal that got up way too early to send emails.  
To get this back on-track and to draw a rather distasteful analogy, there are less than 600 community cases of delta here. They’re jamming the population of Auckland that’s near 2 million people. Something similar rings true of the perverse martyrdom that is crypto-twitter and its NFT schemes on Discord chats. A few whales and dedicated disciples scream their wins from the rooftops, the crowds gather 'round to buy-in, to be influenced, blessed with fiscal return. As if their salvation comes through this sacrifice. Groupthink, as dangerous as the kool-aid. Sorry. A tad dark.  
Right now it looks to me like a celebrity boxing match. The ‘boxers’ win, as does the broadcaster, the venue and Vegas, but the audience who paid for it ultimately loses. Bet on the house, bail through the side door before the split draw is announced.  
A town square turned casino, en plein air… en ligne. Outside… online.  
Atomized. Forced social graphs and tokens. The explosion \- and exploitation \- of community as culture. As commerce. In the past few weeks I’ve seen a lot of paid private clubs emerge to trade in secret. It’s a high school cafeteria full of factions, cliques, where the barrier to entry is already too high for most. And the reasoning for their existence might be, *just because* or *for the lulz*. Or its function is to pump internet pictures to make a quick buck. It’s bafflingly sceney. Le nouveau riche.  
Alpha. The plebeians now seek acceptance from the patricians \- the landed gentry of crypto-saint. The gatekeepers prevail, they dictate the market from ivory balconies. Soho House Miami, baby. The party’s over, or it might’ve just begun. Time will tell. Exclusivity is not rarity. It separates, divides. Tribalism. Being priced out adds insults to injury. It is reflective our time, individualism triumphs. A regime rebundled. The community as product. Sacrosanct.  
*Corporatist as cooperatist.*  
\-\> Insert Groucho Marx refusing to join clubs quote.  
\-\> Insert Plato line on reluctant leadership.  
Creation is compassion. Alright this rant is getting out of control. I enjoy clubs and communities that serve to celebrate, to share and spread the love. Work backward from community, then for it. The spigot drips to build hype, to build brand. Beauty. Then the conversion bucket spills on both sides. No fomo, pure fun. A fervored audience rows together for the betterment of *all*, positive sum. Play to learn. To earn. Create. Rejoice.  
And to toot my own horn, I started an anonymous little movie club on top of this newsletter, [join here if you want](https://devondolan1.typeform.com/to/sjE6qlFQ) and remember to hit submit. The purpose is just to enjoy some films and deepen our knowledge of the classics, namely the films that had a profound impact on the greats of today. They’re generational artifacts. Shampoo was the first winner, and right now it’s a close race between Touki Bouki and The Wild Geese. Join and I’ll happily share the voting link :)  
Side note, I recently discovered there’s an A24 Film Group on Facebook with almost 100k members, created by the fans for the fans. This plays into today’s diatribe, to a degree.  
To wrap this up, I do think it’s ironic that web 3.0 is just a smarter version of 1.0, with the social media consolidators being stripped. But if it keeps sliding the way it’s been going, it’ll devolve into the thing it sought to replace. Last time I explicitly wrote about crypto in late Jan ‘19 ([issue 70](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-70-1-28-19-1591e692d789)), it was at its bottom after the 2017 hypefest. The inverse might be true now. It’s not for the faint of heart. None of this is investment advice, at all. The hands are supremely weak, or diamond encrusted.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Buy the rumors, sell the news. See you next week.
