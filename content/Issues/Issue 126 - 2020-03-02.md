# Issue 126

Hello\! Big news with a16z investing in the kids gaming platform Roblox, which is fantastic. We all probably saw this one from a mile away. It’s a healthy indicator for the space: the metaverse, or whatever. The thing is, this concept has been in action for over 20 years, and I like how Marc and David state that in [their writeup of the investment](https://a16z.com/2020/02/26/roblox/). I devoured every single piece of The Matrix IP when I was a young buck (movie, comic, game, anime, etc). I grew up online, playing video games: Runescape, Second Life, WoW, and reading books like Snow Crash and Neuromancer. Customizable avatars, virtual goods, nodal storylines \- these notions are *not* novel. The thing that is, is the fact that we can take them everywhere in our pocket, and jump back in and build upon the game whenever via the cloud. That is what excites me most. Applying the latest technological advancements to new opportunities in emerging forms of media. That’s what I’m all about, plain and simple.  
I’m going to keep the next couple preambles rather light as I’m heads down panhandling. It begins. I might be prohibited from openly sharing what I’m doing, but if you’re inclined to ask, you can just reply directly to this email. I’ve told a handful of you, and I’m sure all of you can make an educated guess. Can’t wait to roadshow (:  
To that end, Google Maps pushed a notification to me that pretty much nails my thesis to a tee. A digitized world that’s 3D and interactive, updating in real-time. I shared it below. Yep, I’m back in NYC. I actually first saw this notification when I was in LA, but my thumb unlocked my phone when I tried to screen-cap. Second time’s a charm. [Eric Zener](https://www.ericzener.com/) is the artist behind my wallpaper. It’s emblematic of how I usually feel, but in a good way. The ocean is where I’m most comfortable. I own a couple tiny pieces of art, mostly done by a friend [David Molesky](https://davidmolesky.com/). Funnily enough, one of those pieces actually inspired the name of my new company. My favorite markup last year was when one of the most *tasteful* a16z general partners bought some of his work…  
Paper gains.
