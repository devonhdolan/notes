# Issue 218

Hello\! The holiday break is here, things are slowing and spirits are lifting. Holiday traditions make me feel young again. They’re nostalgic jolts of youth, from hanging lights, buying a tree, to giving gifts — all memories to be joyously relived when you’ve got kids (not that I do). They’re habitual events, mainlined in a sort of franchise cycle. Ceremony. I’m always curious to see which artist/film tries to capture the moment and install themselves in it. Mariah wins, and there’s Sandler of course. Seasons greetings.  
As it’s that time of year, I’ve got two silly little gifts that I dug up from an old broken computer. When I was much more naive about a decade ago, I took it upon myself to do some independent research on film crowdfunding, which is seemingly en vogue again thanks to tokenomics. It’s a framework I’ll always obsess over. [Here’s the three-page report](https://www.dropbox.com/s/oy1y0c92mtq39qf/Film%20Crowdfunding.pdf?dl=0) I compiled for nobody in particular. I recalled it earlier this year in [issue 174](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-174) as it helped me get my dream job then… so a purpose eventually revealed itself.  
I had recently worked on Paul Schrader’s The Canyons, the first film by known talent to embrace crowdfunding that you definitely don’t need to watch (here’s the [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1094772583/the-canyons)). I was also investigating the film finance marketplace Slated. The main reason for grabbing some market feedback was because I was considering raising some cash to complete post-prod on my thesis film and wanted to do it right…  
Now if you ask nicely I *might* share that short with you, we somehow got to shoot on a military base and at an airport. It was quirky and spiritually earnest like one of those poppy Sundance indies that people don’t make anymore, even though they perform. The last I’d argue was Captain Fantastic. They were the hallmark of [Big Beach](https://bigbeach.com/), which closed its doors this year, marking the end of an era. There was strangely no IndieWire obit. Now we mainly get small, depressing educational films at festivals. A sad stuffy state, self-fulfilled.  
The other gift, [my truly bizarre cover letter email](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1441443054519390212/photo/1) that got me a coffee with Schrader \- it reads sappily as if it were one of my pubescent newsletter entries. Any who, I also plan on compiling all these issues into a singular, search-able repository (i.e. a *very* long Google Doc). I’ll have that for you next issue.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Happy holidays. See ya next year.
