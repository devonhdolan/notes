# Issue 258: run interference

Hello! The Many-World Order. So my plan is to write the broader group Dec 14th with a brief essay outlining my working framework on our last technological revolution (intelligence) and the three domains it’ll significantly impact, with their interplay. A hint: wet labs, quantum systems. 

It’s my theory of everything lol

I intend to relaunch ‘the newsletter’ with a sandbox version of a private knowledge network to spotlight the filmmakers, financiers, and founders who work at the frontier (of science/sci-fi), along with ‘agentic’ opportunity matchmaking (ie shadchan). A museum and a gallery, to throw it back to last week’s note. 

I am but a humble switchboard operator. And seek to light fire to the walled gardens of capital that encircle creative industry (by running my own). Behind the velvet rope, we are all ego wrappers.

So much to do, so little time.

I was hoping to ‘build in public’ within the safe confines of this group but have gotten carried away with client work (the dangling carrot is such a distraction) and my strange ‘writing’ on the days I should be wireframing. 

You might get a sneak peak on the 7th, that is my first draft deadline.

Keep the champagne and sparklers on ice. 

The birth of a nation. The fiefdom, a context graph (of proprietary deals/data) that users can point their preferred agent to, to let the convos commence. A trusted, referral only network that self perpetuates its own info-aware systems. WOM.

Fertile grounds for new world(s). Crawl, walk, run. Start with what you know.

Early opps will be digital creative economy, experiential and film/TV projects.

Reminds me of the GITS line, “Over-specialize, and you breed in weakness. It’s slow death.” (Which is a riff on the “specialization for insects” quote by Heinlein) but any network needs a focus and a purpose. 

Happy Thanksgiving.

That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. No good deed. See ya next week.
