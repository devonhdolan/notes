# Issue 9

By popular demand, I’m going to open each newsletter with a quick note on what I’m thinking about in relation to the industry. Let’s begin: Netflix’s obsession with branding their original commissioned, and licensed, titles really grinds my gears. Seriously, why do they feel the need to watermark the entirety of a trailer? It’s distracting and obnoxious. Sticker vandalism. It does the reverse effect that the marketer desires. It’s off-putting and utterly lame. Stop it. Case in point, [JIM & ANDY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kB15UFO5ebA) and also [THE DISCOVERY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9j6WcdU-ts). The latter is a real offender, where’s the love for the talent and filmmakers? So self-centered — I hope they’re not trying to set a precedent. Anyways, to end on a positive Netflix note, their Twitter game is strong. [Very](https://twitter.com/netflix/status/926570900866023424) strong.
