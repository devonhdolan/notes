# Issue 223

Hello\! I’m going to be a bit truant this week, sorry in advance. I just reunited with my wife who got back stateside yesterday from NZ — a huge piece of me was missing the last 5 months. I feel whole again. My testiness might finally subside, though probably not completely :)  
[Le Droit Moral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_rights). There’s a thought I haven’t been able to shake since about 2017, which was when I first learned about ‘the blockchain.’ Someone will create a Dapp (decentralized app) that’s a DRM (data rights management) database for copyrights. I said as much [three years ago in issue 70](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-70-1-28-19-1591e692d789). Call it chain of title.  
I think this public ledger could then point to the name, image, likeness (NIL) uses for artists and athletes, and ideally all the individual pieces of media that compose a film. An added layer would show which company has the rights to a certain film and tv show, and where to discover them. The holy grail would give the ability to transact directly on-chain to watch the film, though the tech isn’t there yet (at all). Oh, and the tech should be invisible, unspoken — unobtrusive. Trojan horse the guilds.  
Films would not be lost to the ash heap of history, which is why Scorsese made [The Film Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Film_Foundation). All this is *much* easier said than done, but it’s needed. And it’ll continue to haunt me until it’s done.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Always give credit. See you next week.
