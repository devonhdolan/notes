# Issue 134

Hello\! Well we’re closing this chapter on Michigan and will be making the 36 hour drive back home to Southern California this week. Going to get weird slugging Red Bull and jamming to underground deep-house. We’ll likely be bidding adieu to NYC as well. Disheartening. The pandemic chew us up and spit us out. More on this later.  
A few of you emailed me following last week’s preamble saying something along the lines of, “I trust Netflix’s top 10 recommendations as much as I trust China’s coronavirus numbers.” I tend to agree. Both are platforms. Believe the rumors, check the facts. The data might very well be obfuscated.  
In issue 129 (Mar 23rd), I mentioned that a major entertainment company is *allegedly* freezing development capital on new projects, though they’ll continue to service its existing slate. That company, Disney. It’s been getting hammered. They just claimed force majeure when terminating overall deals with a pair of showrunners. I suspect they’ll likely let all their non-core first-looks and overalls lapse. Yikes.  
It’s been eerie emailing people and getting bounce-backs. I’ve started to lose count. For my film/tv friends, I’ve been thinking of ways to build a sort of creative coalition to band together to progress projects in these times. There are power in numbers. It’d look like a syndicate, a union, or it could be purely commission-based. Don’t wait around for jobs to come, create your own. The market’s flooded with talent. Write me.  
Some random asides. I love that this newsletter discretely reminds people to email me back. If we’re heading into a recession, movie-going will be negatively impacted. Duh. I don’t know why Nolan and Warner Bros are rushing to get Tenet out the door. I’m not going to a theater for a very long time. If I remember correctly, sin stocks (booze, cigs) tend to perform in downturns. I’ve come to realize that work is never-ending, and that a major source of motivation for me is spite. Super healthy, I know.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
