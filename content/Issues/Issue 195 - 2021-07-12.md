# Issue 195

​​Hello\! Be warned, a couple strange half-formed musings in today’s diatribe.  
Interactive storytelling is valuable because the audience will reveal their ‘psychology’ to you, each player choice has an underlying meaning. There’s plenty of opportunity to be found there, especially in story responsiveness. In movement. Data harvest. I’ve thought a lot about this space. To that end, great design is invisible, seamless. The creator as architect, influencing the audience’s experience through controlled presentation. Dynamics.  
The decay of originality. The appeal to the masses is the search for sameness. Attention anchor \- grab and hold \- sink fast to the bottom. The top of the charts is the top of the bell curve. Averaged temptation. This comes in all forms, from movies to songs to software. It’s formulaic, a paint by numbers approach. Fringe-seek; richesse.  
Follow the trends, the topics du jour, and you might live the rewards \- with no prolonged impact. Good enough is not good enough. Replay. They’ll say “if it ain’t broke don’t fix it,” but in charting new territory, you compete in an environment of your own making. These are the hardest, most worthy battles to fight. It’s where legacy is born. Weapon wielded.  
Guilty by association. If algorithms reinforce your desires, the inputs will remain the same, then so will the outputs. As will *our* behaviors. Tangential feeding. Memetics. Multi-sided confirmation. A grey blob of discharge, that your circle approves. Neon gradients in the design community. Cryptopunk conformity. Real-life experience triumphs codified interests, identities. Herd immunity; cattle. Groupthink.  
No man is an island.  
Never a better time to be an explainer on the Internet \- sweet greetings captain obvious. The standalone re-cap, opinion commentary not needed. Unfounded. A cursory review. New brands and personalities are popping up all over social media regurgitating facts from Wikipedia or recent headlines. Whack a mole. The espousal of topical interests that their followers crave, all while leveraging other’s status (namesakes) for their own.  
A grotesque self-promotional game. The hunt for clicks: thread opens, video views, podcast listens, email reads. The new listicle. Work as content, is everywhere. *Being* online. Instagram as research, as surveillance. We monitor each other, our time off and on. All the time. The wane of self-autonomy. The parrots squawk into their shallow grave. Uhm okay, enough of this. I’ve lost the plot.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Nothing is ever easy. See you next week.
