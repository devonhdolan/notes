# Issue 238

Hello\! Hollywood was a 20th century business. It had a good run. The industry is a hallucination, where everyone shares the same fever dream; a delusion. A game of taste, reputation, and relationship. It’s the land of the lucky, where the town flips Maslow’s hierarchy on its head, and its players self-actualize through the other’s creativity. It abstracts, extracts, away from its core. It is *industry*.  
What comes today is forgotten tomorrow. It’s now an assembly line for filmmakers to make commercials for subscription streaming services. Upside: None. This marks the downfall of our collective, creative empire. Goodbye legacy… we hardly knew ye.  
Adapt, or Die. Inflection Point. Paradigm Shift.  
“The old world is dying, and the new world struggles to be born: now is the time of monsters.” ― Antonio Gramsci  
Frankenstein Factory.  
The doors are locked to the lab. The key was melted in its wake. The outsider must *break in*.  
The gates are few, ironclad. They’ve been standing for just one century, getting pelted with acid rain over the generations. They are rusted, begging to be kicked down. Nothing is more empowering. This is an invitation; yes: innovation. Kiss the beast.  
Welcome, next stop unknown… to be written.  
The tried-and-true paths are growing weeds. Ivy to mailroom. A short at Sundance. IFP and book memos. Reps blast samples. Patrons fleeced. The calvary is not coming to save you.  
Scientific Discovery.  
Modal Media. Many friends who work for (*the most celebrated*) filmmakers, say their boss wouldn’t pursue this path now. More immersive, interactive mediums interest them. Global audiences are at the push of a button. And the barrier to entry is ankle-high \-- the ease of access to technology \-- builds bridges everywhere. An abundance of story is still to be made. We are ready.  
There will be new launchpads for emerging talent and original material. [Hit me up](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1561500748378427392).  
Forge the next castle. Rally around an idea. Remain positive. Join the ride.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Ever the Opportunist. See ya next week.
