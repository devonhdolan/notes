# Issue 221

Hello\! Keeping this one light. I’m flirting with some great people about about doing a written series on the creative process, so most of my ‘inspired thoughts’ are being routed that way. They’ll bleed into these emails in due time, promise.  
A few things I’ll openly share. Almost all the projects I’m producing can be expanded into multi-platform storyworlds. In line with that, I’m helping identify the “Story [MVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_viable_product)” for each to prove out the IP, more or less reverse engineering marketing stunts — I said something similar a year ago in [issue 173](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-173). Working on productizing it now.  
Not to worry, I’m still working on a few straight up films and tv shows — just the thought of doing small(er) budget one-offs doesn’t make much sense. I firmly believe the story must necessitate the medium and format, and vice versa. This is all a peak into how I’m thinking for my newco.  
I also [made a basic website for myself](https://www.devonhdolan.com/). This removes a bit of extraneous work for myself (and others) for introductory chats. If you’ve been following along the last few years, you probably know more about me than I do. Sorry if you’ve heard me make that joke before. Sure we can all agree that we continually repeat the same lines on calls just to hear *that* courtesy chuckle. It’s like being a stand up comic. We act, delivering the same joke to a new crowd for a hopeful laugh. At least I do… lame.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Everything’s based on trust. See you next week.
