# Issue 144

Hello\! It’s been hectic moving out of NYC, he writes shedding a tear. One thing that’s been squarely top of mind as of late \- testing & proving IP. [In early April](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-131), I got some heat for “blasting” the antiquated methods of chasing material, or as they say, rights check. Avail. Books, comics, podcasts, articles, and, hey now look at that, games.  
Story, before all else, is the most important.  
Now, *especially* *now*, is the time to experiment. Content testbeds. The film [Zola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zola_\(film\)) is based on a Twitter thread. [Tales from the Loop](https://variety.com/2018/tv/news/amazon-tales-from-the-loop-1202875431/), a series of Swedish paintings. The Martian began as a bunch of self-published blogs and the scientific accuracy was crowdsourced from fans (same with Fifty Shades, which started as Twilight smut… I mean fan fiction). Hell, maybe podcasts should originally start as newsletters, or go earlier as Discord / Twitter / Reddit threads, or even daily texts ([Community](https://www.wired.com/story/ashton-kutcher-tweeted-phone-number/), [Superphone](https://techcrunch.com/2018/01/29/crm-for-sms/))…  
A few things I’ve been contemplating here. You want to: own the consumer endpoint(s), communicate directly w/ your audience (no intermediary) to eventually drive to other outlets (e-comm / svod), monetize to ideally produce steady & consistent income, plot it out as a series where the pilot episode(s) is free. There’s probably a business hidden somewhere in here. Building a loyal audience is the hardest, for sure. I’ve been stewing on the question: what are the verifiable metrics needed to get a film or tv series in the works? I doubt there really are any. One thing’s for certain, *this* time’ll surely spark a wave of animation and audio. Bite sized? Whatever. Moving on.  
Couple of other quick things that are also floating around the old noodle. When I spark to a story, I usually end up creating a playlist for it. I find inspiration in music (+ album covers), architecture, scenes from film/tv, and culture. A mood. I’m lucky to be able to draw upon my personal experiences abroad. There are memories that I try to re-live through story. I want this moment, this scene, to *feel* like that bike ride in Kyoto. A faded resemblance to a distant time and place. Recall. It’s powerful.  
Last remarks. The banner ad atop the Fire or Apple TV has to be one of the most valuable pieces of internet real estate. I bring this up because my grandma called me saying she was having trouble watching Hamilton. I’m serious. I had to lecture her through downloading Disney and subscribing to the service. Ultimately, she thought it was too much of a hassle. She would’ve preferred to just rent it then and there. Oh well. Finally, a question I was asked that I detest with every bone in my body \- will AI be able to write a commercial and critical movie? Answer: No. Piss off :)  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
