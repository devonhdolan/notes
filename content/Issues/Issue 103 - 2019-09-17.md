# Issue 103

Hello\! A day late and a dollar short in hitting send here. I’m in Yellowstone. It’s stunning. Wifi and cell service at our national parks is pretty much non-existent, which is good all things considered. Two quick observations. There are cases of small business owners tipping gamers and other livestreamers to buy “ad time” in the form of shoutouts, and it’s working. Keeping my eye on this space. And, I wrote about this when I was in South Africa, but two-factor authentication can be a serious nuisance nowadays. If you lapse ownership of your phone number, you’re pretty much screwed. Telcos can do better. The phone number is your digital identity, especially if it’s linked to all your social and financial accounts. I just witnessed this last week and it got me thinking about authenticated factorials. I need to spend more time dwelling on this space. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
