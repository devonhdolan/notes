# Issue 26

Hello\! A friend asked me last week what I would do if I were to start a ‘temee’ company in the era of Disney and Netflix domination. This really stuck with me. My gut reaction was to go with my creative instincts, so I declared that I’d produce several story universes, but I backtracked on that quickly because I know I’d fall reliant on distribution channels for film/TV adaptation (which is what gets me out of bed every morning). I then thought about less capital intensive ways of disseminating stories, which led me to online self-publishing. Andy Weir wrote and offered The Martian on his website for free, and anyone can make a podcast for next to nothing. Simple, right? The thing is, this requires your potential audience to have access to the internet, something the modern world takes for granted. There are roughly 4 billion people without internet today. That’s half the world’s population. A massive opportunity. So I started breaking down the corporate structure of major telcos knowing that many own our beloved entertainment companies. Plus, I just love rambling about communication theory, networks, and the content creation/distribution value chain. Pipes and platforms. There are a few startups and big-tech moonshots whose sole purpose it is to provide “decentralized” internet access to emerging markets. This is something I fully support. To name a few, we have [Astranis](https://www.astranis.com/), [Starry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starry_Internet) (the founder, Chet, is an absolute beast), [goTenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GoTenna), [Facebook Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Aquila), [Project Loon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Loon) (Google), and [LinkNYC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkNYC), I’m still toying with creative ways of how to organically ingest and integrate content into these upstarts, but I think now is one of the only chances in history we’ll get to fully take on the dinosaurs (AT\&T, Verizon, Comcast), and come up with new content delivery scenarios. That’s my loose and flexible answer on how I’d start a ‘temee’ in today’s age. A sort of “pipe” dream. Heh. I’ll see myself out. Catch you next week.
