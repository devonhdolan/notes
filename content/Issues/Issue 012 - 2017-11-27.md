# Issue 12

Morning\! Hope you all had a lovely holiday. I got back from Europe (Switzerland and Italy) last night — it was absolutely wonderful. Breathtaking. I spent two days in Florence and a good chunk of time at [The Uffizi Gallery](http://www.uffizi.org/) thinking about The House of Medici. From what I was able to gather from a few YouTube videos and Wiki articles, they would be categorized as “smart money.” They had a clear vision, a sense of purpose, that they implemented to their surroundings by giving art meaning. They used money as a tool. (For those who don’t control money, you have voice and time in your arsenal). The Medici Family allied themselves with the best artists of their time, and shaped their reasoning and sensibility. The message behind the art they financed signaled certain virtues to society. I think values are somewhat subjective because they come from someone else’s perspective, but that’s moot. Idea generators and executors (e.g. writers, entrepreneurs, painters, architects, etc) should take a lesson from the Medici playbook and realize a motive behind their idea/vision, and the impact it has on society should be crystal clear and justified. Always ask “Why?”
