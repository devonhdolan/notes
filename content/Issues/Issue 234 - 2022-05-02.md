# Issue 234

Hello\! A few fleeting fortune cookie thoughts about the industry that touch on taste and validation.  
Arbiters, as beacons of quality…  
Relationship banked. In defense of intermediary, our world is a bundle of curated markets. Promotion is power, turned storefront. Winner take all markets cause concern, the app store is a monopoly. Our natural order is to go full stack, financed. Capital allocated.  
A rep for your rep. Too many cooks in the kitchen never did anyone good, outsource by the hour not by the percentage. A retainer. Successful self-publishers are unicorns.  
Brand as reputation. Many curators and creators put their namesake and reputation on the line when they go out with a pitch and/or run a process. This is where you earn the trust of others, it weeds out the herd. Survival of the fittest. Warm intros win, there’s derived value.  
Destination unknown. Most audiences don’t care who made the show or which network it’s on when it comes to the middle (if it’s USA, FX, Showtime, or AMC), but there is a known difference between HBO and the CW. The Netflix logo might be a turn-off, but its still respected for its convenience.  
Company town. Most are just trying to keep their jobs and hopefully make a project or two along the way. You’re remembered for good deeds, not misfortunes.  
License to profit. Known IP is a privilege, preserve the image of its creator… exclusively or not, in all media forms.  
The duality of sophistication. Attempting to orchestrate overly complex deals either signal you’re too smart to be in the business, or show you’re out of your depth.  
It takes a village (crier).  Those who open doors for others are often the first tossed out of the room, deservedly or not. Never pull the latter up behind you.  
It’s all association games of risk and reward, underpin the motivation.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Lookin out for numero uno. See ya next week.
