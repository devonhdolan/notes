# Issue 66

Hello\! South Africa is magical. I love it here. Alright, social media. Consistency is key when it comes to posts. Too much is overkill. Too little — are you even alive? A feed acts as a television network and it’s programmed by individuals, content hubs. The way people interact with social media is akin to navigating an electronic program guide (EPG). To that end, [here](https://www.instagram.com/hookiemonsters/) is the shared Instagram account with Anmari. It’s called Hookie Monsters. Kinda cute. Kinda catchy. We like to play. It’s like we’re on a work holiday. It was necessary to stay away from names like wanderlust nomadic vagabonds. Ew. We’re still sorting out our workflow and voice. It’s tougher than it seems, especially when one of us isn’t on a cellular network (me). I used to knock on digital influencers, even though I worked with many early in my career, but it is a lot “work.” I think I did so out of envy — the world is fueled by it, not greed. I also made some cheeky videos — [here](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC7b1hwMPVfjDEepcEej1cJw) is my YouTube account. I like that it’s called a channel and not a profile. My favorite thing about the internet is that creators can go DTC. Gaining a following is much easier said than done. Our IG will have more in-the-moment content, namely Stories and Photos, a steady drip of fun. I’ll be making more videos imminently. I got excited and made one too many off the bat. A website is likely forthcoming. That’ll contain brief bullets of what we did each day. More diary than scrapbook. A couple of sidebar gripes. I said this before when I was abroad — it really upsets me that there isn’t a cheap and easy universal telco service. I got locked out of a couple of my accounts (financial and social) because I had two-factor authentication (2FA) setup and couldn’t receive SMS texts. I had to upgrade my plan for a brief period of time. Annoying. (It’s a blessing to not get daily robocalls.) A company called Authy is trying to address the 2FA problem, but I couldn’t receive their initial verification and registration code. So silly. My options were to buy a SIM card or a better data plan. The former wouldn’t work because my phone can’t be unlocked with my carrier. So I opted for a data plan for a few days. On a final note, it is nice having a totally bricked out phone whenever I’m off wifi. Like, really nice. It turns it into a mobile camera and editing station. That’s all for now. See you next week\!
