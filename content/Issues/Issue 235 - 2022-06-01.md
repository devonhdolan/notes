Hello\! I am *incredibly* backlogged, please forgive me. I’ll do my best to claw my way out of this mountain of news and get us back up to date in a week or so.  
I don’t want to share anything prematurely… but I’ve been in a war room the last two weeks in SF working around the clock (literally sleeping at the office) with a web3 R\&D product lab.  
I got the go-ahead 24 hours before I was set to move back to New Zealand with my wife, who I dropped off at LAX exactly two weeks ago (we should reunite there next week). I chose the former, the clock was on me.  
Funnily enough, it was the serendipitous result of me arranging a cool [2.5 hour Twitter Space](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1522063853864951808) in early May between The Fringe filmmakers and the Lootverse leaders about collaborative world-building. I highlighted Loot in [issue 231](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-231). I may or may not have had a few glasses of wine on the call.  
Oh, and there was also the Coastal Fire in Orange County that was just up the hill from my house. Just another day in good ol’ California.  
The long weekend was very much needed to surface for air. I haven’t opened any of my daily news emails for a few weeks so I’ve got some sifting to do in front of me. Yes, all the headlines are manually hand-curated in the link digest section.  
Thanks for bearing with me, hope you all understand. And to those of you who texted asking if I was alright (bc of me not sending the weekly note.. and the fire), ‘preciate ya.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Hot potato, pass. See you next week.
