# Issue 201

Hello\! So we’re in an iron-wall lockdown for another week in New Zealand. It’s a drag. We can’t swim in the ocean or a drive a few miles from the house. Can’t complain if they manage to nip Delta in the bud within a month. With my luck, the country will re-open the day I leave. Yeah I booked a ticket home. See you lovies in LA in late September. Will be back for awhile \- write me if you want to kick it. Flying into the fire, vaxxed waxed and ready.  
Á la lune. I have a strong feeling we’re going to see the tokenization (securitization) of programs that look a lot like the Sundance Institute (labs, grants, fellowships). Access is what it’s all about, plus brand name recognition for that added lift to help open doors for the unproven (and their IP). I’ve gotten lost down the crypto rabbit hole multiple times, but I don’t think I’m going to crawl my way out this time around. Dove deep the last few days…  
There are just too many well-heeled and sophisticated people in the space to ignore it (a lot of rich idiots too, but c’est la vie). It’s beyond found money. It’s a new revenue line at the least, and a totally new system at most. We’re sorting out ways to imbue crypto into a few projects I’m producing \- token as ticket as share ([DDtwtr](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1384683054539493378)). It can sit side-by-side with legacy distribution. These tokens should be functional, digital memorabilia is the low hanging fruit. I’m thinking of penning a full bottom-up exploration. It’d probably be pretty lucrative to build a consultancy in this space right now targeted at Hollywood and major brands. Might ride through town like a crazed Paul Revere.  
Selective hindsight bias. A few of my musings from early May ([issue 185](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-185)) are being re-*hashed* now by crypto-focused financiers around co-ops. All my projects are group projects. It’s wild to me that hundreds, sometimes thousands, of people are working together to buy (pump) pictures of rocks, apes, penguins, squiggles, cats, and 8-bit punks. There are power in numbers. It might look silly, but it’s certainly worth studying and participating. Risk what you can afford to lose, discretionary fund. Pay the pied piper. It’s speculative, but here to stay.  
Play stupid games, win stupid prizes. Everything’s a scam. Flip the float.  
The thin line. Being early is being wrong. It’s looking like generative art is having its big moment. I might’ve mentioned that *generative* would be the buzzword of 2021 ([issue 168](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-168)). [Here’s a good primer](https://medium.com/collab-currency/punks-squiggles-and-the-future-of-generative-media-d2d3e9df623b) on the space as it relates to NFTs. Some of the prices for these jpegs are absurd. I’ve been tangentially involved in the digital art market since 2017 ([issue 76](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-76-3-11-19-66e850534fc)), and beyond the art itself, I found the gallery business attractive with its greedy 50/50 splits. There’s arbitrage to be found in incentive *mis-*alignment. Upstarts will emerge and it’ll be a race, fastest to the bottom.  
Seek dislocation. A treasury.  
As it relates to film, day-and-date distribution is now a core strategy of every studio and streamer. Some still consider it innovative. You don’t want to know how long I’ve been harping the PVOD tune, publicly since I started this newsletter in 2017 ([issue 18](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-18-1-15-18-7c39db565bca)). It’s still getting fleshed out by the industry, but it’s here to stay too (I hope). Obviously the pandemic plays a part in its traction, but if you willfully ignored the idea, then who’s the greater fool now. Persevere, monsieur Revere.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Go hard, go early \- Jacinda. See you next week.
