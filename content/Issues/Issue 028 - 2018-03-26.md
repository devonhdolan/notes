# Issue 28

Hello\! Ever since Drake played Fortnight with Ninja, I’ve been thinking a lot more about esports. I’m not an avid gamer, but I could take your money in a game of OG Super Mario Kart. I dabble with FPSs from time-to-time and appreciate the mission-oriented stories in free-roam RPGs. (I use acronyms because I’m so in the know). Anyways, I was applying traditional entertainment business models to the world of esports and thought it would be interesting if there was a new sort of organization that functioned like a music labels that would advance money to pro-gamers on the rise to “sign” them. Creative assets. This would act as a contract where the ‘esports label’ would recoup the advance against future earnings that the player would generate over a certain amount of time (a big if). The gamer would plug into the network of the ‘esports label’ to maximize their audience, and gain access to leagues/teams, sponsorships, tournaments, and venues (yes, people watch gamers IRL). The main responsibility of a free agent/indie gamer is to live-stream themselves and the game they’re playing at the same time. That’s the product, and the distribution infrastructure can easily be handled by Twitch and/or YouTube. (Side bar: The origin story of Twitch is wild and I’m a big fan of Justin Kan et al.). The viability of this type of financial instrument could be minimal, but the added benefits could become valuable. Two sides to every coin. It’s worth noting that most pro-gamers are salaried by a team, just like pro athletes, as there’s more power in the collective. There are rumors that YouTube, Spotify, and Apple are going to start their own music labels, who’s to say YT won’t employ this sort of deal to lock up gamers before they make it big. Alright, this was a fun thought exercise. See you next Monday. Disclosures: I don’t know the ins and outs of the esports industry, but know that it emulates the sports business. I watched Machinima when I was a young buck, so I’m credentialed. I’m also a shareholder in both EA and Activision (and analysts say that they’ve dropped recently thanks to Fortnight). Exciting times. Have a good week.
