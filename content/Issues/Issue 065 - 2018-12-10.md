# Issue 65

Hello from South Africa\! It is beautiful here, quite similar to CA actually. I’ve been in transit the last \~36 hours and to say my brain’s fried would be a bit of an understatement. To keep it real with you all, this newsletter might slightly thin out over the coming months compared to the usual hodgepodge of headlines, and there’s a good chance it might not land in your inbox on Monday due to wifi access. Please forgive me. I’ve also lightened up my current news outlet and newsletter subscription count (you know, to be cost efficient), so the aggregators from which I curate from shrunk a tad. Need not worry, these issues will still be quality, consistent, and comprehensive. In other fun news, Anmari and I came to a decision on the name of our shared social media handle and blog to diarize our adventures. I’ll share next week once ready. If we’re friends on FB or IG, you’ll be first in the know — look out for a cheeky little video today or tomorrow. I also finally got around to watching the 2018 film “Searching” on the plane. It is good. Really good. Perfect for a plane, maybe not so much for a theater. I don’t mean that to sound like a backhanded compliment. One of my top five, maybe three, this year. I expect massive things from the filmmaker, Aneesh Chaganty. He was in his mid-20s when he made it. I can’t believe I got “Up’d” in the opening montage. You’ll probably enjoy the film if you like this newsletter. That’s all for now. Thanks for tuning in. See you next week\!
