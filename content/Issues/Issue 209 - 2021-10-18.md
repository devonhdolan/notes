# Issue 209

Hello\! People are basic. We’re simple. Sophistication is pageantry. First impressions lead to snap judgements. We overshare online. The internet tells all.  
I don’t know why, but for the last decade I’d put everyone else on a pedestal before meeting them. I guess I felt I had to prove myself to them. Me, beneath you.  
Each day, each meeting, was mock trial. I’d over-research and over-think every word that came out of my mouth. It was a self-prescribed program, like training a dog to not jump. It was weird.  
It was LA.  
An industry town where you’re groomed to be subservient, with no right to an opinion until you have some bonafides. Whatever that means. I made the rounds the last few days with a calendar that looked like a perfect Tetris game. I met with all types, mainly caught up with old friends. It was a joyous time.  
Though I was saddened to hear the horror stories. I was reminded that too many people in LA play games and don’t act in good faith. Everything’s a transaction, and the favor is expected to be returned. Many don’t follow through. There’s no reach.  
Reciprocity is a hell of a drug, especially if you’re the church mouse. A charity case. If you find the honest, transparent, good people, latch onto them eternal. The new class is slowly rising to the surface. Be radically kind, it’s a direct line to the soul. Preach.  
Play by the new rules. Deliver on your word. Deceit, defenestrate. I’ve seen some seriously sizable transactions done on a handshake then a quick two-page doc. Your word, coded as law. Adhere to the social contract. Get the job done. Giddy up cowboy.  
Next. Some social media asides. One, I visited the James Bond [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/007/featured) in hopes of watching the latest gun barrel sequence to get some design inspo \- 007 has its own accounts across all the socials (talk about character owning) \- but they surprisingly don’t have a playlist dedicated to the opening credits. Feels like such a layup, maybe there’s a snag with the music rights. [Billie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BboMpayJomw) posted the No Time To Die song on her page a year ago. I don’t know. Two, somebody really needs to write a Harvard case on Red Bull from a media perspective \- they’re unrivaled. Three, Spotify could be such a fun social platform. . .  
To that end, I want to try something out here. [I made a breezy Spotify playlist that’s collaborative](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/78Of7GyF1TIKX3FbfIhX5P) \- so anyone here can *add, remove,* or *re-order* tracks. Do with it what you will. Nothing makes me happier than discovering new musicians, artists, writers etc  
I think the playlist is nice for evening drives on PCH. It’s mellow. I [made a duplicate](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2FiAERZ1cvxlw1jz8iIflQ) in case it gets out of hand. But I trust you, that’s all life boils down to at the end of the day… and having some semblance of taste/style.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Do it for the culture. See you next week.
