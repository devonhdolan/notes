# Issue 169

​​Hello. Well, seems that now’s another apt time to share [Peter Finch’s “Mad as Hell” monologue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRuS3dxKK9U) from the Sidney Lumet film Network. The world is on fire, everyone is yelling, and I’ve found myself fuming in the sanctuary that is New Zealand, again.  
I aggressively type…  
I try to not comment on the news, nor press releases, and do my damnedest to keep this thing apolitical (knowing we all need a break from it), but when the confederate flag flies in the Capitol I can’t help but say something. My blood boils. And writing about entertainment or whatever else right now just seems outright petty.  
So, I’ll give it to you straight up. I did not like *anything* that happened last week.  
I think the Charlottesville car attack that happened late summer 2017 ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottesville_car_attack)) can be traced back as the cataclysm, the impetus, to the events that unfurled in the years since. And sadly I don’t think this American story is over just yet. As a refresher, this is when a *neo-Nazi* drove into a crowd peacefully protesting **against** **racism**, killing one (Heather Heyer) and injuring dozens of others. This is what we read on *Twitter*:  
“…fine people on both sides.”  
I watched the video of that attack. I saw that response. I wept. The last time I cried helplessly at the news was when I watched the Twin Towers fall \- nearly 3,000 people were murdered by terrorists on 9/11. About the same number of people die from covid each day in America, in large part due to our government’s complete ineptitude.  
What we witnessed last week was a piss-poor attempt to install autocratic powers in the Executive Branch. It was no coup, that would give those morons too much credit as it requires a semblance of an agenda. They were chaos agents. Their acts somehow undermined our intelligence communities and led to two people being shot and killed.  
Faith, all time lows. Market, all time highs. Nothing makes sense. I’m losing it.  
Heather Heyer’s last post on *Facebook*, “If you’re not outraged, you’re not paying attention.”  
Then, technology companies decided to wield their absolute powers. Platforms (now publishers), networks, and infrastructure-level companies silenced the outgoing President of the United States and its affiliated groups. They can do whatever they want, it’s a free market economy, but dystopic science fiction is our new reality. In big tech we trust? Yeah, let’s leave it to Zuck and biased algos to do the right thing. Goes to show how important it is to own your end-audience (numbers, addresses, emails).  
Big tech is not the rescuer. We are. It starts with the power of the people. We need to communicate. We need to build bridges. We need to be optimistic. We need to vaccinate high-risk groups. We need ethical operation/operating systems. Open protocols. Civil liberties. Moral rights. Balance. We need to help each other. We need humanity.  
We can do better. I believe in us. I believe in America. It’s all we’ve got.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. 10 days left. See you next week.
