# Issue 34

Hello\! Donald Glover’s music video [This is America](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYOjWnS4cMY) is taking the world by storm. It is culture. Watch it. Speaking of culture, I went to Frieze Art Fair on Saturday to see what the world’s “best” galleries had to offer. This felt different from year’s past as the exhibits seemed to focus more on the generation of earned media rather than the art itself. In-person network effects. There were lots of neon signs, big bright canvases, and over-sized objects. Bubblegum pop. I think we’re transitioning into an epoch of phone-in-hand spectacle and experience, which creates room for interplay with AR. The Instagram Age. Specifically, location-based entertainment (“LBE”) that’s crafted to encourage people to snap and share across social media (e.g. [Dream Machine](https://www.visitdreammachine.com/)). This will inevitably propagate some form of vanity. Pretty cynical outlook, I know. To segue, I’m still working on my MoviePass diatribe, stay tuned for it. See you next week\!
