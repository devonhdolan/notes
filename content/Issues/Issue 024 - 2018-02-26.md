# Issue 24

Hello. Our democracy is under siege. Media manipulation is one of the biggest problems in today’s world, and social networks are at fault. They’ve become agents of propaganda, weaponized by those opposed to certain political ideologies. There are nation-states that wish to divide democratic governments, and do so by harnessing the power of viral content, causing online civil war. This is a contagion that spreads and espouses an agenda that seeks to tear us apart at the seams. Constructive debates are dwindling; the middle ground is evaporating. If you don’t believe me, I urge you to go online to explore the extreme clusters of the political spectrum. It’s disturbing. Homologous. I did this after the Parkland conspiracy video trended to top of the charts on YouTube ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/youtube-facebook-trending-tools-parkland-conspiracy/)). But in crisis, there’s opportunity. The young Parkland activists are going toe-to-toe with the lobbyists. They’re standing up for their basic human rights, and using social networks to keep their mission in the news. They should not be sidelined, silenced, anytime soon. When change is afoot, it can be ignored, embraced, or challenged, and people can use their time, voice, and/or money to catalyze it. To push their principles to influence others. As easy as it is to focus on the negative fringes of social networks, we must realize that the majority of people use these platforms for good. They’re the most accessible means for creating, distributing, discovering, comprehending, and promoting ideals that align with your beliefs. I’m with the kids. See you next week.
