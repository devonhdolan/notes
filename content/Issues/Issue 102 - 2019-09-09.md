# Issue 102

Hello\! Today marks my last day in CA. It’s time to go back to the fight pit: NYC. It’s been a productive summer at home in Laguna, which allowed me to charge to LA for my bi-weekly strike missions. A special shoutout to all my friends that let me live on their couches and off their expense accounts. Thank you. Those that weren’t so gracious are in my burn book. I’ll be driving cross country the next 16 days with my girlfriend. We’re fired up to see some of the beautiful sights that America has to offer. We’re going to visit the Grand Canyon, Zion, Arches, Yellowstone, Badlands, and Detroit (?) along the way. Natural splendor. It’s funny, we both decided we had to move back to the city about a month into our world tour. It’s our jam. Why? We absolutely love it. It’s calling us back. We’re just not done with it. It’s the most diverse, dynamic, and ultimately convenient place to start something new. It’s the capital of our universe. It’s theater. It’s the greatest city in the world. It keeps you on your toes. The energy is electrifying. And it’s where we’ll call home the next few years. Lease, signed. Homelessness begone. Oh, and in case you weren’t aware, I’m starting my own thing. More on that later. It’s about to go down. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week (pending wifi).
