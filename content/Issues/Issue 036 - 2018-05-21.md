# Issue 36

Hello\! I’ve been thinking about the allocation (and hopeful expansion) of our leisure time, household entertainment budgets, and the basic reasons for cord-cutting. I haven’t come up with anything ground breaking, but think that bespoke utility plans are more attractive than bundled telco packages because they free up a greater T\&E allowance for the masses, which led to the rise of Netflix. A trend we’ve seen in recent years is the sharp increase in unbundled / OTT content services, and now we’re witness to the emergence of mini bundles, notably the Hulu and Spotify partnership. To riff on this, I think that pure-play content companies are rolling out their own minis because they fear that Amazon, Google, and Apple are doing everything in their ability to own the consumer’s attention (and wallet). This is the titanomachy — the 10-year battle between the older Titan and the younger Olympian gods. The power dynamic has shifted as these younger, richer, companies can be construed as utilities because they sell a diverse, addictive, suite of services and hardware. One stop shops of the future, in pole position to hold the consumer’s purse strings. I’m curious to see how 5G will play out on the battlefield, and think that Apple is going to make big moves in this arena seeing as how they’re going to manufacture chips and content in-house. See you next week.
