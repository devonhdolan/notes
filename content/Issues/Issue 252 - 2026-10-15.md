# Issue 252

Hello! First, thank you kindly for opting in last week. This is a secret group until we make our grand entrance again in a few weeks. If you ever want out, just say so. You won’t hurt my feelings. But know that it will come at equal cost to your subconscious as it will to mine.  
Because you’re my new accountability partner. Don’t you dare you break that bond.  
I just need to get back in shape. I’m fat. So here I am, in public working out. And I need to ask we reset the clock. I’m sorry I’m already behind schedule - I want to send these things on Mondays like I used to do, but life and work got in the way. This used to cause me much anxiety. It still does. I forgot the pressure I put on myself to try to come up with snappy (snarky) preambles. Know we’ll both forget about it as soon as I hit send and you quick-skim. C’est la.  
I have a backlog of topics I want to rip apart, but will save those for other issues - exercising restraining here. The thing that’s mostly on my mind is how I can automate myself. [Agent, myself](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-117) (a line from one of my most popular diary entries; when I was 23 I got turned down by the then head of HR at CAA for agenting myself too hard).  
I’m turning myself into a queryable platform, so when my clients (or friends) ask for help, I give them access to a *dashboard, soon my agent (or not). Self-serve.  
Intuition. The writing (which I care about) will always start and stop with me. I am very concerned about people outsourcing their inner voice (instincts lol) or stream of consciousness to the machine, though it can take research, basic modeling, memos, CRM buildout, and dot connecting off my plate.  
I’m exporting data from LinkedIn, X, emails etc so friends (and soon their agents) can e-beg from my interest & network graph  
Anywho, I want to start with something veryyy easy to get my feel for this again. Oh look, a 5 pound dumbbell.  
Last week we had some (passé) [Twitter discourse](https://x.com/adelwu_/status/2108001196506308758) about social media / product marketing calling itself storytelling. Again. This will not be the definitive essay on that). People ate it up because if they saw it, they saw themselves in it. The algo played to their interests, their ego, to a job that doesn’t really exist. And will soon be gone.  
The new taste is a simple synthesis, free association (deep cuts), from a subject or archetype to personal experience(s) and some cult barely scraped material.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Pick up loose threads. See ya next week.
