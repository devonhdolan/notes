# Issue 135

Hello\! We drove across America last week over the course of 3 days. I’m back in Southern California, and I don’t want to step foot in a car for quite some time.  
The governor shut down the beaches here in Orange County, though the lifeguards, sheriffs, and park rangers aren’t enforcing it. I went down late last night after dark. I swam in the bioluminescence, which is caused by an algae bloom (red tide). It was one of the coolest thing I’ve ever done. All movement caused a neon blue/green glow. I felt like Flash Gordon. My fists turned to orbs, and the waves, electric. It was rad.  
I’m still marinating on this creative collective idea. I think I might’ve cracked it on the road. There’s opportunity in crisis… as it’s said in GoT, chaos is a ladder. So many talented people are losing their jobs at agencies, studios, and the like, and there’s a play to offer a turnkey solution for them. Will share more in due time.  
There are some seriously juicy rumors swirling around town, especially with regard to WME / IMG. I’m tempted to share them, but don’t want Ari to send a hired gun to off me. If you’re curious, shoot me a text or email. Again, they’re unconfirmed. I’m no journo.  
I realize this preamble is rather lame. I did the entirety of this issue this morning because I wasn’t able to get to it on the drive. Still pretty fried to be honest.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
