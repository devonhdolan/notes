# Issue 125

Hello\! Content is king, the bell rings of yesteryear. Well, yes. I will never not think content \- story \- is most important (trust), but if you’re starting a digital-first media startup and/or DTC brand, then verified conversion is king. If you’re eager to build social capital from scratch, a solid option in the route to success is to leverage an existing company’s, or person’s, reputation and follower-ship to amass an audience. Draft off the influencer economy. It’s a symbiotic game for remora, those fish that leech off whales and sharks. The old guard wants to be involved with the shiny new thing to be hip, and the upstarts want the legacy’s credibility factor and relationships.  
A two-sided network, where either side spam tags each other online.  
I don’t mean to sound salty or spicy. Media is hard, very hard. And this newsletter is a fun learning experiment to glean insights around online media stats: traffic sources, conversion rates, views likes and re-shares. Last week I was testing out all the above by cross posting issue 123 on my socials in staggered intervals. I rarely market this thing, to my own detriment. Results were, well, *okay*. Lots of clicks, a couple of free sign ups, and *one* paid (thank you\!). Medium’s analytics are seriously robust (it tells you minutes read by post), and Substack’s are fine for my purposes. I have a feeling that the paywall is off-putting. Interestingly enough, direct shares (1:1) leads to the highest conversion. Word of mouth, or in this case, an email forwarded to a friend.  
Referrals. Makes sense.  
Am I desperate, thirsty, for follows? Maybe a tad. Who am I kidding, I think we all are to some extent. I’ve made amends by telling myself that it’s more important to have an audience of quality, rather than quantity. Another way to gain social media influence (yuck) is by building your namesake as a informative or comedic voice on an emerging platform. Seize opportunity by striking when the iron’s hot.  
Recent things I wrote about in the preamble that made headlines. The founders of Trash, AI for video edits, wrote about leveraging TikTok for growth ([TC](https://techcrunch.com/2020/02/17/leveraging-tiktok-for-growth/)), and the Gold Open founder recently started a multicultural film fund (Dead). Ask and you shall receive I guess. Something else that I enjoy that has really been popping up in my YouTube feed \- experts analyzing their respective craft in movies. Conde Nast brands seem to be pumping out these videos daily.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
