# Issue 44

Hello\! A couple of things top of mind. 1\) The World Cup final. What a game. Croatia lost due to their timidness and few, dire mistakes. The French won because they’re so fast, and masters at quickly converting. I can’t wait to watch Mbappe mature. Hard to believe he’s just a teenager. Griezmann’s Fortnight L dance after the penalty was wild. I doubt Epic Games paid him. It’s estimated that nearly half the world (3.2b ppl) tuned in to watch the match — talk about earned media (free advertising in a game that runs in 45 minute halves without it — love it). It’ll always be near impossible to predict the total number of viewers because of viewing parties and illegal live streaming, but the former could be solved with a camera, lidar, and computer vision. Though that’s the last thing I’d want in my living room (oh wait, I don’t own a TV). 2\) I’ve harped on this many a time, and yet again, I find myself thinking about online and offline sub-communities and niche audiences (read: echo chambers). Factions. I feel like social media networks (and their advertisers) are in a constant war with the ideals of freedom of speech and freedom of the press. There’s a lot going on in this space, and I’m focused on forming a clear, even-keeled, well-founded opinion on this topic… you know, the First Amendment. I’m doing this by looking at the interplay of unification & bifurcation, and inclusivity & diversity. The inverse relationships of each should touch upon individual identity and mass culture. But really what this boils down to is morality and tribalism. We see it often, plain as day. Influencers are weaponizing their followers, making them small armies holding pitch forks or tiki torches. The hive mind. It’s dangerous out there. Okay, that’s enough anthropology for the day — don’t want to get burned or skewered. 3\) I recently heard that Charles King often says, “Every dud knows a stud.” I like this because it asks the individual to remain poised and courteous, especially when dealing with halfwits. Goodness and decency. Excellent stuff. That’s all for this week, wanted to end on a happy note. Lots of news below. Enjoy\!
