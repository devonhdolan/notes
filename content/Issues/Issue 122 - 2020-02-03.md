# Issue 122

Hello\! Today I want to write a bit about my early learnings and experiences, the reality, of starting my own thing, but first, I must say that I am so lucky. I trade in ideas. I invest my time and energy (soon, capital) to help will things into existence. That is my job. It is a dream. Life is grand. I’ll yell it from the mountain tops.  
*I am so lucky.*  
Though building from the ground up has a few drawbacks. Namely, the tension. It’s like living in a pressure cooker. Energy’s bottled up, boiling. A high wire act. Full stress ball. The highs are high, and the lows are low. A polarity with drastic fluctuations. Peaks. Valleys. One foot’s in the limo, the other’s in the gutter. Selling the moon; fighting the trenches. If it doesn’t pan out, I failed, not just myself, but the people I commit to. I say yes to the ideas I want to sink my teeth into, which is such a privilege. A treat. Hell, that’s why I got myself into this position in the first place. To be able to do what I want to do. To deliver on my word. To live and die by the sword of it. In it to win it. Namesake, staked. I am so lucky.  
And this leads to discipline. Having some semblance of self-control. A balanced, rigorous, and healthy routine. Time management. My work ethic hasn’t really changed the last decade, and I swear, if people maintained their sense of duty from junior year of high school, the world would be far more productive. Though many are fine skating by on coattails. Reasonably long work days are customary, but I’ve learned to be much more focused and protective of how I spend my time. It’s easy to watch it slip away. Almost every work call, meeting, email, should have purpose, to lead to some sort of outcome. An opportunity. Long lead or short. Though actions don’t always beget the results you want at the irrationally early stage. It’s a pursuit. A thrill. I am so lucky.  
And when I say opportunity, I usually mean money. To generate income for others (and therefore, myself). To be a capitalistic pirate. Eat what you kill. Align interests. Have skin in the game. A pooled partnership. End goal, produce. That’s the hunt. The snipe and shotgun approach. Fish with a spear and a net. Depends on the scenario, of course. Though opportunity could also just mean meeting with a friend \- to later hire, to ask for advice or an intro, or to just shoot the breeze. That’s okay too. My favorite is meeting creatives with napkin ideas and encouraging them to write a treatment \- for a story or a startup. To play make believe. Go stream of consciousness. Then let’s motor full steam ahead, together. A collaboration. Good people only. I am so lucky.  
And this creates pack mentality. Loyalty. Seeing their perspectives, but almost losing objectivity. Because we’re putting it all out on line to build toward that future. And to provide food and shelter. That’s really it, at the end of the day. The basics. Fear of neither is quite the motivator. This is our shared livelihood, and when we orbit a bit too close to the sun, there’s heat. Most people will never feel this burn, that’s okay, why would you. It’s hard. It’s an unpredictable, heat-seeking lifestyle. Earnings are certainly inconsistent at the get-go. The opposite, an expected paycheck, is very addictive, and healthcare is enticing. (Healthcare for another day, our system is so broken.) The runway extends and collapses. It has become fluid. I am so lucky.  
This all leads me to a few principles that were ingrained in me at a young age that I strictly abide by. To be kind, respectful, honest, empathetic, transparent, and over-communicative. To give it your best shot. Hustle. Commit. Simple, really. The rest will take care of itself. I’ve chosen this path. It was my decision. I think it’ll pay off. I really do. I wouldn’t have it any other way. I’m obsessed with what I’ve got. I am so lucky.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
