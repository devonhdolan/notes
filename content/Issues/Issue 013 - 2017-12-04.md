# Issue 13

How do you predict success for the seed of an idea? This is probably my favorite topic of debate with my film and startup friends. I believe we rely on intuition (taste) and historical data to invest time and/or money into founders/filmmakers, but what’s the basis of the decision? What’s art (emotion) and what’s science (logic)? Will the idea work? What’s the TAM? How do you acquire the audience? Everything is execution dependent, and the greatest responsibility sits with the core team to will their idea into existence. I have loads of respect for the doers. I think a key force behind future “yeses” will be sentiment analysis, but even that’s uncertain. No one has the secret sauce. Rambling rant over\!
