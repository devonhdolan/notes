# Issue 206

Hello\! The wave of consolidation continues \- those with power seek to attain more. Centralization. The world, a landgrab. Real estate gets manufactured scarce. The urbanist adds the expansion pack. Seasonal DLCs, subscriptions. War games.  
Risk: concentric zoning… gentrification… domination.  
The zip code is the most valuable puzzle piece in data census and governance. A golden barcode. Near everything can be extrapolated from that alone. Trendlines and territories. Movements and migrations. Markets, to be super-*served* eventually. The walls close in for the rest \- trash compactor. Conglomerates prevail, the remainder get wedged down, then out. Cost synergies, layoffs. Exodus.  
New land. The birth of island nation. A ship of pirates, course set sail \- to be swallowed by aircraft carrier or to become one. Anchors away. Dry docked. Rinse n’ repeat. The ocean privatizes, turns red. You sunk my battleship. We go full stack in success.  
Bienvenue, corporatization… verticalization… industrialization.  
Ideas build armies. Staff up. It begins with individual talent recruitment, discovery of shared belief. A system, an organization, forms. Its mission is the project \- work for it. Get in or get out. Survival. The job hunt. Many friends are lost, looking for their tribe \- even if they’re already in one (or many). Chase heat, you’ll get burned.  
Field *promotion*. The game of chutes and ladders is what so many execs play, not the industry itself \- anticipate and initiate. The table must be level for it to be fair. It will never be. Occupational hazard. Payday. No dice. Full tilt.  
The means to no end. Last issue touched on film/tv salaries and cost of living. Everyone functions as assistant, as a service, some with added autonomy \- call ‘em exec. Higher ranking, higher power. Support the filmmaker, the decision maker; serve the film itself. Build the house. Maximize audience and shareholder value. The process results in the product. Creation.  
Information inference: read ‘the tea’ leaves. Unearth the question behind the question, the reasoning for the role \- how management thinks. The assignment. The why. Job descriptions act as open strategy documentation. With early state companies, if there’s a flood of job listings that usually means there was a capital injection based on that direction, they often don’t go unannounced. They’ll be added to a portfolio, database, newsletter — lists. Leading indicators.  
Operational order. There will always be an over-reliance on lists \- weigh the options, make a choice \- tasks. Execution as creation. Ashes to ashes.  
Indexes. Gather and categorize data. Call it proprietary, intelligence. It’ll be full of zip codes. Real estate agents subsist on MLS *listings*. We work off film/tv *properties*.  
Our world as marketplace… empirical.  
Welp, this turned into a bit of a companion piece to the last few notes \- sorry for all the mixed metaphors. A couple gifts if you made it this far, here’s a list of TV Buyer Org Charts ([Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/s/2mp3xfy8dplak1n/TV%20Organization%20.pdf?dl=0)) and Mike Vorhaus’ Digital Study Survey ([Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/s/phalvrr4h0yf7ff/Vorhaus%20Digital%20Study%20Summary%20Goldman%20Sachs%20Communacopia%20Preso%20Sept%202021.pdf?dl=0)) that he presented at GS’s Communicopia last week. Enjoy.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. It is what it is. See you next week.
