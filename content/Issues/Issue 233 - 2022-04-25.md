# Issue 233

​​Hello\! Our timeline is a ledger, which generally reflects our comfort zone, and for most, that’s routine. A simple life, subtle moments in our history. Groundhog’s Day.  
Sacred quotidian… from today to tomorrow, do what you like with who you like.  
We choose which actions, events, we want to exude \- our agency is personified, in highlights.  
It’s a representation of time and place, of our common memory. The now, ceaselessly captured. There are more data, reference, points than ever before. It’s a market, a vantage, of our individual sentiments.  
Mindspace: an identity web.  
We provide the context, otherwise to be inferred and assigned. Our behaviors have meaning, subliminally imbued.  
Our interests, impact. Each generation has a representative attitude. Emblematic.  
We are chameleon. Disguising ourselves in social circles, improvisational scenes – genres: country clubs, support groups, friends and family. We are our relationships.  
A constant state leads to a culture with no customs. Be fluid, kind.  
Break the mundane. Experience and understand the new, welcome the party’s embrace.  
I am completely different here than there.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Never stop learning. See ya next week.
