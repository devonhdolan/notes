# Issue 232

Hello\! Stories are solar systems – identify the sun, which the core story orbits around, and build outward in layers. Evolve into franchise, stacked in circular design. Rings.  
Adaptive mediums, many projects dream of one day becoming a film or series (now the same), but its form can take on added shape(s) along the way… when media lines diversify, the byproducts multiply. The goal: self-perpetuate and self-sustain, with pen and paper.  
Entertainment is a subtle, serial, advertisement unto itself.  
The value chain, rewritten \- in versions.  
In a world where Tweet threads and Reddit posts are optioned and made, there’s nothing stopping a story from finding its “rightful home”, or better yet – a rabid fanbase. A marketing stunt might be more popular (and more lucrative) than the main event. The tail wags the dog.  
The script is the lowest common denominator to a film or pilot, and on its own, it’s useless (unless read aloud and recorded). A script is a blueprint that only serves the industry. Hate to say it, but shorts are generally the same: calling cards. Demos, reel.  
Pitch. What is futile, must become fertile. Decks, blurbs, and sizzles open doors. The tables turn when the audience encourages, and pays for, the work itself. Loyalty. There are now bi-directional incentive structures that motivate the audience to actively engage with your work (which I should implement). The naysayers and laggards on the adoption curve will stew with regret. Lurk at the least.  
Offering a \$20 tote to the fans is so yesteryear. Group-play then cosplay.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Study character. See ya next week.
