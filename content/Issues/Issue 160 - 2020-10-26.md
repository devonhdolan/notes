# Issue 160

Hello\! So I believe we’re seeing a resurrection of this sort of bubblegum pop aesthetic, which might demonstrate how a sliver of the culture has been infantilized \- especially in the world of games. Cotton candy pastels, they’re everywhere. It’s just too kawaii \- cutesy \- for me. Not my style. No sharp edges. A couple super early-stage startups have fully embraced this design too. There are wildly successful games like Fall Guys and Animal Crossing that illustrate how it’s taking hold on society, especially with the young’uns. Have a hunch we’ll see it adopted elsewhere soon.  
The season is upon us, which begins with my favorite, Halloween. This got me thinking about cyclicality and causality… and how some studios/networks/labels plan their slate \- programming schedules \- around landmark holidays. There’s an attractive repeat-ability factor here. It’s forecastable, to an extent. I know a few years back Netflix was looking for their holiday movie… and they found many. They’re going to release like a dozen “themed” originals over the next two months ([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2020-netflix-christmas-movies-and-tv-shows/)). Apple made one big move last year by swooping in on A Christmas Carol Musical from Ryan Reynolds and Will Ferrell ([Deadline](https://deadline.com/2019/10/will-ferrell-ryan-reynolds-a-christmas-carol-musical-snapped-up-by-apple-1202755763/)). The holidays always get me thinking about NYC window displays, listening to Mariah Carey, and watching Jack Skellington, Home Alone, Elf, and that old Rudolph stop motion film from the 60s that my parents grew up with. It is enduring.  
Some random cookie cutter things I’ve been thinking about: if any festival were to be held in NZ right now, they could arguably call it “the biggest in the world.” There’s never been a viral OTT app. Speaking of, I don’t find apps going viral as much anymore \- it’s usually just the content. Here in NZ, tech cos trial out new ideas, so on Instagram I don’t see ‘like counts’ anymore (just randomized names listed as likers), and I’m served ads in my Stories to ‘follow these people you might know.’ Your digital footprint, online presence, is a controllable extension of self. The phone as an appendage \- drawbridge \- to digital realities. We take many forms, adhering to the brand of the destination. Receiving unprompted inbound is to be desired, especially if it’s just a check-in. Asks are everywhere. My happiness is directly correlated to the amount of meetings I have. Work is never-ending, and spite is the ultimate motivator. We are constantly seeking affirmation, in media and elsewhere. Okay, enough of this.  
Happy to share that I’m on the advisory board for this year’s Future of Film summit \- feel free to send me any top-of-mind ideas for it (you can always respond directly to these emails). The Twitter announcement [is here](https://twitter.com/FutureofFilm_/status/1320772179420540928). Oh the days when I had short coiffed hair… I was so well manicured. I’m growing it out now for Locks for Love, a silver lining from the shutdown.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Pour one out for Quibi. See you next week.
