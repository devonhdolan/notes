# Issue 67

Hello\! Or should I say, how’s it? I just touched down in Durban (Ballito to be more exact — climate is like Hawaii). I literally just saw wild monkeys and freaked out. S’cute. Any who, I like to think that I was in-the-mix for a VC gig at BoxGroup (Tisch $) last month. I was very into the opp because they encourage team members to write checks themselves. It was one of my only hang-ups from hitting eject from NYC to travel. In the interview process, they requested me to fill out 26 questions. [Here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/22okda1acwhjqg4/BoxGroupQuestionnaire_DevonDolan.pdf?dl=0) are my answers — I think some are insightful, and some are overly verbose with consultant-speak. My last answer is probably my weakest, which bums me out. I wish I said talented people ask the right questions. I don’t care for scooters and vapes. Needless to say, I didn’t get the job. Probably because I answered in blue text. That’s okay. I hold zero contempt. I’m doing something way cooler right now anyways. I’ll be pitching them friend’s startups in the near future. I appreciated that they asked people to fill out questions to get a sense of their candidates, but they should’ve skinnied down the list to 5–10 tops. For example: why you, why us, what’s interesting, what are you proud of, what’s your fave tech co, founder, and product, etc. (I grilled potential interns with similar basic questions.) This was a relative chore for both parties. I knocked the assignment out over one night though. I think my responses should hold you over for the next few weeks while I go dormant. \[Two sidebars: 1\) Broadly, I don’t believe in generalists. I prefer specialists with a distinct set of interests and domain expertise. I don’t think one person can know the ins and outs of all the industries. Though I think people need to understand various business models and accounting methods, and ideas that have vertical and/or horizontal impact. 2\) Before meeting you, if a financier asks for an investment memo on a company they likely haven’t heard of before, they’re a total quack that’s desperate for deal flow and ideas. This did not happen w/ BoxGroup, I’ve just seen the bs request before.\] This’ll likely be my last blog for the year — I’ll likely be coming back online on the 14th. The film/TV industry is closed the next few weeks, and I’ll be on a two-week road trip on the Garden Route from Jeffrey’s Bay (surf mecca where Mick Fanning [punched](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xrt27dZ7DOA) a shark) to Cape Town. Can’t wait to walk with elephants and ride ostriches — videos will def ensue. I don’t think I’ll have much, if any, internet access. Wish me luck. As this is my last “blog” for 2018, here are some of my favorites. Fave film: Mandy. A simple revenge story oozing with vision and creative risk. Screw the Academy for disqualifying it, and the last Johann Johannsson score, because RLJ conducted a day-and-date release. They’re a bunch of old farts. Antiquated. I really liked Unsane and A Quiet Place. 22 July and Searching were up there as well. I was craving B-grade edge of your seat thrillers this year. Hard and fast things that punch you in the face. (To put things into perspective, Call Me By Your Name topped my list last year, first by a long shot.) Fave show: Bodyguard. I really enjoyed Killing Eve too. I spent the most time binging Brooklyn Nine-Nine though. That’s all for now. Merry Christmas. See you next year\!
