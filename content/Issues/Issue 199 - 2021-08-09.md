# Issue 199

Hello\! We’re back from our short jaunt around the North Island \- it was a lot of fun and much needed. The vacay blues have already come and gone. A highlight was dropping by the Surrealist exhibit at the Te Papa on the waterfront in Wellington with famed works from Magritte, Dalí, Duchamp, Ernst, and more.  
It was overwhelmingly inspiring (much more-so than the game developers conference a floor below) and got me thinking about the obvious. The Surrealist movement unraveled in the early 20s in Europe (Paris of course) and later spread to the Americas in the ensuing decades. Artists quit their jobs and moved from afar to join the community. To start a movement. To create.  
Dreamworld. The (literal) dreams were painted, and documented, mostly by the artists themselves in self-published magazines and [Minotaure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minotaure) \- curation by committee. Exhibit for show. The collective output wildly impressive. A consciousness. Reminiscent of [Cahiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cahiers_du_Cin%C3%A9ma) and the French New Wave filmmakers. These artists ostensibly altered the course of humanity, and still do to this day. Art prevails. It is forever. A public good. Ownership as ephemera. For audience, everlasting.  
The most definitive *form* of action is creation. It is life-*form*. It is now everywhere all at once, digital. Our physical body as dirt, as compost. Our online body cryonically preserved, a self-selected memento to be shown eternally. Curation of self. Your profile as a gallery exhibit.  
Welcome to the desert of the real.  
I think of us today. Our time as a shared memory. The information age knows no end. Movements, social. Everyone as user, as creator and commentator. All consumers. No artist. It’s excessiveness. It’s disposability and fragility. Where content is service for product and marketing alike. Off-grid escapism, to be uploaded later, to be collected as token. The absurdity of it all might very well be the point. Life-*form* everywhere.  
A game of infinite regress, an Escher staircase. The egg as chicken, (de)coupled.  
When I play witness to a piece of art, I try to let it wash over me. It is innately interactive. All *art* is. Such is creation with intent. I want to inhabit the creator and their creation, to occupy their mindspace \- art as object, as material capsule of a particular time and place. History manifested. A world apart. A window display as experience. No, a mirror. An abstracted reflection.  
Art as portal for perspective and empathy.  
I don’t know if this can be said about the majority of content we surround ourselves with today. It’s a hunt for likes, as nourishment. The great *feed*. It exists as search for kinship. Affection. The content purposefully winks back, in its shimmering plastic glow — a broken 5th Avenue holiday display. This relatability serves as form for discovery, to be known is to be loved.  
The metrics of desire will one day be repackaged just to be sold anew.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Sound Image :: Beauty. See you next week.
