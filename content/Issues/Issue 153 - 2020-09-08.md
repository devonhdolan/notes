# Issue 153

Hello\! Poor time management on my part laboring over the three-day weekend here in the States (and also trying to enjoy it) led this week’s preamble to escape me. I was actually writing \- more like outlining, daydreaming, and researching \- something else. Something way more fun. That one will be awhile… letting it simmer :)  
Couple fleeting thoughts: 1\) I was talking to a friend yesterday about feature film debuts, and think the upstarts will have to achieve something truly groundbreaking (and technical) to cut through the noise. You’ll need to be knighted by a legend, who’ll take an EP credit, to be your bat signal. Think it’s harder now than ever before, not to make the film, but for it to be seen… by the right people. 2\) Social media has morphed, devolved into one great big sales channel \- much of it does not accurately reflect reality, more like perceived reality. It is pageantry for the individual. Depicted fun, a lost cause. The metrics won. 3\) Generational cycles and the media mainstream. I think we’ll be seeing more film/tv calling back to the 90s, rather than the 80s, in the coming years. Last decade we saw retro-inspired nostalgia hits like Stranger Things and Ready Player One. People eat that shit up. Time is a flat circle.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
