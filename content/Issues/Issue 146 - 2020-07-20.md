# Issue 146

Hello\! Tomorrow’s my last day here in NYC. And this is see you later, not a goodbye. I don’t know how I found the time to put this together last week. Moving out’s easier said than done. This town has my heart. It’s an equalizer. Jet fuel. The only thing better than leaving NYC, is coming back. Seeing the skyline on the horizon will always instill a healthy mix of inspiration and ambition. You won’t be waiting for me too long \<3  
I was hoping to elaborate a bit more on a DTC bundled offering around comics as this week would’ve been San Diego Comic-Con. I feel like there’s a ripe opportunity in this space, and I’d love none other than [Image Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_Comics) to pull it off. I curb a lot from their org structure, along with Vevo, Hulu, Nebula, and many more. Though I prefer them creator-owned like Image or Nebula, rather than curator-owned (Vevo, OG Hulu).  
In short, I think a group of verifiable tastemakers \- *not influencers* \- ideally creators themselves, could assemble a springboard of a label for new voices. They could form a bi-weekly, curated subscription box (physical and/or digital) from their syndicate, who in turn are incentivized to share to their following. You could ostensibly de-risk the package with pre-buys as well, and I think it’d be so neat if the audience could sift through their creative notes using a hide-able service like [Readwise](https://readwise.io/) or an annotator.  
Oh wait, am I proposing some sort of creative collective with distribution in place? Of course I am. It’s a concept I’m obsessed with. It’s what [I wrote about in Jan 2019 in issue 70](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-70-1-28-19-1591e692d789), what feels like a lifetime ago, and in [October of 2016 for TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2016/10/14/the-content-distribution-industrial-complex-for-vr/), an eternity prior. I dare say, both of those pieces ain’t half bad. I’ve grown soft with age.  
Two asides. 1\) I enjoyed Palm Springs. It was cute with a classy touch of cerebral-ness. The best sci-fi functions as an entrée into philosophy and psychology. I’m impressed with Hulu’s originals, esp from FX. 2\) Netflix original programming is feeling a lot like the CW, with one giant action blockbuster released every month (Old Guard, Extraction, 6 Underground). Cold take, I know. Buyers, please don’t stonewall me :)  
Last week, I mentioned this PVP game where players fight in their own world. This wonderful idea was born out of the brain of Miles Konstantin ([his site here](https://kmil.es/about)). VCs and game investors, happy to connect you to Miles if you want to further explore it. He’s got a pretty cool job working with Donald Glover. Sorry for lifting your idea my friend\!  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
