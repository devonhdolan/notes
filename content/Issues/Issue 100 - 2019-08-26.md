# Issue 100

Hello\! Oh wow, this is the 100th issue. Pretty cool. That marks roughly two years of blasting this out every week. I was hoping to have a fresh redesign for this anniversary, but I haven’t gotten to it yet. Oh well, that’s on me. Moving on. Last week I wrote about game design systems and I want to briefly expand on it. I tend to chop up the media (content) value chain into three Cs: create, capture, consume. There’s more to it, but let’s just work with those buckets for now. Speaking openly, I’m most interested in technology, repeat: technology, that plays across all those categories. As you all probably know by now, one of the fields that intrigues me most is artificial intelligence, specifically machine translation (ie NLP) and computer vision, for those Cs. I’ve been privately telling people that I’m eager to find a company that will run the subtitle, closed captioning, sound design and mix for automated translation. I believe this will take storytelling even more global in a relatively inexpensive and efficient manner. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Here’s to 100 more. See you next week
