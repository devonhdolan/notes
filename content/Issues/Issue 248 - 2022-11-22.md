# Issue 248

Hello\! The world has cracked open before me. A new light shines through. It is profound. I am moved. My perspective shifts; it is totally re-aligned.  
I’m going to be a dad. To a daughter. And I couldn’t be happier.  
The way I viewed everything changed immediately, forever.  
I’ve never felt this way before. I’ve finally cried tears of joy. I’ve never held someone the way I do now. I am motivated by some higher force.  
A stronger relationship. A bigger promise.  
A nurtured narrative. I scripted two scenarios in my mind, one was cut short and the other blossomed. Its pathways know no bounds. I see doors open, and others shut, before a house is even built. I know nothing.  
Rose branches. Thorns and petals. Endless blue sky.  
Moldings. My mind jumped to possibilities and opportunities. Role models and icons. The stories we’ll share, how ideals are born from anachronistic fables. I see iPads and mind mush. I buy more books. I’m more attentive. Who natures children’s culture.  
Cognition. Inputs. Quality. Neutrality: positives & negatives. Morals: vows & values.  
Generations. I’m thankful for the time we’re living in. We can still do so much more. We are representatives of the moment, a movement. Our shared habitat, its body and systems. A language. A conversation. Experience; anticipation.  
I welcome all advice. The favorite I’ve received thus far is to memorize and recite a weekly poem at diaper time, and to own that shit (yes literally). And to enjoy all the sleep we can get now. The days will be long, the years will be short. I can’t wait.  
We begin anew. Over the moon.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Love you Anmari. See ya next week. 2 left.
