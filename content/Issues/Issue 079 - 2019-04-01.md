# Issue 79

Hello\! It’s been a whirlwind of a week in Tokyo. So much sushi. This place is like a bigger, cleaner, and friendlier version of NYC. It’s quite difficult to describe, but Anthony Bourdain does a brilliant job of cutting it in his [episode notes](http://anthonybourdain.tumblr.com/post/65527686287/parental-advisory-this-program-is-for-mature). A couple of slight observations from walking the streets. 1\) I was finally served my first ad within Google Maps, which certainly spotlighted a restaurant. The promoted pin was purple in a sea of red. It nearly converted us, like moths to a flame. Location data is a serious business. In case you didn’t know, you can find the GPS coordinates from photos by looking up the EXIF metadata. Thankfully Instagram strips it. 2\) Billboards and the subway have sound (mostly catchy tunes), which I find strange considering that it’s such a quiet culture. Except for their fashion: loud and vintage. 3\) Netflix caught on to our VPN usage, so we get territorialized content (hint: lots of anime). In Japan only, Netflix has partnered with a couple of networks to offer “day-after” episodes. They don’t simulcast because the networks need to sell advertisements against the broadcast premiere schedule. That model won’t last forever. Speaking of anime, I would say about half of their advertisements feature animated characters. It’s totally normalized. 4\) Music and food are universal languages. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
