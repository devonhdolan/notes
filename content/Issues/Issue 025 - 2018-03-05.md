# Issue 25

Hello\! Keeping it light today as the Oscars ate up my writing time last night. I’m elated that Jordan Peele won for best original screenplay. In my mind, that’s the most prestigious prize of the night because story is everything. Fun fact, Jordan pitched Get Out as a black man’s nightmare, and it was made efficiently for \$4.5m by Jason Blum (with a \$30m P\&A commitment from Universal). I thoroughly enjoyed the film because it added a fresh, complex perspective that played to both aspects of the Horatian platitude. I encourage you to watch Lessons from the Screenplay’s analysis of the story ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJLHsXw-LFI)). Get Out dominated the domestic box office this time last year (\$176m) because it was made with a bold vision and charted unexplored territory. The film will stand the test of time. It’s my goal to back creators with new points of view, original ideas, that seek to collaborate with a diverse team. Frances McDormand’s speech was by far the best of the night, especially her demand of an inclusion rider. It’d be great to see that take hold across all working environments. Let’s make it happen.
