# Issue 152

Hello\! So here’s a quick [two and a half minute video of the trip](https://youtu.be/E2DCoYQTL-g) from last week. You’ll see we came face-first with dozens of whale sharks, playful seals and dolphins, a massive fin whale or two, and yours truly con mi bronzed familia (and my stupid top-knot). I did swim up to a Mako, my first shark encounter, which scared the living shit out of me. I mistook it for a dolphin from afar, the way their tails move is the tell-tale sign of what’s what. Don’t go jumping into bait balls…  
It’s an unlisted YouTube link because I don’t want Eddie Vedder getting up in my grill. The video made my pops cry, music is an emotional weapon. Sorry. This is one of the only pure nature trips I’ve been on, usually I visit places with surf. Definitely one for the memory bank, says the guy who travelled for six months last year. I just make these baby montages on iMovie on my phone, and the footage is from a GoPro, drone, and my Apple 8\. Too easy, though the audio gets corrupted here and there. Nothing I can do about it. Watch it if you want to, or not. I don’t mind  
I think we could call that Baja trip a honeymoon (with the parents, lol). Oh yeah, that’s the [big news from issue 128](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-128). The cat is slowly getting out of the bag. It’s fun to be secretive. Too many people desperately share their big announcements on social media. Odd. We got hitched in early March in NYC when the world was set to end. We biked to the courthouse downtown. Best decision ever. The strangers who follow me on IG don’t even know this yet, but they will soon :)  
Lastly, word got out about Micelli’s new shop. A lot of people in the industry skewered that deck, it is definitely not the final version. Press was leaking it a page at a time, [here it is in full](https://www.dropbox.com/s/dbyhbhx06v2kt7e/Moxie%20Media%2010.0.pdf?dl=0). I’ll have some thoughts on it the next week or two, in short, I think some of presented “opportunities” are half-baked and stale (to put it nicely), but I wouldn’t bet against that guy and the team he’s locked in. There was also a sheet floating around of talent they’re trying to poach, but I wouldn’t be surprised if one of their competitors put it together. A pretty serious battle is going to ensue across the board \- remember when Ovitz tried to start AMG 20+ years ago, [how’d that pan out](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/features/michael-ovitzs-underlings-reflect-a-supernova-exploded-all-business-1055589)? Yikes. Hell, the deck is just a tool to secure investment, which worked, to the tune of low/mid 8 figs. A couple of close confidants worked under Pete, many say he’s one of the smartest in the game. They’re all pretty smart as well. We shall see..  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Wakanda forever. See you next week.
