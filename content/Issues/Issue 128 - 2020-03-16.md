# Issue 128

Hello\! Now’s the time to take control of what you can positively affect as the world seems to be a spinning top \- teetering on the edge \- in limbo. Make the best of your situation as so much is out of our hands right now. I certainly did \- if you know, you know. I’ll share more widely in due time. It’s uplifting news in an otherwise gray and dreary time. A ray of sunlight. Progress. Happiness.  
Last week was nuts, we can agree on that. I’ve never experienced anything like it. No one has. A tornado swept across society. Wildfire. March is a wash. This is an extreme, multi-month ordeal. We’re all woefully unprepared, in disarray, on hold. The government. Banks. Business. Economy activity will slow. It’s totally foreign. Don’t panic. Be reasonable, practical. Learn from the experts. Follow their lead. Do what’s right for the community. The people. The good of the whole. The brazenly selfish attitude of some is concerning. Stay home. Be smart, safe, and sanitary. Try not to get sick, and if you do, don’t get others sick. Call your family. Tell your elders you’re thinking of them, that you love them. Hole up and get to work. Many depend on it. We all do.  
I’m here in NYC. The Lisbon and London travels were cancelled last minute, which is for the best as I would’ve gotten stuck abroad. I want to pull back the curtain a bit on my headspace. On starting a company \+ fund right now. The skinny \- I’ve been bootstrapping, burning through my meager savings. Bleeding out while building up my slate and servicing it. I just saw three months of runway disappear. Markets wiped it out. It’ll come back. That’s what I tell myself. I tethered my roadshow to a bunch of events and conferences over the next three months. All have been cancelled or postponed. I was supposed to start last week. I went through the five stages of grief in a day. I find it quite humorous now. A coping mechanism, sure. I don’t know what’s going to happen. I’ve been planning this for months. But I have to push through it. Taking temperate checks (heh) on potential partners as we speak. Stay positive. No waffling. Chin up. Move fast; stay low. Be proactive. Have hope. It’s all we got.  
First, my heart goes out to those who have fallen ill, or have family and friends who might have it. Seriously blows. Wishing you the best. Second, I feel for all the up and coming artists and entrepreneurs who were supposed to showcase their work in the coming months. So many people have staked their career, their life and namesake, on a big festival showing, only to have it ripped away. It’ll bounce back at some point. Take it in stride. Do what you can to keep moving forward. I believe in you. In all of us.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
