# Issue 72

Hello\! The South Island was unreal — we just arrived in Wellington. It’s like Williamsburg, but on the beautiful ocean (not the toxic East River). So, I love it. In Queenstown, I was enjoying good conversation and better company at a wine bar when the owner rushed to the door to deny service to Kim Dotcom, of Megaupload fame. He said Mr. Dotcom was bad for the planet. I died, though I have him to thank for my iTunes library. That was just one choice moment from the trip thus far, but [here](https://www.instagram.com/p/Btr7JxWHpwD/) are the actual highlights (it’s my public Instagram — cross-pollination\!). I’m keeping this preamble relatively short because I haven’t had much time to do the link digest section last week due to lack of wifi. Some of my favorite advice was “to prove the naysayers wrong.” The lamest was “to be more gray.” One was an insult to my person and the other was pure motivation. I’ve witnessed people raise dozens of dollars for their fund, or company, just by being colorful and loud (i.e. not gray) on Twitter. That raised an eyebrow. Actions speak, not Tweets… until they do. It certainly illustrates the power of Twitter’s user base, or a particular subset of it. I think having an original style, opinion, and identity is vital in today’s world. Voice as a signal. There’s so much noise out there. An altruistic beacon can help with discoverability though. On that note, a warm welcome to all you new friendly faces. Thank you M.G. for the shout — what a wonderful surprise\! His newsletter First Draught can be found [here](http://newsletter.mgsiegler.com/). It’s good. Follow it. For the uninitiated, I try to blast this out Sunday evenings or Monday mornings (PST). Here are recent, popular issues of my 2019 predictions ([one](https://mailchi.mp/3fd90754e2d0/temeeco-issue-68) and [two](https://mailchi.mp/1d366e47e2e3/temeeco-issue-70)). Those should familiarize you with my thoughts and interests. That’s all for now. See you next week.
