# Issue 69

Hello\! Happy MLK day to those stateside. Keeping this one short because I just touched down in New Zealand and am a bit brain dead due to the long flight. Let me know if you have any must-dos for me here. One quick story, I met an engineer in South Africa and asked him where he watched film/tv. He said he belonged to a pirate local area network. The PLAN. He showed me their content library. It was extensive. Members to the PLAN went through a vetting process aka gang initiation. Each initiation is different, iterative, to improve the PLAN. To automate it. Of course, engineers. Some matched content metadata by syncing it with public APIs (IMDB \[Amazon\], Fandango \[Comcast, AT\&T\]). Some infiltrated a broadcaster’s security to record content. Some built programs to identify and remove ad inventory. Others designed the sleek app that could plug into most modern TVs. The non-technicals laboriously bought and ripped new Blu-Rays, paying a premium than just renting on-demand. This was all for the benefit of the PLAN, which was free. I literally ran across a content crime syndicate and didn’t do anything about it — I actually found it seriously impressive. Sorry if I failed you. That’s all for now. See you next week.
