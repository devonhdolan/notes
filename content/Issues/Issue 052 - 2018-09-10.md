# Issue 52

Hello\! This is temee.co’s one year anniversary\! Fun fact, this isn’t my first rodeo when it comes to curating and aggregating news. I previously had a daily company-wide newsletter at my old firm, which I dubbed [TheDailyDolan](http://dailydolan.blogspot.com/) (lame, I know). I realize the design of temee.co and TheDailyDolan aren’t too dissimilar, and think it’s time for a change. I’m planning on rolling out a re-branded version of the website and newsletter in the near future, and am taking inspiration from the color palettes of my favorite sci-fi movies… so my favorite movies. Second fun fact, The Matrix is my favorite movie of all time. That’s all for now. Keeping this preamble short and lighthearted. See you next week\!
