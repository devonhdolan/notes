# Issue 149

Hello\! I’ve been getting absolutely clobbered the last few weeks with work. It is self-induced. Rocked with indigestion, not starvation. And it’s all so much fun. I went to Catalina for the weekend (hence the delay) to disconnect for a fun two days of kayaking, snorkeling, and hiking. Also so much fun. Highly recommend.  
No long ramble this issue \- one thing I did notice though \- Netflix dropped Knock Down The House [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YCSo2hZRcXk). The full feature. I’m not bothered to use a VPN to check if it’s available worldwide, I assume so. Now just imagine buying something for \$10m at Sundance then throwing it on YouTube for free one year later. For the greater good. And maybe as a lead for a couple new customers. Wild.  
To conclude, Bitcoin is the most successful ARG \- alternate reality game \- of all time. The legend of the founder behind the scheme, the decentralized web, new economy, digital contract, crypto laundromat, whatever you want to call it \- is a mystery. Their *alleged* name, Satoshi Nakamoto ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto)). They’re worth tens of billions of dollars. I’d kill to adapt their story. And if Adam McKay does it, I’ll cry. He’s got the Walton family (Wal Mart), Jeff Epstein, Chuck Blazer (Fifa), and Elizabeth Holmes (Theranos). C’mon\!  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
