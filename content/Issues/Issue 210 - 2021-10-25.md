# Issue 210

​​Hello\! The quest for control leads to conflicts of interest. The art of the self-deal. Selfishness is pervasive, perverse. Equitability lacks. The internal economics remain unknown, the external numbers padded, while the owners pockets get lined.  
Antitrust, we decried.  
The town is littered with it. It’s the name of the game. Make and play by your own rules. Almost every successful business person I know owns and writes off the building they operate in. Neumman takes the cake here playing landlord to WeWork. The scaffolding, a transparent façade. The architect works for a fee, while distributors and financiers pass the chips around the table until there aren’t any left.  
Hot potato fried. Creatives, wedged out. Recycled. Landfilled.  
It’s been on the mind. I flowerly wrote about this in [issue 197](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-197) and [issue 206](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-206)… about going full stack in success. Oligopolistic intent. Talent is the scarcest asset, the forecast is to be in the know on their taste and tendency. Ovitz famously said something along the lines of, why work for a studio when you can control them all. We know how that story ended.  
Next. In another segment of “some social media asides” \- recent observations in the age of the algo. It seems that Instagram is taking a page out of TikTok’s playbook by clocking time spent per post and resurfacing content tagged similarly, especially short videos.  
Welcome to our digital world of homogeneity.  
I first noticed it when I kept getting hit with [IATSE stories](https://www.instagram.com/ia_stories/) whenever I opened up Insta. Because I spent a tad more time on each post to read the story, I got super-served them. It ate up my whole timeline for awhile. The all-consuming great feed. Piglets to a trough.  
A new polaroid. Something similar is happening for me now for analog film roll and photographer posts because I wait around to see the outcome ([case in point](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CVTFHcajkdB/)). The result, anticipated. Revealed then repeated. Drilled down not across.  
We need some more diversity everywhere in our lives. If you want to know someone’s social media inclinations \- peep their “for you” or “explore” page. Mine is mostly cute cats, Sedona red rocks, and vintage De Niro. Many Reels are edited to Zimmer’s original soundtrack for Interstellar ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/3B61kSKTxlY36cYgzvf3cP)). I’m not complaining.  
To finish, a friend brought this up to me a week or so ago and I honestly know next to nothing about it, but it got me thinking. Those who labeled crypto (coins/tokens) an asset class a few years back \- and not just sticking with currency \- might be biting their tongues now… because currencies and securities are taxed differently. That’s where my knowledge ends on that subject. Your gains are theirs. Mmmk.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Joy-seek. See you next week.
