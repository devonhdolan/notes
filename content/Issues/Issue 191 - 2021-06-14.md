# Issue 191

Hello\! I’m thrilled to share a project I helped produce, [Polae](https://polae.net/), is now live at the Tribeca Film Festival. It’s an interactive science fiction for your phone that employs AR and AI to elaborate a near-future technotopia in crisis.   
The experience implicates you, the user, into the middle of the event where you interact with the world’s most trusted artificial intelligence tasked with the deconstruction, and recreation, of our reality. Our identity. Our nature. Society.  
This is what we consider the “comic book” version of the narrative as we intend to expand the story-world in its current form, and for more mediums and wearable devices yet to be released. Think of the spatial web: sandbox-style games, maps, XR, TV, and more. That’s an invitation to reach out directly if you’re so inclined :)  
The app is about 10mins long and runs through the end of this week, Sunday. All you need is decent wifi and an iPhone 10+.  
You can register for free [here at the Polae site](https://polae.net/).   
Funnily enough, we’re running similar AI models to the app that’s currently trending top of the AppStore around digital avateering \- though we’re focused on using technology to drive plot forward.  
I must sincerely congratulate my dear friend, the creator of Polae, Steven Schardt. This project is the brainchild of his, and I’m so fortunate that he brought me into the mix. Appreciate you. And to everyone else on the team, thank you. It takes an army, and an independent one at that. This is just the beginning.   
Give it a go, and, please, let’s try not to crash the Amazon servers ;)

That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Sorry for your imminent loss.
