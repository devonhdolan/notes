# Issue 38

Hello\! I’m back in NYC after a bumpy red eye — mainlining coffee today. The past 10 days in Southern California were absolute bliss. Last week was jam packed with LA meetings, and my favorite focused on [deepfake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepfake) (the ethics\!) and computer-generated influencers like Lil’ Miquela ([brud](https://techcrunch.com/2018/04/23/the-makers-of-the-virtual-influencer-lil-miquela-snag-real-money-from-silicon-valley/)). We predicted that the major talent agencies will start developing their own fleet of Instagram celebrities that could potentially star in their own content, probably reality TV-like shows, where they could peddle merch (tees and makeup) directly to the customer. They should call it AI: Artificial Identity. The future is not what it used to be. See you next week.
