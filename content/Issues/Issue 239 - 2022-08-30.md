# Issue 239

Hello\! Today we went public with a project I’ve been working on with a brilliant team over the last few months: [The Eye](https://eyeforadventurers.com/). It’s a first of its kind… an interactive on-chain media reader that hosts curated fantasy stories, along with an open-sourced publishing system for writers. We can push global updates to it to serialize stories. We crossed \$100k in sales in less than 12 hours. All the data is public if you know [where](https://etherscan.io/address/0xb8a51862964f77025abb65e2c6a39ee8070c8ed4) to look. We might reskin this model for other story-driven studios, communities, and projects. Hit me up.  
Most of the proceeds will be going to writers; let me know if you or your clients or friends would be interested in writing some fantasy stories with us. We’ll be cutting grants that’ll likely be more sizable than what most writers/reps are accustomed to. [Have a scroll](https://librarium.dev/) through the Librarium and you’ll see a short or two that I whipped up for it. We believe The Eye is a symbol, a representation, of the on-chain media movement.  
A collectible, that marks this moment in time… which I will give to you dear reader, see below\*\*  
We hope it speaks to the key features (permanent, secure, open, etc) of the blockchain, especially provenance. *But what are the fail safes if the power grid goes out?* Well if that happens we’ll have much bigger problems on our hands. There’s much to discuss and deliberate around these concepts. Let’s save that for another day.  
Fortuitous timing. If you’re producing commissioned content for a studio or streamer, there’s a chance they’ll shelve your work and it’ll never see the light of day again. Thanks to residual and guild obligations. This happened just last week ([Variety](https://variety.com/2022/tv/news/hbo-max-originals-removed-1235344286/)). With time, most content just turns to numbers on a spreadsheet. Programmable hours. Costs. Performance. Depreciable assets. Yay, beancounters. Get stuffed.  
Let it be remixed, revised, remastered… set it free\!  
Praise the lord of the license, in only select territories. Here are [some titles](https://twitter.com/JonErlichman/status/1564209441100238848) Netflix began with, now they’re all on some other streamer, or collecting dust. Who knows. Preservation and permanence is contained to the Library of Congress — hard format is now not the only immutable object. We have the keys.  
I find this dumpster fire of WBD especially rich because so many in the arts and culture industry are obsessed with their legacy… their filmography (and their net worth) being on their Wikipedia page… that strangers in timbuktu can update. I love the entries that are obviously written by the person themselves. I might have to do that as joke.  
To get back on track, I was writing about [CC0 and open source stories](https://mirror.xyz/devonhdolan.eth) earlier this year. I found the project **Loot** especially intriguing because it was an invitation to dream a fantasy world together — a canvas for creativity. We launched The Eye on its one-year anniversary. It might have appealed to the financially-motivated people in its early days, but the creatives & technologists stuck around to build. Here are recent pieces by [Bethany Crystal](https://bethany.mirror.xyz/A62i2nQ3EKSqHaLAGAlw68jjuBicrnH2gThyLpmG8Z0) (Tech:NYC), [Casey Newton](https://www.theverge.com/23312790/loot-dom-hofmann-nft-project-developer-community) (Verge), and [Andrew Hayward](https://decrypt.co/108354/loot-one-year-later-nft-hype-dead-lootverse-hope-lives-on) (Decrypt). All written in the last month.  
I also put together this [lil open research doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-wED-L_nsAVRlxkcw4zBBOjabxlJK3Tw767fNcNphvU/edit) around CC0 stories that was making the rounds a few months ago, it may or may not have informed [a16z’s CC0 piece](https://a16zcrypto.com/cc0-nft-creative-commons-zero-license-rights/) that was published at the beginning of this month by a Harvard Biz School professor and an influential anon. Neat.  
\*\*I plan to give away a few of The Eyes **to you**, to those willfully read this — these are *currently* going for a few hundred bucks a pop. *If* there’s a market, it dictates price (up or down). This is a no lose deal for you. All you need to do is write me back directly or send a note to my “professional” email at dhd@wavemagnetventures.com — it all goes to the same person: me. Please include your hexadecimal wallet address, this starts with “0x” and is like your social media name.  
If you don’t have a wallet, here’s how to get one:  
Coinbase is most people’s on and off ramp to the crypto world, but you get KYC’d (i.e. personally identified \[taxes\]). [Metamask](https://metamask.io/) is what most people default to for their web browser. And [Rainbow](https://rainbow.me/) is a super simple mobile app (Mom, you could use this one\!\!). To note, never share your private seed phrase — this is akin to your password, keep it safe. Do not keep it in your “Passwords” section on your Notes app — oh god please don’t have that either…  
Any who, let me know if you have any questions. I know the onboarding process can be intimidating and counterintuitive, but you can do it\! Always feel free to ping me. And maybe I’ll do a quick crypto 101 Zoom session for those curious — write me if you’d be interested in attending. Happy to hold your hand every step of the way.  
Motivation levels are at record highs. It feels like the tides are finally turning. Some of my other projects are starting to gain real momentum too. Can’t wait to share more. Some of which will also be *industry firsts* :)  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Redistribution(s). See ya next week.
