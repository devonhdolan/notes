# Issue 193

Hello\! Say nice things to people, life’s easier when you’re kind. Repeat it. Do it. Simple.  
I obsess with how (and why) people behave and organize themselves. My parents drilled it into me. Nurtured nature. Be a good boy. I can’t think objectively about social science, and its hidden, implicit dynamics. Collective tribalism. Entity formation, business creation. I’m constantly referencing (comparing) others against how I’ve structured myself, and my setup. It’s imbued. A hedonic game of loss.  
Morality needs morale. A mindset is subjective, influenced. The fuel: encouragement. Motivation is nourishment. Manage emotions from every direction. State the objective plainly. Work toward it. Harmonize. Share a common goal. The pursuit, a project or product. Row with the flow. Never count on luck. Drive the cause over the finish line. At least try. Together.  
Momentum, it is manufactured through morale. Mentality.  
A yes means the world. Positive affirmations echo. When a no is heard, bricks are laid, walls made. You can only take so many before the flag is raised. Give up. It gets to you, eats you alive. Demoralizing. The parley, yielded. When it rains it pours \- cuts both ways.  
A great leader uplifts and enables success. Solvency. They are the pursuit’s driving force. Their responsibilities are basic: create financial stability, recruit soundly, and the tail that wags the dog \- maintain morale. Hope, it’s all we got.  
Internal morale will lead to external momentum. I doubt the inverse is true, most are good at making themselves (and their companies) appear pretty from the outside. Reality begs to disagree. It’s all sales, a picture painted. Pitching is storytelling, to a degree. And it’s a tussle. A balance of empathy and perspective. Armistice resists.  
Just over a year ago I ran an op-ed from our only guest writer ([issue 139](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-139)), who wrote beautifully about leadership, or the lack thereof, in Hollywood while drawing parallels to Lawrence of Arabia. I visit it often, and so should you if you’re a newer reader, or just missed it. I sort of “expanded” upon it this Feb in [issue 174](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-174?), and I guess in this one as well. Enjoy.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Lead with love. See you next week.
