# Issue 73

Hello\! I’ve been thinking about tangible media and the interplay of the online and offline worlds. Convergence culture. Last week I was fortunate to get a private tour of Weta Workshop, the special effects and prop company that has seemingly worked on every sci-fi blockbuster since Lord of the Rings. It was a real treat seeing the master craftsmen and women at work, many of them 10+ year vets that grew with the times by learning the tech that impacted their life’s work (no job displacement). I signed some ironclad NDAs, so my commentary must be surface level. On top of that, Kevin Kelly wrote about bridging the digital and natural through augmented reality in a great [piece](https://www.wired.com/story/mirrorworld-ar-next-big-tech-platform/) for Wired. My imagination was abuzz with activity. I’m fascinated by the idea of alternate realities, where story sits at the center. This is the entertainment industry. Disneyland. It was magical playing witness to those actually envisioning, creating, and collaborating on the mockup and physical build of the weapon and wardrobe that fit our heroes. Then seeing the visual effects team simulate that hero’s environment, and even some characters, through digital means. The production process and the end-product will inevitably become more seamless and omnipresent through new technologies (3D, XR, 5G), but the world is still largely two-toned with our mobile sitting at the center. Being able to spot the collision point, the nexus, will only advantage you in the future. All the while, these artists will remain great enablers of innovation. Go create. That’s all for now. See you next week.
