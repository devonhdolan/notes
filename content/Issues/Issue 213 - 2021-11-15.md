# Issue 213

Hello\! Tectonic plates, nation states. The ground shakes, the dust settles. Groups remain, leadered with an intent. The platform wars are ever constant, sizings vary. Seller, meet buyer. Market. Go.  
Information silos. Nodal networks.  
I’ve scratched what I’ve written a few times now, so will keep this one unusually brief. I believe in talent, the power of the individual, and that story is everything — the substance of which is subjective. Science and technology can fix many of the world’s problems, but it cannot fix the people.  
We’re a complicated simple bunch. A broken system, that functions… though it seems many markets are being propped up, to be carved up. The jenga stack teeters.  
Every company will have its own lil’ metaverse, just as they all now have a media arm. All it takes is one savvy hire. To note, I work with *a lot* of people who work in games \- none of them have ever seriously uttered the M word. Not ever. It’s a chew toy for the old dogs at asset management firms. Try to be happy with what you got, in consensus reality. Upload everywhere.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Get in the game. See ya next week.
