# Issue 154

Hello\! As mentioned in a newsletter ‘exclusive’ two weeks back, we got hitched six months ago in NYC. Love a good reveal. Secrets are fun, and actually experiencing genuine, in-person reaction is what makes us human. [Here’s a video from our special day](https://www.instagram.com/p/CFDY8PYFKPN/), it links back to my public Instagram. Cross-pollination :)  
As one traditionally does, we biked to the courthouse, signed some papers, said a few words, kissed, and got a certificate. We had Nom Wah for lunch and celebrated with friends at Roberta’s for dinner. God I miss the city, so much.  
We decided to keep the ordeal a ‘relative’ secret, though if we chatted on the phone I probably couldn’t contain myself and spread the good word. We find it strange that people immediately take to social media to announce their personal news (but it’s okay six months later, right?) to bathe in the likes and congrats. Subtle screams for attention. Admittedly, it does feel very good.  
I think social media has bred a near uncontainable “positive affirmation” ecosystem, an offshoot from the participation trophy generation. This includes me… and most of you. It’s not a bad thing, it’s just fake.  
Some passing thoughts. We downloaded Disney+ last week. Late to the party, but I already watched The Mandalorian at a friend’s place when it first came out. The fam wanted to watch Mulan and I wanted to see the PVOD page, and that’s when I saw that Disney+ was pushing its own coin (I can’t seem to find any articles about this\!). It got me chin scratching about Epic’s coin which they offered at a discount \- direct to consumer \- which kickstarted the whole Apple debacle. Hmmm.  
When I see the word “coin”, I can’t help but think of crypto (let me be clear, I like government-issued fiat money, because it’s *real*). I like the notion of smart contracts, but who really prefers the alternative? That’s why we have lawyers. I have been actively researching the blockchain space again, with a keen eye for companies that *necessitate* a decentralized methodology ([Dapps wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_application)), and am interested in those that reward actual compute costs, not just meager transactions. I do appreciate its underlying foundation, in theory.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
