# Issue 150

Hello\! Well damn, this is issue 150, which means I’ve been sending these out for nearly three years. All I have to say is, thank you. Seriously. This is fun. And as a small gesture of thanks, I want to dole out some complimentary subscriptions. If you forward this to a friend and they want the sign-up, let me know by just replying to this email and I’ll hook ‘em up. The offer’s good through the end of this month. Win-Win.  
Some of you might have no idea who I am. [I spelled it out in issue 117 last year](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-117). One of my best to date, in my opinion. I’m not just a newsletter guy. This is a lil’ side project, an experiment, that helps me retain info since I regurgitate an inordinate amount of headlines. What do I do? I started my own shop to produce stem-centered stories (read: sci-fi) for any medium, and help scale early stage media-tech startups. Some of my projects are traditional, others are truly outlandish. I will always bury the lede until there’s a project that I can point to and confidently say, “I quarterbacked that.”  
Some scribbles for the week. [Here is an anonymous spreadsheet of film/tv salaries](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nURzxx0ktzLIhLEsN366L5sVfbN9iND4i3LYf7qFPX0/edit?usp=sharing). If you’re in the industry, you can reply directly and I’ll send over the survey. No I did not create it. I’ve been marinating on localization, and how some stories (well, all media) could be built in a somewhat malleable manner, iterative, to adhere to other cultures. I’ve been thinking about this with regard to comics as French and Japan make up the vast majority (\~80%) of that market. I also think comics will be more of an accepted form of literature here in the States as the world’s attention span is waning, and all we do is addictively consume the picture books of our social media.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
