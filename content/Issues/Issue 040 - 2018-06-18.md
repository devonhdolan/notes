# Issue 40

Hello\! Two things top of mind. 1\) I wish there was a successful scripted show currently on-air that manufactures real talent and celebrity. I like the meta-ness of The Monkees, Seinfeld, and Flight of the Conchords where the main acts can perform in real life on tour. This idea has been swimming around in my head since college. Put together a fictionalized show that focuses on musicians, comics, or athletes on the up and up. Those who are soon to break into the mainstream. This could fall under some discipline of hyper-reality, but sadly “alternative television” killed this idea. Now we’re left with American Idol or X Country’s Got Talent. Lame. 2\) In my opinion, one of the most valuable digital video plays in recent history is Fox Soccer’s 90 minutes in 90 seconds segments on YouTube ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhWuflQYv3A&list=PLKHdj0BT9oN4SaBXdGdlkoNFluCr6zCBO) to playlist). These videos directly integrate in Google when users search “World Cup.” The most watched program in the world on the most popular website in the world. They have a brief ad then show all the key plays from the game. Perfectly convenient in this new era of media. Who needs SportsCenter now? As you all know, Father’s Day was yesterday. My dad and I FaceTimed and we fondly reflected on the times when we woke up in the middle of the night to watch the U.S. play in the World Cup (so bitter they didn’t qualify this year). I could remember certain plays and players down to the match (’98, ’02, ‘06). Talk about leaving a memory imprint. On a related note, I will probably remember Mexico’s win against Germany for the rest of my days. I live in Bushwick, Brooklyn and loved seeing the community band together for the match. I even saw a church projecting the game. Everyone was celebrating like mad post-game. It was beautiful. I’m a huge fan of any good underdog story. I can’t even begin to comprehend how some of these players must feel when they score a goal in the World Cup — a tidal wave of elation. It’s a dream come true. That rush. The roar of the crowd (how I imagine the Colosseum sounding). Hell, it leaves my hair standing on end when I’m just watching\! That’s all for now. See you next week.
