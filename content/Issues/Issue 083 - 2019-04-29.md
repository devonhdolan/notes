# Issue 83

Hello\! It’s good to be back. I’m busier than ever, hence the late delivery. Sorry. Let’s briefly talk about what’s popular in media. No, not Avenger’s Endgame. It did okay this weekend\*. Not GoT. I guess some people watched that too\*\*. I’m thinking about the world’s most valuable media network, Instagram. It’s a vicious cycle of endorphin hunting, with severe personal and societal implications. Users are not just the product (content channels) that fuel the fire, but the recipients of too many ads (20% of the feed), which are targeted to them based off of their provided data (yikes). I had a long winded catch up with friends where we tried to confuse the app. We all deleted our profiles momentarily and followed heaps of accounts and hashtags. Fun stuff for a Saturday night, I know. We also ran our Screen Time numbers. The results were sad. At the end of the day, years of our lives would’ve been wasted on the app at our continued addiction rates. So we set app time limits and muted stories to gain back some control. Small tricks that should ideally give us some peace of mind — I did this with FB a long time ago. Reason being: comparison is the thief of joy and privacy is a luxury. I should just delete them altogether. On another note, I have intel on Netflix’s next (potential) corporate acquisition. It’s technical, not content related. Don’t ask, I won’t say. It makes so much sense though. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
