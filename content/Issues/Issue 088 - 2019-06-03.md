# Issue 88

Hello\! Keeping it light this week — here’s some industry gossip. Host-read ads for digital content (pods, YT vids) are gaining popularity. This concept does goes back to the dawn of radio and tv. Imagine’s Impact, a writing accelerator / independent fellowship, is supposedly raising outside capital. Nearly every Apple TV show is a flaming fireball of madness, though the dailies look great. Lots of hype and anticipation around their film efforts with A24. Wonderscope, AR education for the kids by Within, will likely be featured at Apple’s WWDC. Still lots of chatter about Endeavor going public and the ATA / WGA battle. People think “middle class” writers are going to fall by the wayside, writer-only agencies will emerge, and that journalists need to do a better job at spotlighting Endeavor Content and Wiip. Lots of guesses that Bad Robot will be signing with Disney. The Quest is VR’s breakthrough moment. Curation is buzzy, and brands need to own their domain. I keep talking about interactive media (the Spelling Bee play-along nailed it last week), real-time rendering, and spatial mapping — trying to come up with a new catch-all term for my thesis in this space. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
