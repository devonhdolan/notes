# Issue 200

Hello\! Wow, issue 200\. That’s four years of weekly emails if you’ve been with me from the beginning. Thank you. To you all, seriously. You keep me going on this rollercoaster ride \- the ups and downs occur far more than I’d like. Undulations.  
I must say, it truly means a lot. As much as I pretend to not care, these emails mean a great deal to me. They’re personal. The’ve grown to take on a more blog-ish form of me and my psyche. My desires, reflections, ideas, learnings, justifications, and emotions. It’s my open diary. A self-drawn rhetorical triangle. Life, sketched in word.  
I consider these almost messages in bottles, thrown into an ocean of content, that capture versions of my former self.  
I’ll still untangle industry shifts, consumer behaviors, design systems, and all that jazz \- but I find it generally boring. It’s rather vanilla to explain, synthesize, and comment on the news. It already exists. That’s the job of reporters, essayists, and analysts. I am none. And all.  
I have a strange obsession with pesky one word labels are used to describe and define your whole being. Where do you work and what do you do. It’s shrink wrap, constricting identity. Social contract as construct. Your representation is not your reputation. It’s action. Wear all the caps.  
You are what you say you are, you are your credits, you are your only self. I am me. Outsider.  
I write this once a week, usually hastily morning-of. I procrastinate, like everyone else. I constantly think of what I’ll touch on next week, but I don’t consider “thinking” work \- that pressure strains. I don’t consider *this* work. I don’t consider myself a *writer* \- even though I’ve cashed checks off this thing from celebrated producers and filmmakers. That notion still rocks me, deeply.  
I give you my estranged dichotomy under microscope. Sinless voyeurism.  
Yet I convince myself it’s a relationship. A mercurial one. I feel I somehow owe it to you, and myself, to be more affectionate. You’ve unknowingly held me accountable to something I cherish (blogging?). It’s given me some added purpose. You are my reasoning to continue on with this thing \- it’s my truth. It’s a commitment. It’s a joy ride, and here’s to many more. Excited for what’s to come.  
Funny to think this is just last week’s issue spoken a little bit more plainly. It’s all memorialization of self. A cave painting. I really do appreciate you.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Love ya. See you next week.
