# Issue 136

Hello\! Well, I think I cracked it. A turnkey solution, a system, for many of my creative friends who might’ve found themselves out of a salary, or those who took the leap of faith to bet on themselves. Ideally they’ve hip-pocketed projects they hope to produce. I’m psyched about this idea. I soft pitched it to about 40 people last week and no one said, “that’s crap,” “oh, that’s like XYZ,” or “no thanks.” I’m starting to assemble the avengers. It’s an amalgam of all my interest. It begins in the film/tv/digital industry. It’s built on trust. It’s opportunistic. A highly vetted platform for teams. Now’s the time.  
Hit me up if you’re interested. I’m generally talking to potential 1\) first members, 2\) engineers, 3\) partners 4\) legal eagles, 5\) angels, and 6\) foundations & well-to-dos who are pushing the concept of UBI. You can reply direct. Forward to a friend if you’re inclined. I’m very much inspired by Marc’s [Time to Build essay](https://a16z.com/2020/04/18/its-time-to-build/). Who wants to put me in touch with him once I establish proof of the theory? Thanks in advance :)  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
