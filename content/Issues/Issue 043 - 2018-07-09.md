# Issue 43

Hello\! Believe it or not, last week I discovered the show [Nathan For You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You). This is thanks to Nerdwriter’s latest [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZjwLFZNCjDA) (who I support on Patreon because I think the video/audio essay will be how most soft subjects are taught in the future, and NW is one of the OGs). Nathan For You has been on the air for four seasons and I can’t believe I’m just now catching wind of it. This boggles my mind since I strive to be in-the-know on what’s popular in film/TV, and my friends generally know my taste and humor preferences. I find it entertaining because Nathan uses the media (the guise of a BS reality TV show) to bend people’s desires to his will. He’s a conniving schemer, but in his the show he’s a management consultant. I think I’ve responded to it so favorably because he’s eschewed the preciousness of his reputation to play people. A few others who do similar things are [Oobah Butler](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDbSvEZka6GFOUX4HC8IdJ2rVcNOIe6Yt) (a Vice reporter who infiltrated Paris Fashion Week, and manipulated TripAdvisor) and [George Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Miller_\(entertainer\)) (created the Harlem Shake then went on to make melodious lo-fi hip hop under the pseudonym Joji). They do what’s perceived as forbidden — persona non grata. Rule breakers.Tricksters. Hackers. Social Engineers. The thing I find most captivating about them is that they’re authentic absurdists who hold the mirror back at society to poke fun at it. Social media is full of posers who are so self-serious, always on, that it’s refreshing to see people fall for the ruse…. the conceit of deceit. That’s all for now. See you next week.
