# Issue 159

Hello\! So I saw Tenet last night in Imax. I really missed the theater. There’s nothing like it, and it will never die. Tenet though \- brain is still mush. I’m in a wiki worm hole of research. The beautiful thing about a Nolan film is that you gotta see ‘em twice to really appreciate them… or even understand them. The soundtrack is mental \- links: [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/5gqjdw7uY2SRwuindlWBi8?si=n5SuZFnWS3ehBdJ9UkeIYA), [YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVMkvCTT_yg). As is the heady science. (Fun fact: the depiction of a blackhole in Intersteller literally led to scientific discovery \[[Wired](https://www.wired.com/2014/10/astrophysics-interstellar-black-hole/)\]). Ludwig Göransson did the music ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_G%C3%B6ransson)). He’s a G and got his starts with Ryan Coogler and Donald Glover (fka Childish Gambino). This score certainly has hints of [Daniel Lopatin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneohtrix_Point_Never) (Good Time, Uncut Gems). Synth-y. The tracks [Freeport](https://youtu.be/yywzQbZcDL0) and [Trucks in Place](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NaSiqlwkK7U) got me real good. On repeat. Loud. The after, *after* party. And Pattinson is going to be around for a long time. Guy is a full blown movie star. So good. Big fanboy, of it all, obviously.  
A few swirling thoughts. First one is around marketing and publicity, specifically around film/tv in our new world. There’s a cacophony of voices, of noise, in today’s age and it’s next to impossible to break through it (cost effectively) in a meaningful way. I think of press junkets that celebs have to do, but now the celebs themselves have bigger audiences (direct relationships w/ fans on social) than most networks have viewership and magazine’s readership. The whimper of legacy media. Death rattle.  
I would hope there’d be a ‘fish with a spear method’ but it seems that you gotta use a deluge of nets to catch end-customers. Or perhaps it might be best to string together *trusted* associative outlets that most closely represent your project. Like if I had a space movie I’d want to book time with Everyday Astronaut, NatGeo, Axios’ Space email, Quartz’ Space Business section, Supercluster, etc. Center the campaign around fandoms \- fiefdoms. The specific division, unbundling, of genre. Tastes. And you’ll always need to play the numbers (volume) game here, but brand values create legs above all else. I wonder if studios and networks use cost of customer acquisition and lifetime value formulas. Some metrics to the madness. No rhyme or reason. I just can’t believe that celebs still have to hawk their movies on late nights, who’s actually leveraging who here? I think Fallon is reaping more benefits than he sows…  
Other asides. I’ve been stewing on serializing stories on proven platforms in the name of building IP. Though they’ll be curated around specific subjects \- subcultures. Short form most likely. Amino ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amino_\(application\))) does this for communities in app-form. I keep hearing about story worlds, transmedia, metaverse, and all the this nonsense. There are only so many approaches and mediums to produce and distribute stories. It’s iterative, and pursue whatever best suits your narrative. And when you build outward, is it an extension, socialization, adaptation, or alternate versioning? Idk. Lastly I’ve been drumming up different ways to get crafty with local distributors out here in NZ with other int’l distributors. I don’t know what exactly, but I think there’s something to do here \- like a commonwealth collective. Backstop. I’ll get back to you on that.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
