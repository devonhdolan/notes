# Issue 226

Hello\! So this note we have an especially long link digest because I didn’t send an email last Monday. Excluding the winter holidays, it was my first time ever missing a week — since 2017\. And I don’t know why. There was no warning, nor ceremony. Maybe it was just a needed vacation from my usual grind… perhaps even a departure. Though probably not, it’s all a crippling addiction.  
Information hoarder, doth giveth.   
I’ve been media obsessed the last fifteen-ish years. Unhealthily so, to a point that veers borderline delusional… pathological. Where I feel the compulsory need to comprehensively capture any piece of news that might benefit someone (and/or myself) who works across the shared industries below. A recognizable proof of work I suppose.   
Perhaps I’m afraid that if I don’t publish anything I’ll fade into obsolescence, an out-group state which feels all too familiar. And on the flip side, I feel we’re at a point where we’re innovating ourselves into oblivion.  
There are ups and downs with everything, and maybe I’m sort of burnt out… or stuck in stasis. The entertainment industry feels that way, and the future’s our digital desecration. Sorry, salvation. Hot take: our physicality is what makes us real.  
Togetherness, plus otherness.   
I feel like I’ve been chasing my film fever dream of 2007, when I was in my teens discovering and developing my taste. Yes, fifteen-ish years ago. Around that same time a few major tech companies were going mainstream. Streaming was introduced. All of that seeped into me, stained me. The good outshines the bad. Though I’m now much more of a cynic.    
I’d argue it was one of the last great years for American cinema, with epics like No Country for Old Men and There Will Be Blood. Both had a profound impact on me. And around then we had Little Miss Sunshine, The Departed, and many more that resonated to my core. And then there was Iron Man which kicked off the Marvel of it all, take it as you will.  
Bankability. Talent is our scarcest resource, so they say. There are exceptions to the rule, but I feel like we’ve hardly had any new voices since then, our fields of undeniable movie stars are pretty much unchanged (unless their parents are bluechecks). The mid-ranked execs from then are now rank-and-file in the upper echelons of influence, and many of us have been groomed the same. Let’s go with what we ‘know’ works, the deal is a precedent. Industry standard terms.   
And when I look at it from afar, it all feels a tad trivial. Like a distributor might win two hours of my time, or a subscription service will ensnare me as a cradle-to-grave consumer. To make anything in its confines is borderline impossible, it’s hardly lucrative for the majority \- and it’s never been more difficult. Encouraging, I know.   
Desire. We crave content, some avenue for entertainment. An outlet, to plug in. Rather, go outside. Log off. Our new wave of counter culture.   
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Disenfranchised franchise. See ya next week.
