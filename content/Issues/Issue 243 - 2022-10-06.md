# Issue 243

Hello\! The content creation & consumption value chains will start mapping onto each other, bleeding together. Progressively. Simultaneously. The train will be built (and the track laid) as it leaves the station. Allow room for improvements, modularity.  
Idea intervention: remixing and sampling. Not interference. Co-imagination.  
A Rube Goldberg machine, where are all the media assets are layers to be enjoyed, individually or wholly.  
Beyond identifying and developing story, attaching talent, raising capital, and securing distribution – producers and storytellers will likely soon (some already are) acquire an audience from the jump for the story itself (i.e. running marketing campaigns).  
Recall the term PMD (Producer of Marketing & Distribution) being thrown around about a decade ago at a PGA event ([IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/2011/09/masterlist-of-pmds-producer-of-marketing-distribution-177859/)); Producer’s Guild of America. When the creatives own the whole pipeline, end-to-end, then many studios will be useless (unless a fat check is needed). So many studios lazily outsource their marketing efforts anyway, who better to handle more messaging than the creators themselves.  
Storytellers as startup founders. That’s the call.  
Value chains as value transfers, from creator to community… and back again.  
The means of creation, on either end of the spectrum, have their own levers of production (capital and a clock), a process, soon protocol. This isn’t necessarily patentable... there are only so many ways to hold a paintbrush and gather people together toward collective action.  
On that point, almost every call I’ve had the last month we’ve had a quick constructive conversation about **The Use of AI in Media**, the 5 main questions and topics I bring up [can be found in this short Google Form poll](https://forms.gle/vUpMSZzoC4GYYfXb9), which should take less than a minute to do. I’ll likely write a longer piece about it and share my findings (which are all anonymized). There’s a new breakthrough every other day, it’s absolutely insane.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Cover to cover. See ya next week. 7 left.
