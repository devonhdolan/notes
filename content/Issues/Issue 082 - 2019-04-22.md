# Issue 82

Hello\! I just wrapped a trip of a lifetime. It’s going to be a major source of inspiration for me for years to come. So many wonderful memories. Post-vacation blues are settling in. To state the obvious, it’s important to experience different cultures. To get out of your bubble — the comfort zone. To try new cuisine, fashion, games. To see art, movies, music, performance. To read books, comics, stories, articles. To experiment. It’s one reason why I love sharing so many hot links — to ideally get people thinking. To inspire and inform. Speaking of, I listened to a recent BEE podcast on the plane home that had Chuck Palahniuk on the show. The Fight Club author — a book that had a profound impact on me when I was an impressionable, angsty teen. Chuck briefly touched upon the ideas of pacing and presentation of story. The cut of it. Juxtapositions. I’m obsessed with this concept. I was planning on writing about it in Japan because I bought a couple of manga books in their native language, which are pure joy to flip through. Strictly visual, imaginative storytelling, which allows the reader to fill in the blanks between pictures. It reduces story down to a few images and the audience gets to extrapolate the message. An active, lean-in experience where we envision the action. David Mamet speaks to this in his book, On Directing Film. Recent examples of this are Kahlil Joseph’s [Double Conscience](https://www.moca.org/exhibition/kahlil-joseph-double-conscience) installation at MOCA (debut at Sundance New Frontier 2016), Tyler the Creator’s [OKRA music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQNazsWnQKU) (not much story, I just like it — same with Vampire Weekend’s [Sunflower video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKDesEcDrcc) directed by Jonah Hill), and the Mid90s [movie site](http://www.mid90s.movie/). I dig this side-by-side format and want to see more of it. I find it highly inspirational. It’s reminiscent of the cut story that comics deploy. Page turn, reveal. Into it. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
