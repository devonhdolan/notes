# Issue 85

Hello\! I generally find tech and vc Twitter distasteful\*. It’s a very self congratulatory bunch, yet I’m addicted to the pandering of these execs and investors who tend to sound off more as armchair critics and wannabe philosophers than anything else. But lo and behold, I jumped into the mix on Friday with my spam @s to point out that two Uber winners are surfers\*\*, Ryan Graves and Steve Jang. (Fun trivia fact, Steve connected Jon Chu (director) to Henry Golding for the lead role in Crazy Rich Asians.) They’re unapologetically breaking the “surfers are Spicoli” stereotype, which I find highly inspirational. I encounter that implicit bias all too often (yes I realize there are far more marginalized groups). It’s strange to think that being a surfer can carry such a negative connotation. When I tell people I surf, I’m usually met with derogatory remarks (or the exact opposite). This just fuels my fire. I can’t ignore the fact that many surfers are clowns (aka kooks) — I state that lovingly. I actively maintain a list of successful surfers who achieve recognition outside of the industry. People such as Nick Woodman (GoPro), Adam Neuman (WeWork), Tinker Hatfield (Nike), the Hemsworths, and many more. If I were to ever host a podcast, I would want to meet and speak with these types. It would be called: Wait, You Surf?\! That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week. (\*I’m also just plain bad at Twitter, I save my noodlings for this thing. \*\*I know neither.)
