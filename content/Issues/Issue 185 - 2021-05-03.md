# Issue 185

Hello\! What’s old is new again. People are getting very close to the over-complicated idea I had about a year ago — producer collectives, all on-chain, with baked in legal and financial frameworks on a per-project basis. I was very excited about it then, pressure tested it with like 40 potential members, it held water, but let the ship sit in the harbor for a few months as life had other ideas in store for me. We plan, God laughs.  
And in the clear-eyed months since, I realized I was just proposing a co-op. Communistic capitalism. The ‘secret sauce’ would’ve been the curatorial approach to market (film/tv execs) and templatized legal documents, kinda like the off-the-shelf services that AngelList, Stripe Atlas, and Cooley offer for startups and fund managers. Though most deals differ by case. If I wanted to be trendy (the last stage before tacky), I would now call it a DAO — a decentralized autonomous organization. All the rage these days.  
I had a couple very smart friends tell me I was literally just proposing an online company formation platform with cute community features, with a slightly different spin around income share (I took a page out of Lambda and Pando). And that there were existing merit-based incentive schemes for co-op members, called stock options and dividends/distributions. Shared ownership :: federated partnership. And once someone were to have a runaway hit, they’d break off and create their own shop. And that being on-chain didn’t solve much of anything \- smart contracts, as opposed to dumb ones? They were right, I was wrong \- to an extent. I let that ship sail, well sorta.  
I don’t really know where I’m going with this one, but as I’ve grown older, I’ve become more realistic (read: pessimistic, jaded) about new technologies and their applications. You don’t need to reinvent the wheel. Many are solutions in search of problems; bundle, unbundle ad inf. I admit, beginning with the film/tv business would’ve been a faux pas, it’s ridiculously hard to get a project made (cash inflows with producer fees) \- talk about a long lead sales cycle. Members could make more money growing and selling vegetables at your local farmer’s market. Now that’s a co-op.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. [What is Polæ](https://tribecafilm.com/news/experience-new-realities-with-the-tribeca-festival-immersive-lineup). See you next week.
