# Issue 208

Hello\! Keeping this one light. Yesterday was my bday and I’ve been playing catch up all morning in a daze. Marine layer. I’m also in the thick of architecting the one deck to rule them all, so most of my creative/mental energy is being expended on that thing.  
Plus, I gotta say we’ve been bringing the heat the last couple of months in these preambles. It’s bit of a relief to not wear the burden of dreaming up something moderately profound to write… at least so I think. I put a lot of pressure on myself to deliver the goods every week.  
A couple scattered thoughts and observations:  
One, I’m seeing a ton of kids outside covering serious distance on e-bikes. A new era of mobility is upon us. Doors are opening up, costs need to come down. Freedom, it’s cool to see. Scooters will *always* be wack, no debate here. End of story.  
Two, I think NFTs (collectibles) will track fairly closely to the fantasy sports market (a la ZedRun). A market that speculates on outcomes. Spectator sport. I’m not speaking to the utility that some tokens bring. There’s a lot of activity around the collision of crypto and gaming. Seeing some unique systems being designed here, for the hype.  
Three, ancillary is the new primary. It allows you to own your audience. To go direct. To build your stage. Drive fans to a live experience, a destination. Instigate community. Nothing better than being entertained with some buddies, in-person. Put on a show, make memory. That’s theatre.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. The world’s our playground. See you next week.
