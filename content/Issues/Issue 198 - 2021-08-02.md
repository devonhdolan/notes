# Issue 198

Hello\! So my love language, profit participation, has been trending recently thanks to ScarJo suing Disney over minimized Black Widow box office bumps (because Disney self-dealt to its streaming service \- if it sounds familiar, WarnerMedia did the same with its 2021 slate to HBO Max: [here was my response](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-166?) in Dec).  
I will always side with the talent. If you’ve got a materially profitable project, I’d recommend auditing the studio. Yes, serious. And if they’re on the auction block, they’d probably settle quick.  
I’ve heard that Clint Eastwood literally audits every single one of his movies with Warner Bros, and that relationship goes back decades (to the mid 70s). So no, you’re probably not going to labeled “tough to work with.” You’ve gotta play your own sheriff, judge, and jury. As Clint once said, “I tried being reasonable, I didn’t like it.”  
If you’re getting paid from someone else’s checkbook in this industry and don’t think you’re getting played, then I have some news for you. Studios (and financiers, and sales agents) will create a big a wedge as possible to try not to pay talent.  
I’ve been witness to the numbers on some of the most successful film franchises where deals become borderline number theory, that’s what happens when legal eagles and back-office bean counters serve their corporate overlords. Those cats don’t have your back.  
I’ve seen some shit.. egregious overheard fees, interest calculations run amok, and serious double dipping in marketing and P\&A. Forensically analyze the finances.  
And to those of you who [signed up](https://devondolan1.typeform.com/to/sjE6qlFQ) to the movie club the other week, sorry for lagging on it. I’m going to send out the first note this week. And today is my wife’s bday (she’s the best and reads over these emails before I hit send \- love you Anmari). We’re going skiing next to Mt Doom on New Zealand’s largest active volcano. Toodles\!  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. All outbound, no inbound. See you next week.
