# Issue 92

Hello\! First off, I’ve been thinking a lot about categorizing, or indexing, much of the content on the web in a socially networked fashion. I want to be able to subscribe to people’s media diets, their Pocket. That’s pretty much what I do with this thing. I miss the days when I could explore sites by the content that was most read, liked, or commented, but now it tends to be sorted by an algorithm that tells me what’s trending, or gives me somewhat randomized recommendations in a side-bar. I’d love to comb through Instagram by the most followed accounts globally and territorially, by topic, tag, and time stamp. I’m sure they could do it, but they prob don’t want that viral egg in square one. Reddit is more or less designed in this manner. This would certainly surface the sensibilities and tastes of these platforms and their audiences. I’m happy to share that some of my friends are building what could be the organized solution — more on that when they officially launch. Next. Last week I wrote about some issues facing music video directors and producers, and a few of you in that biz wrote back saying The Daniels (Swiss Army Man) formed [We Direct Music Videos](https://www.wedirectmusicvideos.com/) to work on these problems. Cool. Speaking of music videos, Nick Roney’s [Girl](http://www.nickroney.com/2019/6/27/girl) is the best one I’ve seen all year. It made me cry, laugh, and reflect. To the reps seeking hot talent, you’re welcome. Last but not least, I want to spotlight my friend Tyler for making an addictive morph boomerang of the Golden Gate connecting to the Brooklyn Bridge. It’s on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/TylerFairbank/status/1144267881833738242). It’s rad. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
