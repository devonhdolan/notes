# Issue 240

Hello\! As a quick update to my last email, I’m delighted to share that we sold all our [Eyes](https://eyeforadventurers.com/). This is the interactive on-chain media collectible, and writing key, that serializes curated fantasy stories inspired by Loot. A first of its kind. The summary can be [found here in issue 239](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-239).  
Good news is, I will be steering a **\~\$150k** budget toward writers who want to dream up some fantasy stories with us, with a sprinkle of web3. This will be the safest, most encouraging space to play around with collaborative worldbuilding and to learn the ins & outs of on-chain media. Consider it a grant program that’s a sort of fellowship, think-tank, and lab comprised of established and emerging writers. I’ll play editor and producer. In theory, this probably doesn’t sound too dissimilar from a tact I’m taking with the NewCo I’m building. For another time.  
If you’re a writer, rep, potential creative partner, or just generally into genre worldbuilding with great ideas – please feel free to sing out. You can *always* reply directly to this email or at dhd@wavemagnetventures.com (this is a placeholder name). I have a few tricks up my sleeve \-- can’t say too much about the activation I’m trying to architect, but I’ll do my damnedest to make a positive impact.. and to set some new precedents along the way. I can’t wait, it’ll be a ton of fun.  
I’m starting to talk to a few IP lawyers to more or less invent a new media category. Recs? I’m all ears. We need to retain nonexclusive on-chain digital publishing rights in perpetuity throughout the universe \-- because that’s how the blockchain works \-- to push stories to The Eye.  
Web3 Worlding.  
I also mentioned that I was doing a raffle giveaway… I’m feeling a bit like Oprah, so if you sent me your wallet address in the last two weeks I’ll send you an Eye. Worth noting that these do-good acts happen all the time in this space, all you need to do is participate. [I learned this the hard way](https://mirror.xyz/devonhdolan.eth/J0wqtfxN0MziJK148uzL-1taPannV8bmy3PpSJcIEg4).  
I’ll make the transfer in the next few days after “the merge” as gas (think: wire transfer fees) will cost next to nothing on the Ethereum blockchain. By the way, that also obsoletes the environmental argument against this space too. It’s funny, in theory I’ll have given away more “money” than I’ve ever generated from this newsletter. That’s fine, it’s always been a nights & weekends hobby.  
On that note, this newsletter is soon going to change \-- drastically. The comprehensive nature of the link digest is exhausting, as is the weekly cadence. I’ve got my hands full with a million different things, with *much* more coming down the pike.  
I’m pretty sure that if I took my time to write a well-crafted op-ed/essay I could contribute regularly to a real publication (if you’re reading, holla). Warning: you’ll get my staccato style. I earnestly thought this would result in more opportunities all around, but alas. I do want to make it to **250** as a five-year milestone. Then, I’ll only publish with purpose.  
Curtain Call \~  
Next. I was meaning to write about [style, spectacle, and substance](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1569392149204205569) as it relates to new creative systems (AI / blockchain) and their impact on the creative process, and of course their outcomes. In short, some things will change; nothing happens overnight. It all can feel a bit overwhelming; it is seemingly a never-ending barrage of profound developments. They’re just tools, plain and simple. Look at everything through the lens of media production, and its commercial, creative, and/or critical potential. One is generally in service to the other(s). It is an industry after all.  
I’ll aim to write more about these movements next issue, this email is already way too long and it’ll likely get cut off by your email client.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Entertainment as umbrella. See ya next week.
