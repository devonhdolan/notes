# Issue 151

Hello\! I’m off the grid this week, the new “out of office.” Today was one of the most incredible days. I swam with whale sharks, and thousands of dolphins. I was up close and personal to the world’s second largest mammal, the fin whale (behind the blue). I tried to schedule the sending of this newsletter to no avail (and same with blasting it this morn). So here’s hoping it lands in your inbox unscathed. I’m slurping up all the internet in this rural town. I realize this issue could be construed as a bit of a letdown given the handful of new people who signed up last week, [that freebie offer still stands](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-150-8-17-20-17b24513773e?source=friends_link&sk=53f318ef8b6d2e2f55a6520229758727), but I assure you, I’ll share plenty more spicy rants soon. Visit the archives (see the above link) if you’re inclined, I dare say, the last month or two have been decent. For example, [my latest issue to go “public”](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-147) covers the West Side Story Broadway revival, the critic as wannabe artist, and serving low latency content to emerging markets.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
