# Issue 181

Hello\! First, thanks for all the kind words about the last three issues. In case you missed it, [here’s the last un-paywalled installment](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-180-3-29-21-1854f1bbf030?source=friends_link&sk=43e4a078be85b33a4a633459d81487f2) that contains links to the earlier emails. I attempted to synthesize some of my thoughts on the direction of the new wave of consumer media and the long-awaited embrace by legacy. In case I wasn’t obvious enough, I was certainly alluding to aspects of decentralized social ownership, the [Battleship Potemkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battleship_Potemkin) reference understandably wasn’t sufficient enough. [Agitprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agitprop).  
Crisis hotline. The expression ‘no one wants to hear your problems’ rings true now, but there’s something to be said about being openly vulnerable \- authentic \- on the internet. So I’ll make it short, maybe there’s a learning or two to be had in here too. I’ve been in a very weird mind-state the last few weeks. A disappointment cocktail. High hopes, shaky ground, misplaced bets. Lostness.  
My goal is a be a producer, a capital P producer. At the end of the day, that means being someone who sees a vision and realizes it. You get shit done. Idea, meet industry. You intrinsically self-validate through the process. You build experience \- a reputation \- as someone who executes. Perhaps you’ve even got taste, lucky you. Identity association. Your whole worth is tied to the outcome, and if there’s no outcome \- and progress lacks \- then you feel like a failure. Basket case. It’s not who you know, it’s who knows you. Knownness.  
A lone wolf hunts. I was brought back recently to the first para in [my more auto-biographical issue 117](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-117). Unanswered outbound hurts the ego, especially when you believe \- no, you know \- you bring the goods to the table: meaningful projects. They ride on multi-party relationships. Structured commitments. You’re not without company, but you feel it \- bear weight. You put undue pressure on yourself to get these things over the line. It’s your responsibility. It’s how you actualize and why. Generally, all they need is a (non-insignificant) capital injection to get them off-the-ground. To get them, *produced*. Eat-what-you-kill.  
A full plate. Juggled. I serve a broad slate that ranges from the orthodox to the absurd \- from games and films and podcasts, to immersive experiences and AI research. Selfishly, I’d say they’re all pretty neat, and support the sciences in one way or another. Hell, one could be *seriously* meaningful for society. Most have been developed with named talent, blue chip brands, and hot-ticket startups. They now mostly only require one crucial element: money. Given their unpredictable nature, there’s an over-reliance on the .01%. You risk your time, reputation, and relationships \- they risk a fraction of their trust fund. Unbalanced exertion. That’s fine, it’s the name of the game.  
Your happiness, success, is now in someone else’s hands. The ‘market’ dictates your fate. The factors, uncontrollable. Mood discretions. Mountain highs, valley lows.  
You know what, I’m questioning my approach to the rest of this issue. Too sappy. I just want to hit send here and move on with my day. There’ll be a part two, maybe. I’m reminded of what I wrote [a few months ago in issue 164](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-164) about my mental model of how I *should* diligence potential projects (ownership, control, timing, returns etc). Some words of advice: try to control your own destiny, have some empathy, and don’t bite off more than you can chew.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. It’ll all work out. See you next week.
