# Issue 129

Hello\! We skipped town. We thought we were stuck in NYC, the epicenter on the east coast where the infected are doubling daily. The count now sits at 15k, but it’s likely far higher. The city, full stop.  
We drove to a quaint fishing town in upstate Michigan, population 400\. We’re not too far from Hemingway’s childhood home in Walloon Lake. This place is pure bliss. We are extremely fortunate. Thank you so much to Melissa and Bryan (both readers\!) for sharing your summer cottage with us. It fosters inspiration.  
The locals are welcoming, less one, who mad-dogged our hello. I’d do the same. We’re acting as if we have the virus. It’s probably the best measure of precaution. Keep your distance, stay inside, touch nothing. The phantom aspect of it keeps rearing its ugly head. A slight cause for concern. From a scratch in my throat to a heavier breath. I think we’re good though. But still, isolate. Steadfast. Be preventative.  
Reality hits hard. This wait and see approach to business is brutal, but understandable. There are millions of people who are in a far worse position than us. People are dying. We are not in control. The predictable, stable, machinations of yesteryear are gone in the wake of the pandemic.  
I’m out with a tv pilot now. It’s very good. Sci-fi, of course. I’m excited. I’ve heard some entertainment companies are freezing development capital, a *big* one in particular. But it’s a rumor. Now’s a good time to be Netflix, or backed by a tech monopoly or ISP.  
I figured to pitch now because 1\) people are working through their reading lists, and 2\) there’s going to be an influx of new material in the market in a few months. Try to get ahead of it. Jump the queue. Get creative. Keep busy.  
Film and tv productions adhere to schedules, the calendars of the leading talent and crew to be precise. The studios, release schedules. Actors that are household names are booked for a year or two out, if not more. These are the attachments that get your project to a yes from a financier or distributor. I wonder what will happen here. Perhaps we’ll wait this out for a few months and just come back online. Pick things up where we left off. Hit resume. I hope it’s as easy as that. I don’t know. No one does.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
