# Issue 182

Hello\! The generations. I’ve been marinating on what composes each besides just a range of birth years (age), and how they’re used as critical umbrella terms, catch-all phrases meant to categorize a specific attitude. Psychographic profiling. Each generation, cycle, epoch, is representative of a paradigm shift \- a changing of the guard \- to a new emblazoned mindset. Attitude, meet class structure. Okay, boomer.  
In Friday’s Future Party newsletter, they linked to some Business Insider opinion pieces (err, [studies](https://www.businessinsider.com.au/2-types-of-millennials-mega-llennials-and-me-llennials-2020-1)?) of how there’s an income fork with millennials, the rich and the poor. Barbell shape. Well, this applies to every grouping of people. The middle class slowly erodes. That’s my feeling anyway. It’s on the mind, tax season.  
Zillow dreaming. From what I’ve gathered (empirically), starter home prices have increased about 5x for sought after neighborhoods (the coasts) the last 30 years, but my thinking is that the income brackets haven’t shifted accordingly in these areas. [Here is the *national* average wage index](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/awidevelop.html) from the Social Security site. Wage push inflation marks cost-of-living at a compounding rise of about 3% a year. The biggest income jumps are for the minimums, which have just been effectively doubled to \$15/hr (\$30k) with recent legislation ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/02/03/who-would-see-the-biggest-pay-bump-from-a-15-minimum-wage-.html)). My gut says that making around \$80k when you’re 30 is as admirable now as it was back in the 90s. The down payment is untenable for most. The dream of owning a home is just that, a dream. Keep saving, I mean dreaming.  
The vast majority of support staff in Hollywood earn below \$50k, and too many fall below the \$30k mark, according to figures reported by Pay Up Hollywood this Feb ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/nearly-80-percent-of-hollywood-support-staff-considered-cost-burdened-survey-shows-exclusive)). UTA pays top dollar at \$24/hr (\$48k) ([Var](https://variety.com/2020/film/news/uta-raises-assistant-pay-agency-wide-new-average-hits-24-per-hour-exclusive-1234778549/)). This is how most people spend their early-late 20s in entertainment (and the work weeks are usually around 60 hours). I should probably take another stats class before I harp on this stuff any longer. Moving on.  
I generally think blanket definitions for the generations should be avoided, it’s forced segmentation. Ham-fisted pigeonholing. Animal, kingdom. I think we should focus on uplifting the younger voices, empowering them with the essentials for their respective industries, apprenticeships not free internships. Education tethered to gainful employment. Prospects. [Last June in issue 143](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-143?) I wrote some words about how we should potentially re-fashion the ‘professional’ education system. My friend tipped me to [Thrive Scholars](https://www.thrivescholars.org/) which pairs execs with ambitious students of color, the success data they have on display is seriously commendable. This is what’s important.  
But to bring this back to entertainment, I think the industry does very little to support emerging talent. Seedlings. It requires too much development, time where no one is paid for evangelizing a fresh name \- an untested story \- on both sides of the lens. Most seem to breakout late, in their 30s and beyond, and many have to give up before then. They can’t afford to stick around for their day to come. Instead, the biggest filmmakers and producers have staying power because of how time has evolved… because of the generations. Many haven’t heard No the last 20+ years. They’re old whales, who suck the air out of the room with \$100m+ budgets. And their kids will run the town tomorrow.  
Of course no one is going turn down a Spielberg or Scorsese, the studio execs and financiers who are in positions to say Yes were raised on their stories. They’re not going to deny the filmmaker that inspired them to get in this racket in the first place. And of course they’re brilliant. Yes, I know, ageism cuts both ways. Keep with tradition, respect your elders, but bring some new flavor to the mix. That’s all I’m saying.  
A rising tide lifts all boats. You class, your peers, rise together. Support each other, and when you’ve made it \- extend a hand to someone below. Or just retire \- get out of the way. And do your best to bring stories to life that have some substance. Beyond income kvetching and power struggles, there are far worse generational issues that have stuck around that need to be fixed. Lead with love.  
Tuning out of this one listening to Changes by 2Pac ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/1ofhfV90EnYhEr7Un2fWiv?si=376d8e416ed44e65), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eXvBjCO19QY)).  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Live your truth. See you next week.
