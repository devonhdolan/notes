# Issue 58

Hello\! Okay, bear with me here while my sci-fi imagination gets the best of me, but I’ve been thinking a lot about civilization, colonization, and modern urbanization, in totally new territories. (Because I like the undefined and unexplored.) Space seems to be on everyone’s mind when asked about the new frontier, but we’re overlooking the earth. The ocean. The UN released [this](http://www.ipcc.ch/report/sr15/) Climate Change report, which states that our planet is getting warmer and the oceans will have risen in 20 years. Manhattan will be in constant flood. Waterworld will feel all too real. We can either fix or abandon our problems, and the latter gets all the hype, but we can’t all afford to just hit eject. If we were to start drumming up some different solves, I’d think of self-sustaining (livable) ocean pods, amphibious vehicles, hydroponic systems, 3D-printed reefs, renewable energy, and all sorts of complementary bells and whistles that are eco-friendly. What I’m getting at looks like the Star Wars Underwater City in The Phantom Menace. Surely James Cameron will allude to this in his upcoming Avatar films — that guy is obsessed with the ocean and diving submarines. It’s a shame that BioShock is stuck in development hell at Universal — I think more people would start thinking of this especially when seeing the art-deco city [Rapture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapture_\(BioShock\)). It’d be like Paris’ SOM building, but underwater. Okay, that’s all for now. See you next week\!
