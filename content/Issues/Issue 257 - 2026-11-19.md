# Issue 257: dump on retail

Hello! Of stages and storefronts. I chase inspiration. All I see are museums and galleries. Mini markets with a singular desire: a line out the door - be it (ticketed) visitors or collectors - ideally repeat customers (accompanied by their friends). 

Do they all mingle together? Do they want them to? Do they buy the experience or the product, both? Do they visit to see or be seen? 

Location location location. Do you control where you show up? Am I wedged here in your inbox between political spam, a credit card statement, and an urgent work email? This stage sucks.

All traffic is not equal, sheeple.

No matter, it all must be curated - the clientele and of course the inventory. One begets the other, with wall to wall (self) selection. In his own image. Facebook and TBH did it right, through educational clusters. Get paid, made, or laid - an old adage in consumer.

We are narcissists. Viruses. We are walking networks (with effects!). Our friends, our affiliates. Our crowd, measly but mighty; quality or quantity. Dunbar’s number. 

Lead by example, if you don’t the fish rots from the head. As does its school. Reputational risk.

Access as, or is, acquisition. 

This is all my way of saying: hospitality and entertainment grow more interlinked through eventization. Come one come all?

Punch line. Everything’s a party, a scam. Rejoice. First Thursdays. Grand ceremony. The opening bell rings. The markets open. Rallying cry. What’s our distribution edge and our exit strategy. One in the same. Reinforcing loops. But are you on the list? I can’t afford it sorry.

Life events. I’ve learned from legends in the NYC nightlight scene - birthdays are the heartbeat of bars. If I can do it, so can you. 

New zines and publications are kickstarted by hand selecting writers and artists with an existing network and brand (attention harvesting, clout leeching). It’s barely more tasteful than early Buzzfeed. Music festivals with their acts, museums. Theaters are a gallery. 

Big pimpin. How do we achieve and activate our audience? By cherry picking an existing product, or making it ourselves. Walk down the red light, a gallery. Film festival, a gallery museum hybrid. Everything’s for sale.

Come for the tool, stay for the network.

There’ll be an in-joke here in a few weeks with the name I’m going to hang this venture’s hat on. 

That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Window shopping constructs. See ya next week.
