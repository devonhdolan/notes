# Issue 140

Hello\! So. Today I was planning on elaborating on last week’s issue \- leadership in Hollywood \- written by a friendly unnamed talent rep, though doing that seems trivial when the Black Lives Matter movement is playing out center stage.  
To state the obvious, nothing is more important than fighting systemic racism.  
Candidly, I didn’t know a way-in for this preamble. I still don’t. I figure most people don’t want to read the thoughts of a straight, cis, white dude who grew up in a beach town in Southern California on this topic. But I’ve done some tough reflection the last few weeks, especially around the film/tv business and my own one-man shop, and I encourage you to do the same… as I’m sure you’ll uncover some glaring problems.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
