# Issue 219

Hello\! I’m very excited for what’s in store for us this year, here’s hoping we all live up to our resolutions \-- our personal roadmap defined, to be executed against.  
Our reflections are internal debates. The holiday break is a moment to breathe clarity. These last two weeks allow us to think inward, prioritize, and validate externally in the time to come. It’s a fulfilling exercise because it gives us reason to diagnose our purpose, to listen to our true motivations, and ideally act upon them. To find our root cause (“the why”) is permission-less and full of individual merit. It’s the fundamental search for satisfaction, empathy and perspective.  
Double standards in focus. The first roadblock is an honest argument with the self, playing your own devil’s advocate. The duality of human nature is laden with troubles and treasures. I’m trying to remove myself from those who obsess over performative contrarianism and the ambulance chasing hypocrisy. Hello, hysterics. We can all find fault and victory in betting on (and against) each others ideas and actions. The value is uncovering the learnings yourself… not by being one step removed. Wallow in the luxuries and sorrows of your own making.  
Okay, enough of these platitudes of faux philosophe…  
Delivering on my promise. [Here’s a complete archive of my newsletters](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1lJiJ5K-US_Iw1MPEp9ZNu5RElWIc_BnE?usp=sharing), categorized by year. I actually maxed out the character limit of a Google Doc. Knock yourself out searching for trendy topics — Ctrl+F “NFTs, web3, p2e games, metaverse etc”.  
So with the new year, we welcome our buzzword-verse. I don’t think I’m going to do a lengthy prediction post because they’re trite and [last year’s post works here](https://devonhdolan.substack.com/p/issue-168?) too. All I’ll say is that I’m a firm believer in a few things:

* There’ll be more utility between our digital and physical lives — commit to the connective tissue, the drawbridges for online to offline (and vice versa). No one in their right mind wants to spend more time in virtual worlds, unless they’re an addict (guilty as charged). Are there rehab centers in *le metaverse*?  
* Franchise extensions of cinematic universes are going to increase at a rapid clip to drive people to subscribe to the Maxes and Pluses. The IP cow will be milked evergreen, chafing will ensue. Playability is scalability. Serialize and localize. Your preferred login (email and/or wallet) will grant added access to the stories and celebs you desire. The trusted keys jingle, cross-platform.  
* Creators will gain more ownership and freedom (financial independence) by going direct, companies (distributors/publishers) will become leaner and meaner until consolidation’s forced. Sovereignty of the self. Be your own maker.

Things are moving faster than ever, it’s overwhelming, try not to get left behind.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Here’s to a banner year. See ya next week.
