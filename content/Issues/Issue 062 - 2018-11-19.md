# Issue 62

Hello\! I’m writing from the road, where I’ll be the next six months. I’m in CA now, then I go to South Africa, New Zealand, Australia, SE Asia, and Japan. I plan on spending about a month in each place. I’m beyond stoked. I left my job at Cinetic (on very good terms) to travel the world and focus on personal projects, from stories to startups. I’ve not a clue where I’ll end up at the end of this adventure, but I’m not too worried about it. I’m confident in my ability to make a buck. I’m working on a couple of exciting things, and will continue with the newsletter. That’s the big news from last week — I’m going full digital nomad and I couldn’t be happier with the decision. But why you might ask? Well, I’ve never experienced total freedom. That sounded pretty intriguing — to have complete control of how I choose to spend my time, voice, and money. To be the master of my own destiny. I also like to play the game of what I would do if I won the lotto, and exploring the world with loved ones was always top of the list. Then I realized, nothing was holding me back from it. No debts, commitments, or obligations hanging over my head. So I’m taking my small nest egg and investing it on experience. Yes, I realize how fortunate I am to do just up and go — privilege checked. Everyone I spoke with about my plans, from family to high-level execs, all encouraged me to seize the opportunity. They all voiced that they wished they had done the same at my age. So I’m doing it. It helps that my partner in crime, Anmari, was going to be in SA and NZ from Nov thru Feb, so really I’m just piggy backing on her plans. We’ll probably start a photo and video travel blog to document our journey — influencer life here we come. Though we liken ourselves to Nat Geo Explorers. That’s all for now. Feel free to reply directly to this email if you have recs at any of those destinations. See you next week\!
