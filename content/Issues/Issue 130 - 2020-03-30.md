# Issue 130

Hello\! Gray days and Oberon up here in northern Michigan. My work pace has actually increased. Got my first commit. Psyched on that \- more later. I’m going to scale back my musings on the pandemic’s impact on the film/tv industry. It’s how every single conversation starts, and not that I don’t like it, but because I’d rather provide something different than add to existing weight in these times. An outlet of sorts.  
Today’s ramblings are rather bloggy…  
I haven’t been able to shake this thought that the world is riddled with professional labeling and stereotyping based on how others assess and perceive your sole value to them. Class systems and rankings. I wish Twitter was more about the exchange of ideas and interests (Reddit prevails here because of anonymity), but instead you get these ego-maniacs who try to act cool (which is never cool) because they’re being followed for the first time. And they’re being followed usually because they have access to capital, or they’re the source. It’s a sick joke. Comedy. Theater of the absurd.  
Social media goes downhill when the professional qualifier enters the bio line. Then it becomes a marketing tool. The tweets and posts that should be fun and relatable devolve into fortune cookies, predictions, and/or vainglorious boasts. All push an agenda. Deliver your sermons inside your company. Church and state, please. You’re just an eBoy in a strange pocket of the internet, not a cloutbeast. The persona you’re trying to embody is in fact, not you. Don’t be a fraud. Perception reflects a pebble of the truth. The treachery of images.  
Being defined by where you work and your title, rather than what you do and what drives you is rather upsetting. It’s like opening with “are you in a frat?” back in college. Like really, this is how you start a conversation? (Author’s note: I wasn’t.) I don’t know how to navigate these situations. It just is what it is. Your path is pre-ordained. A script that can be easily mapped, based on your LinkedIn profile.  
Careerism is just leveraging someone else’s reputation, or work, for your own benefit. We all do it to a degree. Don’t hate the player, hate the game. The fat cats get fatter.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
