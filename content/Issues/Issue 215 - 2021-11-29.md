# Issue 215

Hello\! Hope you all had a relaxing holiday. Like I said last week, these opening rants will likely be lighter than usual as I’m putting the pieces together on a NewCo, and working with a couple others to get theirs off the ground simultaneously. It’s… a lot.  
If you’re ever feeling a bit down professionally, just remember that last year HBO — the multi-billion dollar prestige content powerhouse — had multiple over-the-top apps with Go, Max, and Now along with their linear channel… mercilessly confusing the customer.  
And CBS All Access is now Paramount+, but there’s also Paramount Network too. Legacy Hollywood says they’ll self-cannibalize, but they’ll never let go of the ad-inserted channel, to be bundled. A golden goose… foie gras. I dislike being force-fed ads, it represents our collective time spiraling down the drain. But the ads are evolving, and some are considered to be original content. Tentacles everywhere.  
Free the IP. Some fighting words on Twitter this morning remarking on Mr Beast (one of the most-followed YouTubers) recreating games from Squid Games in a video that now has over *100 million views* in a couple days. From statements like everything’s a remix, to standing on the shoulders of giants, to what’s yours is mine, and Sam Barlow drawing a rather [hilarious](https://twitter.com/mrsambarlow/status/1465324027766087684?s=20) Tolkien reference.  
Piggy back to the piggy bank — the sow cannot control its uncaged litter of piglets.  
Cease and desist. All I could think about were the (likely) unlicensed rights. In a recent earnings call, Netflix’s COO said [he didn’t really mind](https://twitter.com/Roblox_RTC/status/1451259668131717128/photo/2) fans creating games in Roblox and filters for socials. The Crab Game video game maker took it a step too far, and their repugnant attitude was no holds barred ([WashPo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/video-games/2021/11/05/squid-game-netflix-video-games-mobile-stranger-things/)) who felt entitled to reap the benefits of others.  
All this is quite a watershed moment. A new form of earned media is being birthed, virality. Everybody expects IP to be free in some way or another. I don’t think this bodes well for industry vets who play by the book. Respect’s gone by the wayside. Capitalize on someone else’s brand recognition — the associative act… a game.  
Nearly three years ago [in my 2019 predictions post](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-68-1-14-19-9fd0b812c3ec), I wrote that long-form brand financed content will soon become more of a norm… and that the penance will be labeled as patronage. Think that’s coming down the pike quick but in tandem with online creators (where a brand fronts the license fee for IP derivatives). Everything else I put forth seems to ring somewhat true now too with deepfakes, NLP in interactive media, and spatial mapping. Yes, patting myself on the back.  
We lost two culture creators last week, pour some out for Virgil and Sondheim. RIP legends.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. We snowballing. See you next week.
