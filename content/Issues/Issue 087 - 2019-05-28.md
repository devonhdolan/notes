# Issue 87

Hello\! Jumping right into it. I think the dawn of the studio-owned SVOD age is going to kill most of Hollywood’s ancillary revenue lines, and will severely hurt the theatrical business (exception: spectacle films). My one party trick is knowing where studios have their pay-tv outputs so I can back in to where I can watch a film for “free” on a subscription service. But this fun won’t last because soon everyone can easily say, oh that’s a Warner Bros movie, I’ll just wait for it until it’s on their site. This won’t fare well — I give it like five years until that sentiment is the norm. I think reps, maybe even the guilds, should really start thinking this through to get their clients and members “backend” deals for potential net profits on SVOD platforms (there is no net). I alluded to this last week. Royalties. Next. I’ve been obsessive about music videos and onstage performances recently as those producers and artists are some of the first to adopt and showcase technical innovations. Eminem’s Rap God used volumetric capture six years ago, Michael Jackson’s Black or White employed VFX morphing techniques, and the Tupac hologram at Coachella was unforgettable. Finally, I coordinated a private “idea dinner” for a few friends in the industry this week, where we’re going to cut up transmedia and podcast deals and pitch our versions of what should be precedents. We’re not recording it — it’s fun to be secretive. I did the same in NYC with VR entrepreneurs and investors a few years back and it was a big success. I want to make this a bi-weekly occurrence over the summer for all sorts of categories (esports, avatars, livestreams, spatial media) — let me know if you’re interested. You can reply directly to this email. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
