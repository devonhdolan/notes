# Issue 17

What’s up 2018?\! I didn’t send out the newsletter last Monday as there were about five headlines to report, and, well, it was Near Years morning. It’s my last day OOO — I’m currently in Kauai scrubbing lime into my reef cuts from a good wipeout (I surf), and hoping that my flight delays aren’t pushed even further. My Monday will now be spent flying and in layovers, which is why I’m sending this now. Okay, enough of the pleasantries. We have a huge collaborative section in Issue 17 from a bunch of 2017 roundups and 2018 predictions. Take them with a grain of salt as there’s a lot of hindsight bias, but I highly recommend Tren’s analysis on Moviepass ([25iq](https://25iq.com/2017/12/30/moviepass-premature-scaling/)) It’s been a week since setting some fresh goals, have you been making moves to achieve them? I plan to meet the basics (read, write, workout, smile), come up with a \[new\] media monetization model (DTC, non-transactional, curated bundles), and get a comma in my subscriber-ship while turning temee.co into more of a content destination with interviews and in-depth blogs. Luckily, I can call on you to help with the last one, and would be over the moon if you were to share [temee.co](http://bit.ly/2wRSb8J) on your socials or forward this newsletter to a friend or two who would dig it (totally cool if you don’t). In case you didn’t know, the Golden Globes are airing so I’m going to tune into that now. See you next Monday\!
