# Issue 47

Hello\! It should come as no surprise that I love the great outdoors. The last few weekends I’ve spent either surfing in Mexico or adventuring in upstate New York. These experiences seriously improve my psyche and morale — they make me a much happier individual. I realize that being able to escape and unplug is a luxury, but I think these offerings are going to become more commonplace (and cheaper) as people are constantly connected and on. I believe that this ‘call of the wild’ is a deeply rooted human trait, and I encourage everyone to listen to it because it instigates creativity. There are a couple of early-stage companies that do so as well called [Getaway](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/getaway-2), [Hipcamp](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/hipcamp), and [Tentrr](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/tentrr#section-overview). I suspect at least one of these is an acquisition target for Airbnb, which now sells experiences… or what I call memories. I’ve ruminated about replicating (read: stealing) Getaway’s business model for fun on the side. It’s very cool and seems relatively cost efficient. All of this reminds me of the very short story about [The Fisherman and the Businessman](https://bemorewithless.com/the-story-of-the-mexican-fisherman/). It’ll resonate and stick. Fun aside, I tend to reference [this](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2017-03-20/surfers-finding-paradise-are-also-helping-spur-economic-growth) Bloomberg article about how surfers put tourism destinations on the map, which then improves that place’s economy. Good stuff. That’s all for now. See you next week\!
