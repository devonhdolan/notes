# Issue 30

Hello\! I finally feel it. There’s a shift in professional attitude (in Hollywood) around digital. I define digital as vloggers, podcasters, livestreamers, VR/AR creators, and pro-gamers. This word has been frowned upon since its emergence in the industry, and the people who work in digital might feel like they’re relegated to work in the shadows. I’m sure this is how many of the alternative programming (read: reality TV) folks felt in the mid-2000s, but they were (and still are) absolutely raking it in. Film is still largely viewed as the ultimate goal. It is not; it is a pillar of it. In my opinion, what people should be striving for in today’s age is the knowledge and ability to produce content across platforms in a multitude of creative formats, ideally forming a story-world. Transmedia. I’ve waxed poetic about this subject before, and won’t harp on it now. I bring up digital and story-worlds because I watched Ready Player One over the weekend. It was a very fun movie that I’d recommend. (Anyone who says this is Spielberg’s best work is wrong — they wants your clicks.) Anyways, I’m compelled to write about digital because RPO is composed of its genetic makeup. I once thought RPO was going to be VR’s watershed moment, but it’s much larger than that. It’s a love letter to the new media ecosystem written by the most successful filmmaker of our time. This will undoubtedly alter the perception of digital going forward and will heighten the social reach for all involved in the space. It’s about time. To cap it off, last week I had the pleasure of meeting a few subscribers (that I didn’t know before) that said they enjoy temee.co and my writing and curation. That made me feel warm and fuzzy inside, which was immensely gratifying. Thank you all\!
