# Issue 197

Hello\! The industry contracts and expands. It breathes \- a snore of conflation \- where most execs fail upward by doing some ambiguous version of a similar task: identify story, attach talent, raise money, and/or secure distribution. All much easier said than done. Trust.  
It's a dreamy, dreary place where everybody does everything samely. Where on a stroll down Wilshire you can collect shopping agreements like parking tickets and convince yourself you’ve made it. A boulevard paved of false promise.  
The established oligarchs, so regal, have been sitting on their thrones for decades. Absolute power centers, their tendrils dipping into adjacent business lines. Once agent, now manager, turned financier, no studio head, all the while, producer. The new moguldom… enchanting.  
Wear all the hats at once.  
Access and information are the name of the game \- if you’re not in it, you’re out. Just buy in. Secrets as status symbol. Everything’s for sale for the right price. Brand value, factored. Entertainment companies are worth whatever someone’s willing to pay. Dirt simple.  
The wave of consolidation washed over the seaside town. Nothing’s safe. Go full stack in success. Responsibilities evolve. Studios now own management companies, production companies start management firms, agencies own content finance companies, every manager produces, agents represent everyone \- buyer and seller alike. Deals abound. Pucker up. Packaged and peddled.  
Lip service spoken from both sides of the mouth. A submission goes sideways. Roadkill in the rearview. Eat what you kill.  
It’s unregulated. No courtesy. Standards and practices thrown by the wayside. No one traffics in their lane. Everything’s a *media* company. The amorphous abyss.  
Hollywood, it burns oh so bright.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. No rain, no rainbows. See you next week.
