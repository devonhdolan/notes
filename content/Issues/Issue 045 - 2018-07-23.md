# Issue 45

Hello from Mexico\! I’m here on a surf trip for my dad’s 56th bday — the guy is a weapon. Seriously. He’s smart, fit, attractive, and best of all, perpetually happy. These virtues can’t be bought. I admire him greatly. I’m with him, my younger brother, and a great childhood friend. We’re staying at Punta Conejo Resort, and we’re absolutely scoring perfect waves… like [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zZjXQ_ABZ4o). We’re also with Alex, the featured surfer in that video, and that childhood friend I mentioned earlier made it. The ocean is my sanctuary. I’m thinking about the following: 1\) Surfing — it’s like 12 foot and firing. C’mon\! 2\) Local, decentralized (P2P) ISPs in emerging markets (kinda like Loon\*). 3\) Internet-connected livestreaming cameras that can act a mesh network to prop up point two and additionally provide entertainment (GoPros with GoTennas). Like CCTV but way better. Oh, I have another scoop for you all. Megan Ellison’s Annapurna is launching a theater division helmed by Kevin Emrick and Skye Caruso. The economics of theater are crazy. It costs roughly \$300k/week to run a Broadway production. Yikes\! Lastly, Michael Lewis once said something along the lines of, “The internet disrupts middlemen that make a living by having proprietary access to information.” True, but we’re not there yet. Let’s all be a little bit more transparent. Okay, that’s all for now. Happy birthday dad\!
