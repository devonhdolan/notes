# Issue 236

Hello\! I’m back. Welcome to Season 2\. That was my first real hiatus after nearly five years of doing this thing, each and every week. I apologize for giving zero notice and falling off the face of the earth \-- landing back in New Zealand exactly two months ago.  
If you feel snubbed, don’t shoot, I got the illness and tackled an intimidating stack of many-thousand emails to grab the headlines you might’ve missed, [here’s the archive](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1hOMV1wNWmWnhdYBmhaGgUBkOgqNVH9ZKapEMtP_OTJY/edit?usp=sharing). You can play catch up in about \~20mins. It took me much longer to do. Such is a life of duty.  
I’ll be honest, the time away was needed. I had bottled a serious resentment towards this thing. It became a chore with little reward \-- that, and a good friend earnestly called me a tech blogger not too long ago. A piece of me died inside that day.  
After some soul searching, I found that what I desired most was reciprocity.  
And I was standing in the way of myself, nixing ways to open lines of dialogue. I thought each email was an invitation, but never framed them as such.  
I was doing myself a disservice all along. So I’m now fixing it.  
We’re channels, efficiently resourced & outsourced.. or not.  
I’ll rant about this next week, but what I’m thinking about looks a lot like the proto-social networks of the 80s: community forums in [BBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_board_system), along with [RSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS). The current “social” mediascape is anything but. It’s a quasi-social hellscape. Platform capitalism prevails.  
I promise these will still largely have the same personal tone & feel, and will never devolve into a careerist page for industry analysis junkies. Boring, will steer clear.  
That said, I plan to get a bit more organized to share: 1\) short snippets of convos with creatives from all walks; 2\) opps that arise from my day-to-day (producing, syndicating \[*5 deals here*\], writing, et al); 3\) an invite-only Discord [for people in this space](https://twitter.com/devonhdolan/status/1553553231821025280) \[email me back if interested\]; and much much more (maybe even titles\!). The initiatives will all be subtle, a sidebar of sorts.  
It’s time to turn words into action. The game is meant to be played.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Our return to form. See ya next week.
