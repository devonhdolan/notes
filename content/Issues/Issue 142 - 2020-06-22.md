# Issue 142

Hello\! I’m flying back to NYC this Thursday. I’ll be in town for a month. We’re moving out. It’s gut wrenching. I love the city so much. It’s the fight pit. The dream. The center of our universe. Our culture. I laid out my love for the city in [issue 102](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-102-9-9-19-6b98f590695f). We weren’t done with the it, but this covid bullshit prematurely weeded us out. We’ll be back, that’s for certain. Our future home base is a big question mark. TBD. Hit me up if you want to have a mask-to-mask jaunt around the block. We can sip to-go drinks though paper straws. Fun. You can always respond directly to these emails...  
There’s a big difference between living in New York City, verse *living* New York City. I don’t know when the lights’ll turn back on, but I’m sure as hell going to make the most of what I can. You’ll find me biking around town at the least. It looks like all the cultural institutions will remain closed for the summer. What a pity. Heartbroken.  
There are heaps of links this week (esp from Virtual Cannes), so I’ll keep this one *kinda* short. A few things floating around the old noodle. I’m inspired by the visionary filmmakers who started their own VFX / SFX labs purpose-built for their work. George Lucas’ ILM, Peter Jackson’s Weta, and Jim Cameron’s Digital Domain. Pirate ships of carnies… band name? Each of those houses has pioneered *at least* one crazy new technical invention, and those directors often got their start with pulpy B-movie faire. I’m obsessed with their latest major development \- virtual productions. I have a project or two that fit the bill. I keep dreaming that you could re-fashion certain dynamics of the production into a play or exhibit with a giant screen above the stage (once the film is made of course). More ways to milk the cow. I think [Lars Jan & the Early Morning Opera](http://earlymorningopera.com/artists/lars-jan-3/) could crack it. Same with the cats at [Punchdrunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punchdrunk_\(theatre_company\)). We shall see.  
Next. I don’t know the solve for this, but I expect there’s going to be an increase in quick subscriber turnover, churn, with SVODs. Simply, there are too many. People will pay \$10 to binge a show for a month and then will un-sub and wait until the next season. I know many that did this with Showtime for Billions, Disney for Mandalorian, HBO for GoT & Westworld etc. Finally. A quick addendum to last week’s email. I referenced an old issue and said I wanted these meta-narratives with real-life musical or comedic talent. There’s a great show called Dave on Hulu that hits the bullseye. It’s very fun. Can’t believe I didn’t mention it. Whoops.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
