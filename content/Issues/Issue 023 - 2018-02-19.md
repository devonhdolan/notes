# Issue 23

Hello and happy three-day weekend\! This quote from Sam Altman of Y-Combinator ([Twtr](http://bit.ly/2ENSapQ)) really struck a chord with me, and I believe it applies to more than just school, but to life. By proxy, it made me think of one of my favorite YouTube channels, [School of Life](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC7IcJI8PUf5Z3zKxnZvTBog), and the Sunday morning newsletter, [Brain Pickings](https://www.brainpickings.org/). They’re both phenomenal resources that synthesize the work of the masters and present it in a coherent and consumer-friendly way, which for me, causes personal inspiration, aspiration, reflection, and ambition (as should also the best stories). The act of learning is the most the valuable asset to our being. It’s core to our nature and allows us to evolve. And we possess the world’s information in the the palm of our hand — the phone. The Internet. The grand democratizer. The disseminator of knowledge. A platform for new voices. And to that end, I’d like to highlight a thought piece penned by Chris Dixon of a16z ([Medium](https://medium.com/@cdixon/why-decentralization-matters-5e3f79f7638e)) on decentralization and cryptonetworks. As we look to the future, there’ll likely be a new generation of self-taught creators. The ones who deconstruct and collapse the value chain with a reimagined P2P protocol stack. This notion ties into the Age of Abundance article ([Redef](https://redef.com/original/age-of-abundance-how-the-content-explosion-will-invert-the-media-industry)) written two years ago by Matthew Ball and Tal Shachar, formerly of Otter Media. In essence, the content and company explosion will be stabilized by curators and not centralized application layers. With all this in mind, we should recognize that there are no more excuses. Go learn, build, and connect.
