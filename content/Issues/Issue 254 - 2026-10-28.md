# Issue 254: copy of a copy

Hello! Put up or shut up. Pardon last week’s whining. I picked it up from my daughter. She’s very much in her three-nager phase. Let’s be real. She gets it from me. Who am but I to blame. Bad dad. 

Reflections or reverberations. The chamber, a mirror, it echoes. 

I need to take an oath: for the preamble not to be my outlet. I solemnly swear this won’t only be a “what grinds my gears” confessional. That’s too easy, but it’s in my nature. Aspects of it will always be here. Pay it no mind. 

I refuse a sliver of accountability, in part due to our all knowing algo rage baiting - to me you everyone - and just society at large. The situation. Rainbows and butterflies I think not. So I bottle it up, and release it here. Upon you. Sorry. I’m working on myself by writing to myself, to you?

Farmed. Control. Fate. Free will. Chewed up and spit out. Digest, your feed. Distractions abound. 

Often these little rants are a continuation of something that stuck with me in the last week or two. My spin on it. They’re rarely prepared, nor are they fully original. The writing is my own. I am working toward having more structured essays for the public relaunch (about the content value chain). I don’t want to exhaust myself before getting to the starting line, but it’s already feeling that way. We’re 5 weeks away. Shit.

The machine, a siren song. The scroll, our subterfuge. 

A few topics I want to write from now until then, but I’m unsure if they merit much elaboration:

Bot-on-bot warfare. Everything’s a market, and the buy side tends to have its defence system established. An agentic wall. Semantic space, a moat with crocodiles. Prospective talent needs counter-ATS weaponry. I want an agentic intermediary to be the first line of defense to Claudeslop. Streeteasy is riddled with AI interior design and faux refurbishings.

Plagiarism. Machines don’t kill people, people kill people. My data, my rights. Vanished. We are all parrots, but to purposefully pawn off something as your own is a cardinal sin. Give credit where it’s due. Duplication duplicity. Crop out the watermark. Copy/paste the text (I know a few people did this to my newsletter to their bosses; now it’s from Chat into an email thread). Clippers should be shot on site. Polluting what’s left of the internet - its death knell tolls. 

Rebundled. There’s the famous Craigslist example. We’re going back to that inside of Claude and Chat, perhaps with MCPs. Are the incumbents well-positioned (with their brand) to have a plug-in (connect your agent here) or is there a small window of time where an upstart can wedge its way in there. 

There are scattered thoughts on touring, audience activation, regional theaters, game engine simulations for training data, and agents for fun.

That's all for now. Thanks for reading. (Dis)intermediate. See ya next week.
