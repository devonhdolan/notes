# Issue 119

Hello\! I think the device of tomorrow will remain the phone. People claim it’ll be built around advancements in other facets of consumer hardware: Airpods, AR/VR headsets, brain machine interfaces (BMI). Or, upticks in “new” formats around those endpoints: audio, XR content, I don’t know for BMI (?). Those are all enhancements to our current experience, and they all mostly rely on the phone to be the computer at its core. Plus, half the world has a smart phone. That’s an entry point. Most importantly, those phones house our contact list. Our friends and family. Our address book. Network. The reason for its existence: communication.  
People as publishers. I’m keeping a close eye on the “link in bio” space because I believe it to be the quickest way to migrate audiences and build direct distribution channels. The *off-platform* shift toward personalized customer relationship management (CRM). Omni-channel marketing will always be important. First, go where the audience is \- the incumbent social networks with millions or billions of users, and I guess traditional media outlets. Then, fractionalize for the purposes of “owned” conversation. The exchange of ideas and information. Or, commerce.  
Messaging as an ecosystem. I think a neat space here is in messaging for niche, social circles focused on a subject where the dynamic is one to one (or a few), one to many, or many to many. Some of my most helpful, collaborative environments can be found in private groups on Slack (mostly Slack), Google Docs, Discord, Facebook, emails and texts (Superphone, Community, Superhuman, newsletter services). WeChat wins the inbox game. The tool or service of tomorrow will service this market, I think.  
Something I did recently that I quite like: I memorialized my IG stories in highlights by quarter. They’re no longer ephemeral, and luckily nothing too incriminating was in there. [Here’s my Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/devonhdolan/?hl=en). You can flip through them all in about 7 minutes. Cross-pollination (or pollution, however you see it). You’ll find some short videos I made from our travels last year. If you work in film/tv (most of you do), I encourage you to make a quick edit on your phone this week. Go create.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
