# Issue 15

A lot went down last week\! Before we dive into it, let me start you off with a cool find — the Twitter account [Cinema Palettes](http://bit.ly/2yOiMQq). I admire their appreciation to those involved in making the image itself come to life. Okay, so riding on the tail of THE LAST JEDI’s stellar North American box office opening to \$200m+ ([Variety](http://variety.com/2017/film/box-office/star-wars-the-last-jedi-box-office-ferdinand-1202643209/)), I’ve become transfixed on the notion of story universes. Mythology. The grandest, and most lucrative, entertainment properties circle around a set of rules applied to a new world and/or character(s) that a visionary writer willed into this existence. I’d argue that the creators of comic books, video games, and anime are the most well versed in this curriculum, and I’m surprised that more video games haven’t been adapted to the big screen. To that end, these story formats usually require expertise in computer animation and real-time graphics (or the ability to draw concept art), and there needs to be a more democratized testing ground for these story universe ideas, for individuals and groups. I’m headed to a majestic place tomorrow where THE LORD OF THE RINGS (a story universe\!) was filmed — New Zealand — any recs? (You can reply to this email directly, thanks\!)
