# Issue 141

Hello\! A quick warm welcome to you new friendly faces who likely joined after seeing my memoji’d mug in [Industry Outsider.](https://www.theindustryoutsider.com/) [Here’s the link](https://us4.campaign-archive.com/?u=dd005feac9b003070e46619ce&id=64cc9851f0) to my short text-message style interview. Thanks to Titi and Ang for having me on \- you’re awesome. Check out iO. Our newsletters cover similar industries (I mean beats), though they write with more flavor than I. Hit subscribe if you’re so inclined… my newsletter featuring their newsletter which features my newsletter \- please insert an Xzibit Yo Dawg meme :)  
Okay, so this is jealousy typing, but one thing that I can’t really shake are these venture-backed content companies that keep emerging. You won’t see them called for what they really are, producers. They might be called game studios, audio publishers, or social interactive platforms. Don’t get me wrong, I love seeing creatives get paid (it’s how I generally earn my keep), but these startups rarely reach the scale necessary to “return the fund”. There are outliers, certainly, but the sustainable ones are so few and far between. Keep ‘em nimble. Many might look successful based on fundraise announcements, but they could be more aptly construed as ponzi schemes of dumb money chasing good. Case in point, nearly every multi-channel network and media website founded in the mid-2000s. Start strong, yet at some point, they’ll likely be rolled up by private equity and morphed into an ad exchange, or written off to zero.  
I don’t want to sound like a clown prematurely dancing on the graves of content companies, but producing and distributing stories is not for the faint of heart. Most fail. Hindsight is crystal. You could be an insanely well-capitalized SVOD with heaps of smart people on your side like Luminary or Quibi, or you could be one of the biggest companies in the world (Amazon) shipping a game like Crucible. The attention market is over-saturated, it doesn’t care about how much money you raised or who sits on your board or which celebrity has a show with you. You’re better off building bespoke tools, systems, and/or frameworks to service the current & next-gen media shops.  
Alright, I’ll quit my whining. I needed some inspiration for my rant today and went back to see what I wrote [this time two years ago](https://medium.com/devons-newsletter/issue-40-6-18-18-4a5177002aa3). A throwback. A distraction. I think it’s pretty interesting. I wrote about meta shows that feature the real musical or comedic talent in it (Flight of Conchords), and the World Cup in 90sec “quick bites…”  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
