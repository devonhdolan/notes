# Issue 253: value begets value?

This is a private email to a dozen early-access readers. I’m relaunching my “newsletter” in early December. If you want out, just say so. No hard feelings. All feedback is welcome.  
Hello! I’m running diagnostics. Reticulating splines. Peering into my soul. This is my self-assessment. I’ve experienced ego death more than once this year (take my word for it), and would rather inflict it upon myself than let the other take me down. Seppuku.  
Simply, after one week back in the gym I’m looking at the mirror and pulling at my muffin top. Flab, not fab. Mark me up with that purple pen, incision lines here here and there. Or hit me with those black market peptides.  
In search of self, a brand, a business, and what I (or this thing can) represent. My.. truth?  
» Directly asking you readers this - what do you come here for? What do you expect?  
Be undefinable? There are many people out there who still consider me “the newsletter guy.” That is crushing. Perhaps I should embrace it, or hide behind a namesake that’s fitting. I’m planning the latter - scooping up my domains and socials now. I associated my newsletter with my rambling diary entries, and link curation that I’ve since outsourced to the machine. But I think it can be much more.  
[My ongoing identity crisis:   > to creatives, I’m a business boy > to business people, I’m a creative  I try to make it work 🤷‍♂️](https://x.com/devonhdolan/status/2110763310157303918?s=20)  
This is the least consequential of about a dozen projects I’m working on at any given moment, but this is the most public (for now). The others live in my inbox. These notes are the new beginning of me opening the door to it. An invitation to my home. If you (or your agent) reply, that is taking the first step in.  
So these emails will include some of my dealings. The work that’s on my desk. Many would call it proprietary. Every day I get hit with an ask - someone wants me to read their work, raise capital, attach talent, secure IP partners, and/or expand into other media forms. The list goes on.  
And I’m honored. I’ve made a living(ish) doing so. It’s relational, centered in trust, and a collaboration. I know a guy (to a fault).  
Often, a first pass goes unpaid. I’ll offer one freebie intro, a sniper shot, in good faith to prove my salt. Recently, this has led to an acquisition, investment from EGOTs, a partnership with an infamous music mogul.  
This is my death by a thousand cuts.  
It’s a very strange feeling when someone says you’re the reason I bought a house, and you’re looking under the cushions to pay for preschool. I believe in some form of (positive) karmic retribution.  
Extraction, all the way down.  
I collect people. I need to do a better job at interesting myself in this kind of origination (one of my schemes with the relaunch). I need to champion myself. Or separate it completely into its own identity, a tool. I’m only to blame.  
I am a people pleaser.  
What am I even good at? Getting in velvet-roped rooms (with bird in hand; as an imposter). Writing (I think, waiting for your affirmation). Matchmaking (I now have a bank of goodwill to draw against). Discovering and adopting tech (and defining / translating its benefits for media types). Dialing for dollars.  
And I do enjoy doing all of those things, for the most part. So I’m packaging it into its own thing, that is not devon, but still distinctly me.  
“If I put this candle in an all white gallery space it looks like a piece of art. If I put this candle in a garage it looks like a piece of trash. I can either spend my time designing the candle or I can spend my time designing the room that it sits in.” — Virgil Abloh  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. Never fight progress. See ya next week.
