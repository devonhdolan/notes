# Issue 108

Hello\! Here’s the stitch up. I’ve been hinting at a newsletter facelift for quite some time and things are moving. The “update” should take effect in three weeks on 11/11 — Issue 111\. Cute. Why? NYC is expensive and this isn’t putting food on the table. It also takes me 20 mins to do each day (give or take), which adds up to about 5 days of my life per year.   
Time is precious, especially when you’re starting your own thing (this isn’t it, btw). So yeah, you guessed it, I’m going to doll this thing up and monetize it. But first, a heartfelt thank you to you all. The ride or dies. You’ll continue getting the goods en gratis. Free. So will anyone you forward this to who subscribes up until the proposed date. And if you want to unsubscribe, go for it. Button is at the bottom. No hard feelings, just a flicker of judgement.   
My plans are as follows. I’m going to attempt to implement a “paid” 30-day window, as you’d find in a film’s release strategy. I don’t think any publication or outlet has cracked this notion, so I’m going to experiment with the model here. I think the news is fleeting (especially in this day and age), and my preambles are either gold or trash, but the curatorial nature of this thing is valuable enough to warrant some shekels.   
So I’ll host this on Medium and toggle the “partner paywall” once the initial 30 days lapse, which I hope works fiscally. It’s sort of a bummer that potential readers will need to be paying Medium subscribers to access, but it’s for the good of independent writers as a whole (and if you’re a well-paid VC — don’t paywall your FAANG analysis like a clown). The archive here will be avail for free. I’ll either sync Stripe to Mailchimp or try Substack for the paid subscribers (I try to use American companies when possible, sorry Revue\!). Price will be cheap, like \$2 per month. Maybe more, should it be more? The behind the scenes process isn’t super fluid as I’m using an array of third party sites that don’t really think in this fashion, but want to be transparent with you. Please hit me with questions, comments, and/or suggestions about this stuff. Two final things. One, I went to the Brown Institute [showcase](https://brown.columbia.edu/announcing-the-2019-brown-institute-showcase/) last week and was thoroughly impressed, and amused at the lack of investor and exec presence. It’s where smarties from Columbia and Stanford exhibited their works in hopes of cracking the future of media for journalists. Of most interest to me: audiovisual analysis of tv news, learning to engage in conversational ai, and paraframe. Two, I’ve been hooked on this German sci-fi show called Dark, on Netflix. It incorporates those side-by-side shots I wrote about back in [April](https://mailchi.mp/c56082959032/temeeco-issue-82). Didn’t realize they were doing it then, but, big fan of the aesthetic. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
