# Issue 95

Hello\! As mentioned last week, these next few preambles will be relatively short and personal as I’m working on some time intensive assignments. Here’s what has been kicking around the old noodle. 1\) Account and password management. Last week Google pinged me saying an IP address in Russia was trying to log into an old Gmail account that I didn’t even recall creating. I shut it down quick and changed all my passwords. Big headache. This gave me quite the spook when coupled with the FaceApp implications. On that note, this is the year of the viral face filter. Snap: gender; FaceApp: age; what’s next? 2\) I stumbled upon an Instagram post that used the swipe-able carousel to instigate a story that played across photos and videos — that was really cool. It was a journalistic piece — I’m thinking through how it could work for fictional stories. 3\) I’m hawk-eyed when it comes to the use of possessives (e.g. our, you, me, mine, I, we for/with, etc). Credit grabbing rather than credit sharing really bothers me. This is problematic in the entertainment industry. 4\) I’d like to be able to see people’s likes and comments on Instagram as you do on Twitter. 5\) The “next episode begins” countdown was much quicker for Stranger Things than other Netflix shows. I think it was 5 seconds rather than 15\. I couldn’t even turn off my tv fast enough before the next episode started playing. That’s almost as annoying as the automatic trailer playing on Netflix. I’d like to be able to read the description of the show before the trailer goes off. That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
