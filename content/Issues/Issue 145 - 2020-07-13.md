# Issue 145

Hello\! Keeping this one *relatively* light because the link digest is stupidly long today. But first, I squealed with glee when I saw that [Team Deakins hosted a podcast with Denis Villeneuve](https://teamdeakins.libsyn.com/denis-villeneuve-director). Together the’ve made some of my most favorite recent films: Prisoners, Blade Runner 2049, & Sicario (rip Jóhann). They discuss their favorite scene they’ve made with one another, the final exchange between Emily and Benicio in Sicario. They also elaborate how important it is to have a shared sensibility with your key collaborators, and to be open and amenable to improvisation. Loved it.  
Something that’s been top of mind, I’m surprised more interactivity hasn’t been incorporated into our existing video services. I’m sure we’ve all encountered YouTube surveys asking about brand recall, and the “you still up?” button after a zombified Netflix binge. There could obviously be more play-along aspects to existing shows \- like it amazes me that Jeopardy or Who Wants to be a Millionaire hasn’t added voice input answers for the audience. There’ll likely be more audience data harvesting before we get to that fun stuff. We are gifted these app-usage summaries (Screen Time) from Apple, and fitness reports from Peloton and the Health app, why not our media diet?  
A few more random thoughts and things. 1\) I feel like I might’ve read this before, but I think it’d be so cool if a game developer were to make a PVP (player versus player) where they share the fundamental base layer, architecture, but where they get to design and play in their own world they’ve created. 2\) I like the notion of very sophisticated people re-selling their used books with their notes. Scribbled thoughts. Coppola did this in The Godfather Notebook. I’ll dive more into this next week, but with regard to comics. Influencers could band together to form grouped offerings. 3\) I’m seeing more comedians and entertainers (talk show hosts) gaining access to some of their acts for their own YouTube and IGTV channels. The content is generally watermarked with the logo of the network. Case in point, Jimmy Yang x Amazon, Trevor Noah x Comedy Central, and Team Coco. I’m sure we’ll see more of this.  
That’s all for now. Thanks for reading. See you next week.
