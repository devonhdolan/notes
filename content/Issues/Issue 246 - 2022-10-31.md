# Issue 246

Hello\! Impressions were once meant to last. Creative signals. Now they’ve been assembled in bulk to be sold to advertiser. Actual attention, a fleeting commodity. It’s a volume game, much like Costco \-- where my ashes will be spread. Pallets stacked sky high. To heaven.  
Impressions: essentially meaningless, yet the industry assigns them so much value. They’ve built mountains out of mole hills; they’ve minted the new breed of entertainer, now platform. We all must now play to, cater to, the almighty algo. Supreme ruler.  
Bow down, to just another empty vessel. Skip ad. Zero brand recall. We’re trending honey.  
What lies ahead of engagement. What is actually effective. Events, experiences as markets, an ideology. References & referrals are where it’s at. To be known is to be loved. The ruse as a muse, femme fatale.. and her fatal flaw.  
CPMs aren’t conversion. Fish with a spear, not a net. Play to the audience, or piggyback off existing ones (Ball did it best with Hirschhorn). The forums from a decade ago are now industrialized meme accounts. We live in a world of bots. Social graphs, social science. Spiderwebs. Share inspiration, not information (or both). Curators as tastemakers, not critics.  
Incentives everywhere. Two-word tags, sell.  
Let’s use me as a free sample, Armie. Would I rather be read by 100 junior creative execs in Hollywood, or 10,000 mid-level product managers working at big tech companies. Or how about one widely beloved filmmaker, or 1,000 finance bros. Easy answers. Size isn’t everything lol  
Quality \> Quantity. Showing vs Growing. Blow up.

Popular prestige.

Nothing isn’t digital anymore, and the online x offline convergence will only continue to ramp up. Full steam ahead.
